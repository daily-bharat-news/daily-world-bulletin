Total Solar Eclipse Visible in Spain and Other Regions

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 01:38 AM · Source checked

NASA has shared an image of a total solar eclipse captured from San Millán de los Caballeros, Spain, on Wednesday, August 12, 2026. This astronomical event occurs when the Moon passes directly between the Sun and Earth, completely obscuring the solar face. The celestial phenomenon swept across several territories, including parts of Greenland, Iceland, northern Russia, the Atlantic Ocean, Spain, and a small additional corner, offering a rare view of the shadow alignment.
