Karnataka cabinet expansion today with 20 new ministers taking oath

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 12:31 PM · Source checked

The Karnataka cabinet is scheduled for a major expansion with twenty ministers expected to take the oath of office at 5:00 pm. Meanwhile, discontent has emerged among some party members following cabinet exclusions. Two Congress legislators, including Yashavantaraygouda Patil, have resigned after failing to secure ministerial berths. Media outlets have accessed the list of names included in this expansion phase led by the state leadership. Further details regarding the complete portfolio allocations and swearing-in proceedings are expected as the day progresses.
