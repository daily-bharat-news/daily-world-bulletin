Ryanair Window Smashed After Engine Blade Broke, Report Says

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 03:13 AM · Source checked

A preliminary report released by US investigators reveals that an engine fan blade broke during a flight from Greece to Germany last month, causing engine parts to smash a window. According to the report, a passenger's head was partially sucked out through the damaged window during the incident. Authorities are continuing their investigation into the exact sequence of events that led to the dangerous structural failure. Official details regarding the condition of the passenger and further safety recommendations from the aviation authorities are currently limited.
