Jana Nayagan box office: Vijay starrer steady on day 8

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 05:59 AM · Source checked

The Thalapathy Vijay and Pooja Hegde starrer film Jana Nayagan maintains steady collections, earning Rs 3.98 crore on its eighth day. While reports note mixed box office trends, the regional movie has crossed the Rs 250-crore milestone globally, with its worldwide total reaching Rs 260.25 crore. Additionally, the film has surpassed the Rs 100-crore mark in Tamil Nadu alone, setting a new record as Vijay's farewell project.
