Spider-Man: Brand New Day nets USD 927 million globally

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 02:46 AM · Source checked

The Tom Holland starrer 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' has recorded a massive worldwide box office debut, grossing USD 927 million globally. According to reports from major entertainment publications, the film secured the second-biggest opening weekend in box office history. While domestic and international estimates vary on the exact opening weekend collections, with figures ranging around USD 355 million in certain regions, the overall global response marks a historic high for the franchise. Further specific details about regional breakdowns remain limited at this time.
