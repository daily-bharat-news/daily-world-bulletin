Ugandan opposition leader Kizza Besigye in ICU after court collapse

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 03:04 PM · Source checked

Kizza Besigye, a seventy-year-old veteran opposition figure in Uganda, has been admitted to an intensive care unit after collapsing during a court appearance. His wife confirmed the medical emergency. Besigye has been held in detention since November 2024 and currently faces serious treason charges, which he consistently denies. Authorities have not yet released official updates regarding his current medical condition. Further information remains limited at this stage as family members and supporters await more news from the hospital.
