Car washes and vape shops can still sponsor skilled workers despite rule changes

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 04:36 PM · Source checked

Recent policy adjustments implemented in July 2025 were intended to limit the skilled worker visa system primarily to graduate-level personnel earning a minimum salary of £41,700. However, businesses such as car washes and vape shops remain eligible to sponsor foreign employees under the existing provisions. Further details regarding the implementation and impact of these specific visa exceptions are currently limited, based on the provided information.
