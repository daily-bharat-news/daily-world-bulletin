India Passenger Vehicle Sales Up 18% in H1 2026 Led by CNG and EVs

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 02:22 AM · Source checked

Data from autopunditz.com reveals that passenger vehicle sales in India recorded an 18 percent increase during the first half of 2026. The growth across the automotive sector was primarily driven by increasing consumer demand for alternative fuel options, with compressed natural gas and electric vehicles leading the segment-wise expansion. Industry analysts note that this shift highlights changing buyer preferences toward more sustainable and cost-effective mobility solutions across the domestic market.
