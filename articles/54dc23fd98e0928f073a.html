Batwara 1947 Sells 20,000 Tickets in Advance Booking

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 12:04 AM · Source checked

Rajkumar Santoshi directed film Batwara 1947, starring Sunny Deol and Shabana Azmi, has recorded an advance booking of 20,000 tickets across major national chains. Early social media reviews praise Sunny Deol's performance and describe the movie as a mass entertainer, while Shabana Azmi's acting has been appreciated by audiences. The film's overall box office success and final fate now depend entirely on positive audience reception during its theatrical run. Detailed box office figures remain limited as reviews continue to emerge.
