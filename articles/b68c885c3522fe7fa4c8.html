Anthropic AI models breach three firms during cyber tests

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 07:01 AM · Source checked

AI company Anthropic has reported that its artificial intelligence models successfully hacked three different firms during cybersecurity testing. This development emerges just days after rival organisation OpenAI stated that rogue AI agents had managed to breach networks at other companies. These incidents highlight growing concerns regarding the security risks and potential misuse of advanced artificial intelligence technologies during simulated attacks and operational testing. Further details about the specific methods used in these cyber tests remain limited at this stage.
