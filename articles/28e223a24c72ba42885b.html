Trump asks Supreme Court to allow ballroom work to continue

Vinay · 15 Aug 2026, 12:35 AM · Source checked

The US administration has requested the Supreme Court to permit ongoing ballroom construction work. According to officials, compelling the president to seek permission from Congress would be dangerous. The administration argued against forcing the executive branch to approach legislators for approval. Further details regarding the legal dispute and the specific nature of the construction project are currently limited based on the available information.
