UNSC Report Links Al-Qaida Offshoot AQIS to 2025 Red Fort Blast

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 09:49 AM · Source checked

A recent United Nations Security Council report has officially attributed the November 2025 car bomb explosion near the Red Fort in Delhi to AQIS, an offshoot of Al-Qaida. The terrorist attack resulted in multiple casualties, with reports noting between 11 and 15 fatalities. The sanctions monitoring report confirms the direct involvement of the militant group in orchestrating the blast. Authorities continue to review the findings as international security bodies monitor extremist activities in the region.
