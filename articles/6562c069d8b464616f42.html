Apple shares drop after warning of future supply constraints for devices

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 04:55 AM · Source checked

Technology giant Apple has cautioned about upcoming supply constraints affecting popular products including the Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Following this advisory regarding slower sales anticipated in the coming months, shares of the company experienced a significant decline in the market. The warning highlights ongoing challenges within global manufacturing and distribution networks that could impact the firm's financial performance during the upcoming quarters.
