Perseid meteor shower peaks tonight with shooting stars

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 07:29 AM · Source checked

The Perseid meteor shower reaches its peak on Wednesday night, providing stargazers with the finest opportunity to spot shooting stars. Observers hoping to catch a glimpse of the celestial event can look to the night skies for activity. Details regarding exact visibility timings and specific viewing locations are limited in the current report, but enthusiasts are encouraged to look upward during the peak period for the best possible experience.
