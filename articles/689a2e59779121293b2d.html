Banned Afghan Women's Football Team Reunite Abroad

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 04:58 AM · Source checked

Players from the Afghan women's football team, which is officially recognised by Fifa, have reunited thousands of miles away from their homeland after being banned from playing football domestically. Members of the squad shared that the sport has provided them with a sense of freedom and independence despite the severe restrictions and challenges they have faced. Further details regarding their current location and future matches remain limited.
