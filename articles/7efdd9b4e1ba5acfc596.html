Ashish Yadav Wins Javelin Silver at World U20 Championships

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 10:32 AM · Source checked

Ashish Yadav secured a silver medal in the javelin throw at the World U20 Championships, ending India's ten-year wait for a medal in the discipline since Neeraj Chopra's historic achievement. Meanwhile, Ashmita advanced to the Korea Masters final. At the U20 World Athletics Championships, Pooja Singh qualified for the high jump final, and two Indian athletes entered the long jump medal round. Additionally, Amanat Kamboj made it to the women's discus throw final, showcasing strong performances by Indian competitors across multiple athletic events internationally.
