Spider-Man Brand New Day crosses Rs 100 Crore advance in India

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 09:46 PM · Source checked

Entertainment sources report that the Tom Holland starrer film 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' has achieved a major box office milestone by crossing Rs 100 crore in advance bookings within India. Reviewers from various publications have shared their perspectives on the superhero feature, noting its strength within the Marvel Cinematic Universe and comparing its scope to a reboot. Additionally, entertainment platforms have published watch order guides for fans revisiting earlier films starring Tom Holland prior to experiencing this latest theatrical release.
