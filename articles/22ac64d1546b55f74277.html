The Sopranos actor Vincent Pastore passes away at 80

Vinay · 02 Aug 2026, 04:27 AM · Source checked

Actor Vincent Pastore, renowned for his role as Salvatore "Big Pussy" Bonpensiero in the popular television series The Sopranos, has passed away at the age of 80. According to US media reports, the actor was discovered at his residence located in New York City. Further details regarding the cause and exact circumstances of his death are currently limited based on the initial reports from the sources.
