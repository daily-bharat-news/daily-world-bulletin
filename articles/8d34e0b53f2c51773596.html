SC asks Centre to preserve RAF ammunition logs from Jantar Mantar protest

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026 · Source checked

The Supreme Court of India has directed the Central government to preserve records and logs of Rapid Action Force ammunition deployed during a protest at Jantar Mantar organised by Citizens for Justice and Peace. While considering a plea seeking a ban on pellet guns, the apex court indicated that such weapons may be used in exceptional circumstances and announced plans to examine existing crowd control protocols. Additionally, the court directed the Delhi government to guarantee proper medical treatment for injured protesters. This development comes as medical experts warn that pellet gun injuries can result in irreversible damage and permanent disability.
