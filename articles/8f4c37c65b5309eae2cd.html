SC Collegium Recommends Chief Justices for Four High Courts

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 02:18 PM · Source checked

The Supreme Court Collegium has recommended the appointment of new Chief Justices for four high courts across the country. According to reports, the recommendations include Justice R.V. Ghuge for the Calcutta High Court and Justice M.C. Tripathi for the Bombay High Court, along with the elevation of two other judges. These judicial recommendations are aimed at filling key leadership positions in the respective high courts. Further details regarding the official notifications and swearing-in dates are awaited as per the standard administrative procedures.
