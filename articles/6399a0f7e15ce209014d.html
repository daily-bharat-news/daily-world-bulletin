Actor Avika Gor Hospitalised After Dengue Diagnosis

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 10:46 AM · Source checked

Actor Avika Gor has been admitted to a hospital following a diagnosis of dengue. Her husband, Milind Chandwani, shared a health update stating that she had continued working despite running a high fever of 104 degrees. According to him, her condition worsened to the point where she was unable to move or eat. Several media outlets reported on her hospitalisation, noting her previous work in television including the popular show Balika Vadhu. Further details regarding her current recovery status remain limited.
