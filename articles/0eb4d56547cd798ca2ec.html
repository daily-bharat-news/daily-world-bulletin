Todd Blanche confirmed as US deputy attorney general

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 03:49 PM · Source checked

Todd Blanche, the former lawyer of Donald Trump, has been confirmed as the United States deputy attorney general. Republican Senator Bill Cassidy cast the deciding vote following a prolonged stand-off in the Senate. Details are limited regarding the specific timeline of his formal assumption of office and the broader policy priorities he intends to pursue in this key Department of Justice role.
