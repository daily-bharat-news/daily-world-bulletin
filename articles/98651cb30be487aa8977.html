Half-price rail travel extended for 18-year-olds

Vinay · 04 Aug 2026, 05:04 AM · Source checked

Young people in the UK can now continue purchasing the 16-17 Saver railcard until the day before their eighteenth birthday. This extension allows eligible individuals to access half-price rail travel for a longer period. The update to the railcard scheme provides financial relief on train journeys for older teenagers as they transition into adulthood, offering continued discounted fares across the rail network based on the recent policy announcement.
