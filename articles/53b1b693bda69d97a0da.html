Rift in NCPI as 3 MPs break ranks over ties with BJP

Vinay · 10 Aug 2026, 06:12 AM · Source checked

A rift has emerged within the NCPI as three members of parliament skipped an NDA meeting and refused to join the alliance or the BJP. While two NCPI MPs met West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari amid political speculation, MP Abu Taher stated they are not in the NDA and only attended the Prime Minister's breakfast meeting to raise public issues. Meanwhile, Yusuf Pathan wrote to the Chief Minister demanding SIR tribunals in every district of West Bengal.
