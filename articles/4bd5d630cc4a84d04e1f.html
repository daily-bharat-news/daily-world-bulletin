Second-hand smoke and late diagnosis heighten lung cancer worries

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 01:21 PM · Source checked

Recent discussions highlight growing concerns regarding lung cancer among non-smokers in India, driven significantly by second-hand smoke exposure and delayed diagnoses. Reports indicate that a substantial share of lung cancer cases involve individuals who do not smoke, challenging traditional assumptions about the disease. Medical experts emphasize the need for screening approaches that account for risks beyond smoking. Additionally, specialists are working to dispel common myths surrounding the condition to improve awareness and understanding following a diagnosis.
