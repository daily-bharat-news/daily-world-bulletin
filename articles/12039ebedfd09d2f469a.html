Travis Kelce shares details of wedding to Taylor Swift

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 06:49 AM · Source checked

Travis Kelce has shared initial details regarding his wedding to Taylor Swift, describing the occasion as the best night of his life. Responding in the comments section, Kelce referred to Swift as his wife for the very first time. He also mentioned that getting married at Madison Square Garden had been a childhood dream. The wedding details emerged through his public comments about the event, capturing significant media attention globally.
