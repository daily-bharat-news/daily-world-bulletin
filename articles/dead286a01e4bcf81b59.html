Croatia Wildfire Leaves Dozens Injured and Thousands Evacuated

Vinay · 15 Aug 2026, 01:52 AM · Source checked

A major wildfire near the port town of Omis in Croatia has resulted in dozens of injuries and the evacuation of thousands of residents and tourists. Local authorities report that this incident ranks among the worst fire disasters in the country's history. Emergency response teams and firefighters are actively working to control the blaze and secure the affected areas, while officials continue to monitor the evolving situation closely as recovery and relief operations proceed across the region.
