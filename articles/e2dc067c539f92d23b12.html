Sudan army drone strike on court session kills 35 people

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 03:59 PM · Source checked

A rights group has reported that a drone strike carried out by the Sudanese army on a court session in a paramilitary-held village resulted in the deaths of thirty-five people. The attack targeted the village while it was under the control of rival forces in the ongoing conflict. Meanwhile, the military has not yet issued any official comments or statements regarding the allegations that civilians lost their lives during the operation.
