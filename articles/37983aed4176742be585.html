Relaxed mortgage rules aid first-time buyers

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 04:46 AM · Source checked

Recent changes in mortgage regulations are offering new opportunities for first-time buyers to enter the property market by easing lending criteria. While this shift may help more individuals secure a home loan, financial experts caution that it also introduces additional risks that borrowers should carefully evaluate before committing to a purchase. Understanding these financial implications is crucial for anyone navigating the property market under the updated guidelines.
