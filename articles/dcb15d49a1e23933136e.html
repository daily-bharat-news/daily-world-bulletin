Supreme Court directs Centre to ensure encroachment-free walking space on roads

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 01:10 PM · Source checked

The Supreme Court has directed the Centre to ensure that every road features encroachment-free space designated for walking. In connection with pedestrian rights, the Kerala High Court has also addressed hazardous road protrusions in Kochi and sought a protective plan from the Kochi Corporation. These judicial directives highlight growing concerns over pedestrian safety across the country, prompting authorities to prioritize proper infrastructure and clear pathways for people walking on public roads.
