Amit Mishra backs Gautam Gambhir amid Test defeat concerns

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 05:00 AM · Source checked

Following ten losses in nineteen Test matches and declining World Test Championship prospects, cricketer Amit Mishra has urged that head coach Gautam Gambhir be given additional time. Meanwhile, reports indicate that the BCCI will continue to support Gambhir regardless of upcoming Test series outcomes against Sri Lanka. Discussions surrounding the security of his position have intensified, with various sports platforms highlighting key factors influencing the cricket board's final decisions regarding the coaching staff.
