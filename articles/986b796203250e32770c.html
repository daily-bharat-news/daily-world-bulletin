Israeli Troops Order Village Families to Evacuate Homes

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 01:42 AM · Source checked

According to the mayor of Qusra, Israeli forces have instructed Palestinian families to leave their residences in a village where Israeli settlers besieged local houses. The mayor stated that troops are also using some of these vacated properties as military barracks. Further details regarding the unfolding situation and the exact number of affected residents are currently limited based on the available reports from the area.
