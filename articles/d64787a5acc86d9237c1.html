DC Box Office: Lokesh Kanagaraj's Debut Recovers 18 Percent Budget On Day One

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 12:08 PM · Source checked

Director Lokesh Kanagaraj has made his acting debut in the newly released Tamil film titled DC, which has emerged as the sixth biggest Tamil opener of 2026. According to box office reports and early reviews, the stylishly reimagined gangster drama blends elements of a classic tragic romance with gritty violence, drawing comparisons to notable crime narratives. The film's strong opening performance has already recovered nearly eighteen percent of its total budget on its very first day, while critics have also praised the lead performance and direction.
