Trump Media Reports $238M Loss Amid Falling Crypto Values

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 07:51 AM · Source checked

Trump Media has reported a significant financial loss of $238 million as cryptocurrency values declined. In response to the setback, the firm announced plans to refocus its strategic efforts on its core social media platform. This shift in direction includes a specific proposal to sell faster access to posts made by Donald Trump. Further details regarding the financial results and implementation of the new social media strategy remain limited at this time.
