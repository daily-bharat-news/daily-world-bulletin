Twitch allows users to opt out of AI training using content

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 08:16 AM · Source checked

Streaming platform Twitch has introduced a setting enabling users to prevent the company from utilizing their channel content for artificial intelligence training. This update follows criticism from creators regarding the platform's previous approach to using user data for AI development. Users can now adjust their privacy preferences to restrict how their broadcasts and materials are processed by the company for machine learning purposes.
