WWE SummerSlam 2026 Night 1: Oba Femi Beats Brock Lesnar

Vinay · 02 Aug 2026, 06:37 AM · Source checked

During the first night of WWE SummerSlam 2026, Oba Femi secured a victory against Brock Lesnar inside a Hell in a Cell match. Post-match developments included Brock Lesnar acknowledging Oba Femi as the future of the company, alongside the return of Randy Orton and a surprising betrayal. Additional highlights from the event featured CM Punk retaining his title, while media discussions emerged regarding Brock Lesnar's future in the sport.
