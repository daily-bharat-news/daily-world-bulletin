Trump Warns Iran Over Hormuz Strait As Oil Prices Drop

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 09:14 AM · Source checked

United States President Donald Trump stated that Iran will face severe consequences if the Strait of Hormuz is not reopened soon. Meanwhile, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent confirmed that discussions have advanced to help resume oil shipments. These developments coincide with a fall in global oil prices. Further official updates regarding the maritime route and diplomatic talks are awaited as authorities monitor the situation closely.
