Thousands of young people missing out on money

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 02:40 PM · Source checked

Young individuals aged between 18 and 23 could be losing out on thousands of pounds resting in unclaimed government accounts. Many people in this age group remain unaware of these funds, meaning significant amounts of money are left untouched. Details regarding how to check for or claim these specific accounts are limited based on the provided information, but the situation highlights a lack of awareness among young adults about government schemes established for their financial benefit.
