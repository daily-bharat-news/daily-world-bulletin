Ancient Roman Shipwreck Found Off Sicily Coast

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 05:57 PM · Source checked

Italy's culture minister has announced the discovery of an ancient Roman shipwreck off the coast of Sicily. Authorities have described the underwater archaeological find as one of the most significant discoveries made in recent years. Experts are currently examining the site to learn more about the historical vessel and its cargo. Further details regarding the exact location and recovery operations are expected to be shared as the investigation progresses.
