Canada Wildfire Forces Over 12,000 Evacuations in Okanagan Region

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 12:47 AM · Source checked

A fast-moving wildfire in Canada's picturesque Okanagan region has forced more than 12,000 residents to evacuate their homes. Evacuees have reported experiencing numerous close calls as the blaze tore through the area, leaving many uncertain about whether their properties are still standing. Authorities and emergency services continue to monitor the situation as residents wait for updates on the damage caused by the severe fire.
