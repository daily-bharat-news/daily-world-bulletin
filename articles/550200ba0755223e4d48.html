Cristiano Ronaldo marries long-time partner Georgina Rodríguez

Vinay · 12 Aug 2026, 05:32 AM · Source checked

Portuguese football star Cristiano Ronaldo has married his long-time partner Georgina Rodríguez. The athlete shared a photograph showing two hands wearing wedding rings, with his hand resting on top of hers. The couple has been together for several years and shares a family, prompting congratulatory messages from fans and followers across social media platforms following the announcement.
