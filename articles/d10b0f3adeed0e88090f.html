Basant Kumar Meghwal wins historic silver at World Athletics U20 Championships

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 09:18 AM · Source checked

At the World Athletics U20 Championships in Eugene, high jumper Basant Kumar Meghwal made history by securing a silver medal, becoming the first Indian to achieve a podium finish in the event. Meanwhile, Shahnavaz Khan added to India's success by bagging a bronze medal in the long jump. These outstanding performances helped India equal its best-ever medal tally at the prestigious global under-20 athletics competition, marking a memorable night for Indian athletics on the international stage.
