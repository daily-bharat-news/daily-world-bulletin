Jana Nayagan box office: Vijay and Pooja Hegde film crosses Rs 268 crore worldwide

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 06:11 AM · Source checked

The Vijay and Pooja Hegde starrer 'Jana Nayagan' entered its second weekend by crossing the Rs 268 crore mark at the global box office by day nine. Although the film experienced a dip in daily domestic collections on its eighth day, it continues a strong theatrical run. Reports indicate the movie is nearing the Rs 300 crore milestone and is poised to surpass the lifetime collection of 'Good Bad Ugly'. It had earlier crossed the Rs 250 crore worldwide milestone within its first week of release.
