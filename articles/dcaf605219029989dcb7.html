Maruti, VinFast and Tesla Drive India EV Growth

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 12:41 PM · Source checked

Major automakers including Maruti, VinFast, and Tesla are actively driving the expansion of the electric vehicle market in India. These companies are increasing their focus on the country's growing green mobility sector through new strategic developments and upcoming vehicle launches. Industry observers note that this heightened activity reflects intensifying competition and rising consumer interest in eco-friendly transportation alternatives across the nation, marking a significant phase in the transition toward sustainable automotive solutions.
