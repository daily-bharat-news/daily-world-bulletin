Ergonomic tools for young professionals with neck and back pain

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 02:01 PM · Source checked

Occupational therapist Bharati Jajoo has shared five ergonomic tools to help young professionals manage neck and back pain. With many individuals spending long hours working at desks, musculoskeletal discomfort has become increasingly common among the younger workforce. Proper workplace ergonomics can significantly reduce physical strain and improve overall posture. Incorporating these supportive tools into daily routines may help prevent chronic issues and promote better spinal health in office environments.
