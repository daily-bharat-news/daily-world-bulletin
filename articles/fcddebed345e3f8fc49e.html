Indian journalist Tarun Tejpal sentenced to 10 years in jail for rape

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 03:19 PM · Source checked

An Indian court has sentenced journalist Tarun Tejpal to ten years in prison following a rape conviction. The case relates to an incident that occurred at a hotel in Goa in 2013. Tejpal, who has consistently denied all accusations against him, retains the legal right to challenge the verdict by filing an appeal in India's Supreme Court. Further details regarding the court proceedings and the ongoing legal case remain limited based on the current available information.
