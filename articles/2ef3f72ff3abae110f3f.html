Heatwaves impact summer weddings and spark shift to October

Vinay · 04 Aug 2026, 04:50 AM · Source checked

Extreme heatwaves are causing last-minute expenses for the wedding industry as planners scramble to manage rising temperatures. Couples are increasingly altering their traditional schedules to avoid peak heat, leading to a noticeable surge in October bookings. Industry professionals are adopting new strategies to cope with these weather challenges, ensuring events remain manageable for guests while dealing with unexpected logistical costs.
