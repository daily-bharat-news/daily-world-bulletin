Australian military whistleblower David McBride freed on parole

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 07:53 AM · Source checked

Australian army whistleblower David McBride has been released on parole after serving more than two years in prison. McBride was jailed following his guilty plea for leaking classified military secrets concerning operations in Afghanistan. His case drew significant attention regarding the treatment of whistleblowers who expose alleged wrongdoing within defense forces. Authorities confirmed his release, noting that he will complete the remainder of his sentence under supervised community conditions. Further details regarding specific parole restrictions were limited at the time of his release.
