IMD Forecasts Heavy Downpours in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 09:57 AM · Source checked

The India Meteorological Department has issued weather updates forecasting heavy downpours across the states of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Residents and local authorities in the affected regions have been advised to check for further updates and take necessary precautions as the heavy rainfall is expected to impact normal activities. Detailed forecasts regarding the intensity and exact timings of the precipitation can be obtained through official weather channels and updates.
