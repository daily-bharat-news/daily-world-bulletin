Bird flu threatens nearly 400 Australian species including penguins and dolphins

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 10:16 AM · Source checked

Recent reports reveal that nearly 400 Australian species, including little penguins and dolphins, face significant threats from bird flu. Australia has confirmed its first mass wildlife mortality linked to the virus. Authorities warn that H5N1 detections continue to rise and the disease is likely to spread further across the region. Experts estimate that specific local penguin colonies could potentially lose up to forty percent of their population. Officials and researchers are closely monitoring the situation as the true extent of the ecological disaster unfolds.
