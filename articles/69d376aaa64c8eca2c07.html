Easier mortgage rules open doors for first-time buyers

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 03:25 PM · Source checked

Recent changes in mortgage regulations are making it potentially easier for individuals to secure their first property loan. While this shift offers new opportunities for aspiring homeowners to enter the property market, industry observers note that it also introduces additional financial risks. Borrowers are advised to carefully evaluate their financial readiness and the long-term implications before committing to these mortgage products, as regulatory easing does not eliminate underlying lending hazards.
