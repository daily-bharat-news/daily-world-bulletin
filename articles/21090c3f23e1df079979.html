Peru ex-president leaves jail after corruption term overturned

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 07:51 AM · Source checked

Former Peruvian leader Ollanta Humala has walked free from prison after his fifteen-year corruption and money laundering sentence was overturned. Humala had previously been convicted alongside his wife in 2025 in connection with financial offences. Following the legal reversal, he has now left custody. Official updates regarding the ongoing legal proceedings and the status of related cases remain limited at this time.
