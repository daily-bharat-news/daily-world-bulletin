NASA Repaints 737 Aircraft for Reduced Gravity Testing

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 01:11 AM · Source checked

NASA's 737 aircraft received a new paint job in Oklahoma as part of ongoing modifications to prepare it as a reduced gravity test vehicle. NASA's Armstrong Flight Research Center in Edwards, California, acquired the plane from the United States Air Force in June. The modified aircraft is scheduled to conduct lunar-gravity parabolic flights to validate equipment and procedures for future space missions. Further details regarding the testing schedule and specific mission parameters remain limited at this stage.
