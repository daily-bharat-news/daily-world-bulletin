EU commends Spain's prompt response to Ceuta migrant crisis

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 03:15 AM · Source checked

The European Union has praised Spain for its swift action during the recent migrant crisis in Ceuta. According to Spanish authorities, approximately 70,000 out of the 72,000 individuals who entered the enclave unlawfully last week have already been sent back to Morocco. Officials continue to monitor the situation closely as diplomatic and border security measures remain active between the two nations following the sudden surge of arrivals.
