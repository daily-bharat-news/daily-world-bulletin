Delhi sees significant rise in H1N1 and dengue cases

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 10:11 AM · Source checked

Delhi has witnessed a sharp six-fold increase in H1N1 influenza cases alongside a surge in dengue infections during the monsoon season. Hospitals report a twenty to thirty percent rise in viral fever and respiratory patients, with some COVID-19 cases turning moderately severe. Medical professionals note that swine flu is triggering more intensive care admissions, compounded by public complacency regarding flu vaccinations. Doctors have urged residents not to ignore warning signs as viral illnesses continue to spread across the capital region.
