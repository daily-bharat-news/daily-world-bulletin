Stagnant water raises mosquito breeding concerns in Amritsar

Vinay · 10 Aug 2026, 02:51 AM · Source checked

Stagnant water accumulation in various areas of Amritsar has sparked serious concerns regarding potential mosquito breeding. Local authorities and residents are worried that the untreated standing water could lead to a surge in vector-borne diseases. Health experts have frequently warned about the risks associated with such conditions, emphasizing the need for timely drainage and civic intervention to protect public health. Currently, specific details regarding official mitigation measures or affected localities remain limited based on the available reports.
