Japan business sector slow to adopt AI due to risk aversion

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 04:30 AM · Source checked

Japanese companies are showing a sluggish pace in adopting artificial intelligence technologies. Experts point to deep-rooted risk aversion and conservatism within the country's corporate sector as the primary reasons for this delay. While global markets rapidly integrate automated tools and advanced software to boost productivity, businesses in Japan remain cautious. This hesitation highlights cultural and operational barriers that hinder technological modernization across various industries in the region, according to recent business reports.
