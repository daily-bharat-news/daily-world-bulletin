India records 3.28 lakh EV retail sales in July

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 08:15 AM · Source checked

India witnessed a remarkable growth in the electric vehicle sector, with retail sales reaching a record 3.28 lakh units in July. This milestone represents a significant 66 percent year-on-year increase compared to previous figures, according to reports from ET Auto. The surge highlights the rising adoption of eco-friendly mobility solutions across the country. Further details regarding specific vehicle segments and regional performance remain limited at this time.
