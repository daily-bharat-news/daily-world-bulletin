Iran denies direct US talks despite Donald Trump's assertion

Vinay · 04 Aug 2026, 01:12 AM · Source checked

Iran has clarified that it is currently engaging in discussions with Oman, but firmly denied holding any negotiations with the United States. This statement follows remarks from Donald Trump claiming that talks between Washington and Tehran are set to resume. In response to Iran's denial, the former US president issued a strong condemnation of the Iranian leadership. Further details regarding the diplomatic exchanges remain limited at this stage.
