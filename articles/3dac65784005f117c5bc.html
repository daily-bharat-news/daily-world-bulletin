Spider-Man: Brand New Day Makes Box Office History in North America

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 09:52 AM · Source checked

The Tom Holland starrer 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' has achieved a significant box office milestone in North America, becoming the fastest film to cross the $700 million mark and securing a place in the all-time top six movies. Meanwhile, the film continues its strong theatrical run in India, nearing substantial gross collections by the end of its second week and preparing to compete with upcoming domestic releases.
