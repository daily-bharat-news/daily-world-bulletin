Indian strawberry farmers eye shift from imported crop varieties

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 03:34 AM · Source checked

Indian strawberry growers currently depend heavily on plant varieties imported from the United States and Europe. However, this reliance could soon change as domestic efforts grow. Local agricultural developments aim to improve strawberry farming within the country, potentially helping India join major global producers. Details regarding specific timelines, government policies, and new indigenous crop varieties remain limited in the current reports.
