Shaik Rasheed and Sarfaraz Khan emerge as top Test squad contenders

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 02:54 PM · Source checked

Young cricketers Shaik Rasheed and Sarfaraz Khan have emerged as the leading candidates to replace Sai Sudharsan in the India Test squad. Sai Sudharsan has been ruled out of the upcoming India A series in Sri Lanka and is doubtful for matches due to a foot injury sustained during his recovery. While reports indicate he might travel to Sri Lanka by the weekend, selectors are considering suitable replacements from the available talent pool as the team prepares for the crucial matches ahead.
