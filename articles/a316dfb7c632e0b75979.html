SC Rules S.69 GST Arrest Order Must Be Communicated

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 10:04 AM · Source checked

The Supreme Court has ruled that the Section 69 GST order containing reasons to believe must be communicated before making an arrest. Setting aside the protection from arrest granted to Future Group director Sunil Biyani, the apex bench held that High Courts cannot grant post-dismissal arrest protection in pre-arrest bail matters concerning GST cases. The court also observed that relief cannot be granted by High Courts if an anticipatory bail plea is premature, clarifying the legal parameters for arrests under the GST framework.
