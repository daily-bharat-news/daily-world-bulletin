All English rivers and lakes polluted with chemicals, reveals assessment

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 03:30 PM · Source checked

A comprehensive evaluation of water bodies across England has revealed that every single river and lake is polluted with harmful chemicals, according to official findings. This marks the first major assessment of the nation's waterways in six years. The report indicates that there has been very little overall improvement in water quality during this period. Officials released these concerning details following the extensive review, highlighting widespread environmental challenges facing aquatic ecosystems throughout the region. Further information on specific pollutants and mitigation strategies remains limited at this stage.
