Uttarakhand on High Alert Amid Heavy Rains and Landslides

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 03:05 AM · Source checked

Uttarakhand has been placed on high alert following a heavy rain warning issued for the next twenty-four hours. Persistent downpours have triggered severe landslides, causing boulders to crash into government residential units. In response to the worsening weather conditions, authorities have ordered schools and Anganwadi centres in Dehradun to remain closed. Across northern India, heavy rains and subsequent floods have reportedly claimed at least forty-six lives. Officials continue to monitor the situation closely as rescue and relief operations remain active in affected areas.
