Varun Tej Announces Korean Kanakaraju Premieres

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 04:05 PM · Source checked

Actor Varun Tej has announced the premieres for his upcoming project Korean Kanakaraju, directed by Merlapaka Gandhi. Entertainment reports indicate that extra shows are being planned for the release. While discussing the project, the actor assured fans he would give his best effort, though he humorously noted it may not reach the level of Ram Charan. Director Merlapaka Gandhi stated that audiences will appreciate the distinct Korean element integrated into the film. Further box office updates and details regarding whether this venture can achieve major milestones remain limited based on current reports.
