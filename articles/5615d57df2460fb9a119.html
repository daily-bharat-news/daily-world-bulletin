US blogger Perez Hilton in hospital following livestream incident

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 10:02 AM · Source checked

American blogger Perez Hilton is currently receiving medical care and faces an extended recovery period after reportedly self-harming during a live broadcast. Further updates regarding his health and well-being have not been immediately detailed, while well-wishers and followers monitor the situation closely.
