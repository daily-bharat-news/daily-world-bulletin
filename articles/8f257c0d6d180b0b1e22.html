Tejaswin Shankar Wins Historic CWG 2026 Decathlon Bronze

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 01:28 AM · Source checked

Tejaswin Shankar scripted history at the Commonwealth Games 2026 by securing a bronze medal in the decathlon event despite an injury. He accumulated 7976 points to become the first Indian athlete to win a decathlon medal at the games. This achievement highlights his resilience and adds a significant milestone to Indian athletics. Further details regarding the competition and other Indian performances on Day 9 of the games were also reported.
