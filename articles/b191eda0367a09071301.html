PM E-DRIVE Scheme allocates Rs 2,000 crore for EV charging stations

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 12:34 PM · Source checked

An allocation of Rs 2,000 crore has been made under the PM E-DRIVE Scheme to deploy Electric Vehicle Public Charging Stations across India. This financial provision aims to strengthen the country's EV charging infrastructure on a pan-India basis. The initiative is expected to support the growing adoption of electric vehicles by improving public access to charging facilities. Details regarding the exact timeline and phase-wise implementation of these stations remain limited in the current reports.
