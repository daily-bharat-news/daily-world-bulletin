Vikas Gupta slams Shilpa Shinde over controversial remarks

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 10:51 PM · Source checked

Entertainment sources report that television personality Vikas Gupta publicly criticized Shilpa Shinde for her recent comments discussing actor Shivangi Joshi's personal life and body-shaming Ram Kapoor. Gupta strongly rebuked Shinde on Lock Upp 2, questioning her behavior and labeling her remarks as negative and inappropriate. Media reports highlighted his strong disapproval of the comments made regarding Joshi and Kapoor, drawing widespread attention from various entertainment platforms.
