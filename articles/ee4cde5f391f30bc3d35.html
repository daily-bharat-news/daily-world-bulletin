Aung San Suu Kyi seen in first contact in 2.5 years

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 04:28 PM · Source checked

Myanmar's military government has released new photographs showing detained leader Aung San Suu Kyi meeting with an official from the International Committee of the Red Cross. This marks the first confirmed outside contact for Suu Kyi in two and a half years. The newly published images aim to demonstrate her current status, though further details regarding her specific health condition, exact location, and the full context of this rare meeting remain limited at this stage.
