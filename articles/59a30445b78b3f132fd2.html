August 2026 Skywatching: Solar Eclipse, Perseids and Venus

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 04:05 AM · Source checked

NASA has released skywatching tips for August 2026, highlighting several major celestial events. Stargazers can look forward to a solar eclipse, the peak of the Perseids meteor shower, and Venus appearing at its brightest in the evening sky. Additionally, a deep partial lunar eclipse will occur towards the end of the month, bringing August to a close. These astronomical occurrences offer prime viewing opportunities for astronomy enthusiasts and casual observers alike.
