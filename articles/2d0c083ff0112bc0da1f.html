US climber's body recovered following Pakistan avalanche

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 01:52 AM · Source checked

Rescue teams in Pakistan have recovered the body of an American climber following an avalanche. This marks the third recovery operation on the mountain, after the bodies of two other climbers were previously retrieved. Meanwhile, search efforts continue as several other individuals remain missing in the area. Official details regarding the ongoing rescue operations and the exact location of the incident are currently limited based on the initial reports received from the region.
