Seven Dead and Three Trapped After Water Floods Tunnel in Uttarakhand's Chamoli

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 10:50 AM · Source checked

Seven workers died and three remain trapped following a water flooding incident inside a tunnel in Chamoli, Uttarakhand. Fourteen others sustained injuries in the disaster. Rescue teams have launched operations, including underwater efforts, to locate and save the missing individuals. Uttarakhand Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami visited the accident site to monitor rescue work and met with injured workers receiving treatment. The state government announced an ex-gratia financial assistance of four lakh rupees for the families of each deceased worker, as relief and rescue efforts continue at the location.
