Nirmal Purja and nine others feared missing after Pakistan avalanche

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 06:22 AM · Source checked

Renowned mountaineer Nirmal Purja and nine other climbers are reported missing following an avalanche in Pakistan. The group includes citizens from the United States, China, Oman, and five individuals from Nepal. Authorities and rescue teams are monitoring the situation closely as details regarding the incident remain limited. Efforts are underway to gather more information about the missing climbers and assess the conditions at the site.
