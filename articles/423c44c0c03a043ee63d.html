Actor Pradeep Rawat of 'Ghajini' fame passes away at 74

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 12:15 AM · Source checked

Veteran actor Pradeep Rawat, widely known for his memorable performances in popular films such as 'Ghajini' and 'Lagaan', has passed away at the age of 74. Following the news of his demise, several film personalities, including Thakur Anoop Singh and Yashpal Sharma, paid heartfelt tributes to the late actor, remembering his inspiring journey and impactful contribution to cinema. Further details regarding his passing are currently limited based on the available reports.
