First areas for 'pounds for pylons' energy bill discounts announced

Vinay · 12 Aug 2026, 04:33 AM · Source checked

Residents living within 1,600 feet of upgraded electricity pylons will receive a £250 annual discount on their power bills. Officials have revealed the initial locations where this scheme will apply. The initiative is designed to benefit households situated near major new transmission infrastructure upgrades. Further details regarding the rollout and exact criteria for the electricity bill reductions are expected as the program progresses in the selected communities.
