Messi Scores First Goals Since World Cup as Inter Miami Wins

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 12:53 PM · Source checked

Lionel Messi has scored his first goals since the World Cup, helping Inter Miami secure a victory against San Luis in the Leagues Cup. His performance led the team to a strong start in the tournament. Sources noted that his goals contributed to a significant win and set a new tournament record during the goalfest fixture.
