Polish PM says missile that created crater in Poland was likely Russian

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 02:51 PM · Source checked

Poland's Prime Minister has stated that an unidentified object which caused a large crater deep inside Polish territory was probably a Russian missile. The impact created a ten-metre-wide crater near the village of Tarnawa Kolonia, located roughly 100 kilometres from the Ukrainian border. The incident took place while Russian forces were conducting an attack on the city of Lviv in Ukraine. Specific details regarding the projectile remain limited at present, as authorities review the event.
