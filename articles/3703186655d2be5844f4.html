Trump Administration Drops Reflecting Pool Vandalism Charge

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 05:50 AM · Source checked

Prosecutors have dropped a vandalism charge related to the National Mall Reflecting Pool in Washington, acknowledging that contractors rather than malicious actors caused the damage. Previously, President Donald Trump had attributed the incident to vandals and labeled them sick, while a former Olympian had also faced charges. Officials now admit the repairs were mishandled, concluding that the structural marks were the result of contractor work instead of intentional destruction. Further details remain limited regarding the settlement.
