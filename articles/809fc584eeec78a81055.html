Nick and Joe Jonas enjoy Indian food in Toronto

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 07:25 AM · Source checked

Nick Jonas and Joe Jonas recently visited a Michelin-recognised Indian restaurant in Toronto, where they enjoyed a variety of traditional dishes. The brothers were spotted relishing culinary items such as onion bhajia, pista lamb chop, murgh makhani, kachori, and papad. Footage from their outing has drawn significant attention online, with fans enthusiastically reacting to their love for Indian cuisine, with some playfully commenting on Nick's connection to the culture.
