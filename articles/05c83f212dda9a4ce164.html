Suspected Irish crime boss Daniel Kinahan appears in court

Vinay · 10 Aug 2026, 02:28 AM · Source checked

Suspected crime boss Daniel Kinahan has appeared in court following his extradition from Dubai. The Irish Prison Service arranged special security for the transport of the 49-year-old, purchasing a bulletproof and bombproof van specifically for the court appearance. Authorities managed the high-security transfer amid tight precautions. Further details regarding the court proceedings and subsequent legal steps remain limited based on the current available information from the authorities.
