Child and two others killed in Russian missile strikes near Kyiv

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 11:42 AM · Source checked

Three people, including a child, lost their lives in overnight Russian missile strikes near the Ukrainian capital of Kyiv. This escalation follows recent warnings from President Volodymyr Zelensky regarding the rapidly depleting stock of missile interceptors in Ukraine. Emergency response operations and assessments of the damage in the affected areas are currently underway as authorities deal with the aftermath of the sustained regional bombardments.
