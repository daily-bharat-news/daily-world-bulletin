Iran denies US talk claims as Oman negotiations continue

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 03:53 PM · Source checked

Iran has dismissed claims made by Donald Trump that fresh discussions between Washington and Tehran are scheduled to begin on Monday. Despite rejecting any direct engagement with the US, Iranian officials are presently holding talks with Oman concerning access to the strategically vital Strait of Hormuz. Official sources have clarified the current diplomatic stance, emphasizing that reports of upcoming negotiations with American representatives are incorrect while regional discussions proceed.
