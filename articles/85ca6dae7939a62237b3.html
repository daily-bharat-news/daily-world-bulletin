Egypt arrests individuals for allegedly impersonating judges in a scam

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 12:55 AM · Source checked

Egyptian authorities have arrested several people accused of running a fraud scheme by impersonating judges. According to officials, the operation involved staging fake courtroom scenes as part of a plot to deceive victims. Details regarding the exact number of arrests and the total financial impact of the alleged scam remain limited at this stage as investigations continue.
