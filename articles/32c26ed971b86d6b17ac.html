Armed man arrested near California golf course ahead of Trump visit

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 05:49 AM · Source checked

An armed individual was taken into custody near a coastal golf course in California. The arrest occurred shortly before a scheduled fundraising dinner hosted by President Trump at the location. Authorities apprehended the suspect as preparations were underway for the presidential visit. Due to limited available details, specific information regarding the suspect's identity, motives, or the exact nature of the weapon recovered has not yet been officially released by law enforcement agencies covering the unfolding incident.
