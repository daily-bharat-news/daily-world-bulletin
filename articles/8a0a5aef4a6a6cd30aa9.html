Scientists use artificial intelligence to design new viruses

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 04:50 AM · Source checked

Recent reports indicate that scientists have utilized artificial intelligence to design sixteen new viruses not currently found in nature. According to the sources, these AI-designed viral entities are being explored as a potential new pathway to combat drug-resistant bacteria. While the development highlights significant advancements in the application of artificial intelligence within the scientific and medical fields, specific details regarding the methodology and safety protocols remain limited in the current reports.
