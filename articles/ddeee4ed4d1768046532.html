Rihanna is currently in the studio making music, partner A$AP Rocky reveals

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 10:14 AM · Source checked

Pop star Rihanna is back in the recording studio and working on new music, according to her partner, rapper A$AP Rocky. The singer last released a full studio album in 2016. While speaking to a fan, the artist gave a brief update confirming that she is actively working on new material and cooking up fresh tracks. Fans have been eagerly awaiting her next musical project for years as she focused on her business ventures and motherhood in the interim. Further details regarding the release date or tracklist remain limited at this stage.
