The Odyssey Box Office: Christopher Nolan Film Eyes Major Milestone

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 02:59 PM · Source checked

Christopher Nolan's latest directorial venture, The Odyssey, continues its steady theatrical run in India, completing twenty days at the box office. Reports indicate the movie is performing well against other major releases, accumulating significant collections over its initial weeks. Industry trackers suggest the film is on course to secure its position among the top-grossing Hollywood releases in the country, reflecting strong audience interest in the acclaimed director's work.
