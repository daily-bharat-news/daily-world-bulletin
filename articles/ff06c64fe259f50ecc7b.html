New Spider-Man film achieves second-biggest global opening weekend

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 07:57 AM · Source checked

The latest Spider-Man film has recorded the second-highest global opening weekend in history. This strong box office performance provides a crucial boost for Disney as the company prepares for the highly anticipated release of Avengers: Doomsday. The impressive debut highlights strong audience demand and offers positive financial momentum for the studio following recent market challenges.
