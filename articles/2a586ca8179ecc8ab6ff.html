Srinivasa Mangapuram Review: Love Story Overshadowed by Violence

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 02:56 PM · Source checked

The newly released film Srinivasa Mangapuram stars Jai Krishna and Rasha Thadani in a central romance. According to reviews, the narrative struggles as the central love story gets overwhelmed by excessive violence. The movie has garnered significant attention within the industry, receiving support and debut celebration wishes from prominent Telugu cinema figures like Mahesh Babu and Prabhas for lead actor Jaya Krishna Ghattamaneni. Meanwhile, audiences are already looking ahead to its eventual digital streaming release, though specific platform details and dates remain anticipated.
