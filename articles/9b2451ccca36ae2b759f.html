Irfan Pathan denies Pakistan media report of Babar Azam interview refusal

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 12:13 PM · Source checked

Former Indian cricketer Irfan Pathan has addressed a controversy involving Pakistani media, stating that reports claiming Pakistan captain Babar Azam refused to interview him were entirely false. Pathan clarified that his face was photoshopped onto a fabricated story designed to defame him. Speaking on the matter, he emphasized that one cannot spend life constantly responding to such baseless claims. The incident has drawn attention across multiple sports news platforms, highlighting issues regarding misinformation in regional sports media.
