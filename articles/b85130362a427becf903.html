Praggnanandhaa Leads Day 3 at Saint Louis Rapid & Blitz, Enters World Top 10

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 01:27 PM · Source checked

Indian grandmaster R. Praggnanandhaa has taken the lead on the third day of the 2026 Saint Louis Rapid and Blitz tournament. During the rapid section, he secured one win and two draws, which also helped him achieve a career-high rapid rating and enter the global top ten rankings. He is currently closely followed in the standings by Javokhir Sindarov and Wesley So. Further updates from the event are expected as the competition continues.
