Aamir Khan and Sunny Deol question Amitabh Bachchan over KBC 18 theme

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 12:04 PM · Source checked

Actors Aamir Khan and Sunny Deol playfully questioned host Amitabh Bachchan regarding the new 'Sochna Padega' theme for Kaun Banega Crorepati Season 18. In response, Amitabh Bachchan offered a witty reply during the star-studded premiere episode. The iconic television game show has marked its 26-year journey with this fresh concept. Meanwhile, the current season has also registered significant growth in Sony LIV advertising revenue, attracting numerous sponsors as the popular show continues its successful run on television.
