Disabled campaigners warn heatwave advice poses risks

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 05:48 AM · Source checked

Disabled campaigners have expressed serious concerns that standard official advice issued for staying safe during severe heatwaves can actually place vulnerable individuals in greater danger. Highlighting the stark realities of coping with extreme temperatures, activists emphasized that the current guidelines fail to address their specific needs, turning a period of extreme weather into a critical struggle for survival. Details regarding alternative official recommendations remain limited at this time.
