NASA and SpaceX Complete Wind Tunnel Tests for Starship

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 12:09 AM · Source checked

NASA and SpaceX have finished new wind tunnel tests on the Super Heavy Version 3 rocket booster at Ames Research Center in California. These evaluations support preparations for the upcoming Artemis III demonstration mission. Experts examined the extreme aerodynamic forces the rocket faces during re-entry to gather critical data. Such collaborations between the agency and industry partners aim to enhance vehicle design and mission safety for future space exploration goals.
