Todd Blanche formally withdraws Trump's anti-weaponisation fund plan

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 04:37 PM · Source checked

Todd Blanche has formally withdrawn a controversial proposal known as the anti-weaponisation fund plan. This decision follows demands from two Republican senators who insisted on receiving a written guarantee confirming that Donald Trump would not revive the disputed initiative in the future. Further details regarding the withdrawal and the specific concerns raised by the senators are currently limited based on the available information.
