Fifa chief Infantino says sorry for investment errors

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 03:07 AM · Source checked

Gianni Infantino will stay on as Fifa president after securing support from senior executives. He offered a sincere apology regarding mistakes connected to his debated private investment plans. Despite the criticism over these financial matters, the leadership backing ensures he will continue in his role at the head of world football.
