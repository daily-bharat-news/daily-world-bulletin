New study shows mosquito bites could help boost immunity against malaria

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 11:59 AM · Source checked

Recent scientific research explores an innovative approach to combat malaria by targeting the parasite inside the insect while allowing mosquitoes to survive. Instead of solely preventing bites, this method investigates whether controlled exposure through mosquito bites might help transform them into natural immune boosters. Institutes such as the Walter and Eliza Hall Institute of Medical Research and HHMI are involved in studying this potential new front in disease eradication. However, further details regarding the clinical application and widespread effectiveness of this method remain limited.
