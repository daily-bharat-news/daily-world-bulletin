Nicole Kidman reveals warning against marrying Tom Cruise

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 02:28 AM · Source checked

Actor Nicole Kidman has disclosed that she was advised against marrying Tom Cruise, with people cautioning her that the decision could negatively impact her professional career. In recent interviews, Kidman reflected on her past marriage and the choices she made during that period. The actor discussed how her life and career trajectory were influenced by her high-profile relationship and subsequent divorce, offering insights into the challenges she faced in the film industry at the time.
