AI used to design new bacteriophages to tackle bacterial resistance

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 11:13 AM · Source checked

Recent studies indicate that artificial intelligence has been successfully utilized to design brand-new bacteriophages, which hold the potential to overcome growing bacterial resistance. While this scientific advancement opens up new avenues in healthcare and microbiology, it has also raised safety concerns among experts regarding the creation of novel viruses that do not exist naturally. As researchers continue to explore these capabilities, careful evaluation of safety measures remains essential to address potential risks associated with AI-generated biological agents.
