US reports first two deaths linked to parasite outbreak

Vinay · 04 Aug 2026, 01:35 AM · Source checked

Health authorities in the United States have confirmed the country's first two fatalities associated with a parasitic illness outbreak causing severe gastrointestinal symptoms. Officials noted that the condition, which typically spreads via contaminated food or water supplies, is generally not considered life-threatening in most cases. Investigations by health agencies are currently underway to trace the source of the infection and manage further spread. Residents have been advised to follow safety guidelines regarding food and water consumption.
