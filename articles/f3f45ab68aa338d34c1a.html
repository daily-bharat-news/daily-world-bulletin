Maruti Suzuki leads India EV export surge with 15,000 shipments in Q1

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 12:00 PM · Source checked

Maruti Suzuki has played a leading role in the electric vehicle export boom from India by shipping more than 15,000 battery-powered units across 47 international markets during the first quarter. This development highlights the growing global footprint of Indian-manufactured electric mobility products. Business Today reports that the automaker achieved this milestone as part of its ongoing international strategy. Further details regarding specific model breakdowns and regional performance figures were not immediately available from the provided sources.
