Severe Infections Linked to Mental Disorders Risk

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 12:18 PM · Source checked

Recent findings published by The Indian Practitioner indicate a concerning connection between severe infections and an elevated risk of developing mental and neurological disorders. Medical experts emphasize the need for heightened clinical awareness and timely intervention for patients recovering from major infectious diseases. While further research is required to fully understand the underlying mechanisms, these insights highlight the critical importance of monitoring psychological health alongside physical recovery in affected individuals.
