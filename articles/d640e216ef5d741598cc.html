Delhi Court Acquits Brij Bhushan Sharan Singh In Harassment Case

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 10:41 AM · Source checked

A Delhi court has acquitted former Wrestling Federation of India chief Brij Bhushan Sharan Singh and Vinod Tomar in the sexual harassment case filed by women wrestlers. Following the verdict, wrestler Vinesh Phogat stated that they will not give up and plan to appeal against the acquittal. Meanwhile, the Maharashtra Congress criticized the government over the court's decision, calling authorities mute, deaf, and blind regarding the allegations raised by the athletes.
