Data centres may pay millions in deposits for power demands

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026 · Source checked

According to reports, operators of data centres could be required to pay hundreds of millions of pounds in deposits to secure their power demands. The energy regulator has suggested imposing a fee ranging between £237,500 and £712,500 per megawatt for new and upcoming projects. Further specifics regarding the implementation timeline, impacted regions, or broader policy frameworks remain limited in the provided information. As details are currently limited, more comprehensive updates from the regulator are awaited.
