Pathirage wins Commonwealth javelin gold as Chopra takes silver

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 03:31 AM · Source checked

Sri Lankan athlete Rumesh Pathirage secured the gold medal in the javelin throw event at the Commonwealth Games, defeating competitors including Neeraj Chopra and Arshad Nadeem. Neeraj Chopra claimed the silver medal, while Yashvir Singh earned the bronze through a strong final throw, resulting in a double podium finish for India. Meanwhile, Indian judokas Asmita and Harsh won gold medals, Tejaswin secured bronze in the decathlon, and ten Indian boxers advanced to their respective finals on Day 9 of the competition.
