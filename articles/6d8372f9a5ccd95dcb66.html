More than half of England now in drought, Environment Agency confirms

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 08:07 PM · Source checked

Following an exceptionally hot and dry period, the Environment Agency has officially declared that more than half of England is now experiencing drought conditions. This prolonged dry weather has severely impacted local rivers, growing crops, and natural habitats across the affected regions. Officials are closely monitoring the situation as water resources remain under pressure. Due to the brief nature of the provided report, further specific regional details and immediate restrictions are currently limited.
