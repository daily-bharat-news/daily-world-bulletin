Russian grain export terminals hit in Ukraine port attack

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 08:58 AM · Source checked

Recent attacks on Black Sea ports and shipping facilities have led to a decline in grain exports from both Russia and Ukraine. As both nations continue to target each other's maritime infrastructure, agricultural trade in the region faces significant disruption. Detailed information regarding the extent of the damage and specific casualties remains limited based on the available reports from the affected area.
