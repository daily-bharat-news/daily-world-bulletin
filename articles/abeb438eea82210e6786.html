Russian missile strikes on Kyiv kill at least nine people

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 09:36 AM · Source checked

Ukrainian officials reported that recent Russian strikes on Kyiv resulted in the deaths of at least nine individuals. This assault is part of an ongoing escalation by Moscow, which has increased deadly ballistic missile attacks targeting the Ukrainian capital. Further details regarding the extent of the damage and rescue operations remain limited as authorities continue to respond to the unfolding situation in the region.
