IIT Bombay Alumnus Jagdish Aphale Appointed Secretary of Ram Temple Trust

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026 · Source checked

The Ayodhya Ram Temple trust has appointed Jagdish Aphale, an IIT Bombay alumnus and RSS leader, as its secretary to oversee temple management. This key appointment follows recent reports regarding an alleged donation theft controversy surrounding the temple's funds. Aphale steps into the role as the trust's first secretary designated to handle administrative responsibilities. Further operational details regarding his specific plans or official duties remain limited in the available context, but his selection comes amid efforts to manage temple administration effectively following the recent donation row.
