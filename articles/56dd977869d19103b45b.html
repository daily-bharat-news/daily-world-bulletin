IMD issues flash flood warning for northeast as monsoon intensifies

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 04:11 PM · Source checked

The India Meteorological Department has issued a flash flood warning for the northeastern region as the southwest monsoon intensifies across the country. Weather authorities are closely monitoring the situation amid heavy rainfall forecasts. Residents in vulnerable areas have been advised to remain cautious and stay updated with official bulletins as weather conditions develop.
