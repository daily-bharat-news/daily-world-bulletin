Vinicius rejoins Real Madrid training amid transfer talk

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 03:27 PM · Source checked

Vinicius Junior has returned to training with Real Madrid. Meanwhile, reports indicate interest from Arsenal, with manager Mikel Arteta reportedly attempting to convince the player to move. Additionally, Vinicius's agency released a strong statement criticizing media reports and calling for greater accountability from journalists. Transfer discussions and contract details continue to circulate across various sports media outlets, highlighting ongoing speculation regarding his future at the club. Further official updates from Real Madrid are awaited as the player resumes squad activities.
