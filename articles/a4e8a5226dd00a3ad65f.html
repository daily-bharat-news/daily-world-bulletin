Kerala rain havoc: Two dead and several trapped amid landslides

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 08:49 AM · Source checked

Heavy rainfall has triggered severe landslides and floods across Kerala, resulting in two fatalities and fears that several people remain trapped. Eight districts are currently placed under a red warning as central regions face the brunt of the weather system. In response to the crisis, the Revenue Minister has convened an urgent meeting to oversee ongoing rescue operations. Authorities have issued high alerts in affected areas, including Kottayam and Idukki, while rescue teams work to assist stranded residents.
