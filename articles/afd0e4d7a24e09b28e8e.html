Tejaswin Shankar wins historic decathlon bronze at Commonwealth Games

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 03:10 AM · Source checked

Despite suffering an injury, athlete Tejaswin Shankar made history by securing a bronze medal in the men's decathlon event at the Commonwealth Games, becoming the first Indian to achieve this feat. His remarkable performance drew praise from Prime Minister Narendra Modi, who congratulated him on the achievement. The milestone highlights a proud moment for Indian athletics on the international stage, showcasing great perseverance and consistency despite physical challenges during the competition.
