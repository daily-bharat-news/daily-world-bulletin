Harbhajan Singh reveals masterplan to stop Vaibhav Sooryavanshi

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 12:56 PM · Source checked

Former Indian cricketer Harbhajan Singh has shared his perspective on how to counter young cricketer Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, suggesting that attacking him is the only way to survive. Meanwhile, other former players including Matthew Hayden and Brett Lee have also praised the young talent, with Lee noting that his actual age remains a major talking point in the cricketing world. Expert analyses highlight the growing attention Sooryavanshi is attracting from cricket legends due to his exceptional abilities on the field.
