Suspected crime boss Daniel Kinahan to be extradited to Ireland

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 06:09 PM · Source checked

Suspected crime boss Daniel Kinahan is set to return to Ireland following his extradition from Dubai in a high-security operation. To facilitate his transport to court, the Irish Prison Service has purchased a specialized bulletproof and bombproof van. Authorities are ensuring strict security measures for the 49-year-old individual's arrival and subsequent legal proceedings.
