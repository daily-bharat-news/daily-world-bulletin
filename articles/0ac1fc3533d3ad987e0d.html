Mohamed Salah in talks to join Turkish club Trabzonspor

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 11:11 AM · Source checked

Egyptian football star Mohamed Salah is reportedly in negotiations to move from Liverpool to Turkish club Trabzonspor. Various sports media outlets indicate that the club aims to finalize the transfer this week. As discussions progress, questions are being raised regarding whether this move is the right career choice, with interest also noted from teams in Spain and Saudi Arabia. Further details regarding the agreement remain limited at this stage.
