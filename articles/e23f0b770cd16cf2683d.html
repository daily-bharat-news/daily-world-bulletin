NASA's Curiosity Rover Spots Vast Martian Polygon Field

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 09:36 PM · Source checked

NASA's Curiosity Mars rover has documented a massive expanse of terrain featuring surface patterns known as polygons. Captured by the rover using a 360-degree camera view on June 19 and 20, 2026, corresponding to the 4,930th and 4,931st Martian sols of the mission, this discovery stands out because the vehicle has encountered such formations before, yet never in such high concentration within a single location. Further details regarding the exact geological origins of this specific region remain limited based on the current update.
