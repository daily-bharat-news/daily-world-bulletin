Delhi Police denies Jantar Mantar closure amid fresh CJP protest threats

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 01:12 PM · Source checked

Delhi Police has refuted rumors regarding the closure of Jantar Mantar, confirming that the location remains an active site. Security has been stepped up with barricades welded in place following fresh protest threats from the CJP over alleged AI-assisted tactics. Authorities are actively monitoring the situation to maintain law and order, while dismissing speculation about restricting access to the designated demonstration area.
