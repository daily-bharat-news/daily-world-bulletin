India EV Penetration Rises To 8.26% As Registrations Cross 1 Crore

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 12:12 AM · Source checked

Data reveals that India has achieved a significant milestone in its green mobility transition, with electric vehicle penetration increasing to 8.26 percent. Total registered electric vehicles across the country have now surpassed the one crore mark, according to the latest industry reports. This growth highlights the rising adoption of eco-friendly transport options among consumers nationwide, driven by supportive policies and expanding charging infrastructure. Further details on segment-wise contributions and regional distribution are presently limited.
