Kabaddi Player's Wife Dies By Suicide In Haryana

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 12:24 AM · Source checked

The 23-year-old wife of international Kabaddi player Shilu Balhara allegedly died by suicide in a Rohtak village in Haryana. Local police have detained the player following a family dispute and are conducting a thorough investigation into the suspicious circumstances surrounding the tragic incident. Further details are currently limited as authorities continue their probe.
