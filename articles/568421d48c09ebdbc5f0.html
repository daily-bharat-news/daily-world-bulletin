Ebola deaths in DRC cross 2,000 amid vaccine race

Vinay · 12 Aug 2026, 12:28 AM · Source checked

The death toll from Ebola in the Democratic Republic of Congo has surpassed 2,000 during the country's fastest-growing outbreak. Amid this escalation, scientists are racing to develop a vaccine. The World Health Organization and the Africa CDC have called for urgent, community-led action to help contain the virus. Furthermore, deaths occurring at home have highlighted critical gaps in the ongoing response efforts within the nation.
