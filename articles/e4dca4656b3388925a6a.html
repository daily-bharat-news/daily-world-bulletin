NASA Funds 18 Visionary Aerospace Technology Concepts

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 02:34 AM · Source checked

The NASA Innovative Advanced Concepts program has selected 18 new projects to advance aerospace technologies, focusing on solar system exploration and universe comprehension. These Phase I awards total $3.2 million, granting up to $175,000 per project for a nine-month initial study. This funding supports creative concepts aimed at improving future space missions and scientific investigations.
