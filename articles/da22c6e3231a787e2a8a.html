Stoinis leads Southern Brave to first win in Hundred

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 06:21 AM · Source checked

Marcus Stoinis played a key role as Southern Brave secured their first victory of the tournament at the fourth attempt, defeating Birmingham Phoenix by 24 runs. Contributions from players like Lee and Bouchier further supported the team's effort in Matchday 10. Meanwhile, coverage from various sports networks highlighted the ongoing action in the competition, including updates on Indian players participating in the tournament.
