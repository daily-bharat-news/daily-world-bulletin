VVS Laxman praises Virat Kohli as an exemplary role model

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 04:03 PM · Source checked

Former cricketer VVS Laxman has lauded Virat Kohli, describing him as a great role model for young players. Laxman highlighted Kohli's exceptional professionalism, elite fitness standards, and high work ethic. According to him, numerous cricketers wish to emulate the star batter due to the remarkable benchmarks he has set throughout his career and his positive influence on youngsters at the Centre of Excellence. Further details regarding the statements are limited as per the available reports.
