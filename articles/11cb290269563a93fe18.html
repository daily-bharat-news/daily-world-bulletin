NASA Announces 2026 IGARSS Hyperwall Schedule

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 12:27 AM · Source checked

NASA has released its Hyperwall storytelling schedule for the upcoming IGARSS 2026 event. Visitors can join NASA experts at Booth number 100 in the Exhibit Hall for scheduled presentations. The agenda begins on Monday, August 10, featuring sessions by Mike Seablom on future Earth science technology and Karen St. Germain discussing new missions and technical innovation for Earth system insights. Further presentations continue on Tuesday, August 11, including a session on open-source multisensor platforms. Details for subsequent events are limited based on the current schedule announcement.
