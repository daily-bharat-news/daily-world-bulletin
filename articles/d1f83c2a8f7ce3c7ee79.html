Liverpool prepare move for PSG winger Bradley Barcola

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 09:50 PM · Source checked

Liverpool are reportedly preparing an opening offer for Paris Saint-Germain winger Bradley Barcola, with discussions expected to commence soon. Sources indicate that the Merseyside club is pushing for a deal valued at around £100m for the French player. In addition to these efforts, reports suggest that Liverpool have already identified three alternative transfer options if the move for Barcola does not materialize. Further details regarding the potential negotiations remain limited as developments unfold in the transfer market.
