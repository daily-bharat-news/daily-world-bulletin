Are AI recruitment tools impacting women returning to work?

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 03:12 PM · Source checked

Concerns are rising that artificial intelligence recruitment tools might disadvantage women who are attempting to return to the workplace. Job seekers are reportedly altering their resumes to bypass automated screening systems, raising questions about fairness and bias in modern hiring processes. As companies increasingly rely on technology to manage job applications, experts are examining whether these systems create unintended barriers for female candidates seeking career opportunities. Further details on the extent of these impacts are currently limited.
