Saudi-led group finalises $55bn acquisition of gaming firm EA

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 04:39 AM · Source checked

A consortium led by Saudi Arabia has successfully concluded a $55 billion purchase of Electronic Arts, taking the major gaming company into private ownership. This massive buyout loads the maker of popular titles like The Sims and EA FC with substantial debt. Details regarding the long-term financial restructuring and strategic plans for the publisher remain limited following the completion of the transaction.
