Brett Lee Picks Jacques Kallis as the Greatest Cricketer of All Time

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 08:10 AM · Source checked

Former Australian fast bowler Brett Lee has named former South African all-rounder Jacques Kallis as the greatest cricketer of all time, overlooking batting legends like Sachin Tendulkar and Virat Kohli. While Lee acknowledged Sachin Tendulkar as the best batter, he selected Kallis for his exceptional all-round contributions to the sport. Lee also discussed the challenges of facing Indian batting icons such as Tendulkar, Kohli, and Rohit Sharma during his playing career, highlighting what made them exceptionally difficult to dismiss.
