Air India A320 faced rare flight control loss on Phuket route, Airbus report shows

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 10:24 AM · Source checked

An initial Airbus analysis indicates that an Air India A320 aircraft briefly lost key flight controls during a Phuket-Delhi flight, leading to a sudden altitude drop. Pilots reportedly struggled to manage the rare malfunction. Airbus has shared its initial report with the airline and requested specific maintenance records. Meanwhile, this incident follows separate reports regarding an Air India CEO warning issued after a pilot drug test controversy. Further details regarding the exact technical failure and subsequent safety measures remain limited.
