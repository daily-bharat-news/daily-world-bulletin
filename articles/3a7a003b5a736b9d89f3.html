Prime Minister Andy Burnham announces new powers for councils

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 03:03 AM · Source checked

Prime Minister Andy Burnham has announced that local councils are set to receive additional powers to curb the spread of vape and betting shops. The Prime Minister stated that town centres across the region had been hollowed out following decades of continuous decline. The new measures are intended to help local authorities take better control of their high streets and address the changing landscape of retail areas.
