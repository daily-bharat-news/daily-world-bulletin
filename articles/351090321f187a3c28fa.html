Thames Water boss calls current leakage targets unrealistic

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 04:36 AM · Source checked

Thames Water Chief Executive Chris Weston stated that the company's leakage reduction targets are currently not achievable. Speaking about the utility firm's operational goals, Weston mentioned that while the organization genuinely wants to improve its performance and do better for consumers, certain mandated targets remain unrealistic. Details regarding specific future adjustments to these regulatory goals were limited in the recent announcement.
