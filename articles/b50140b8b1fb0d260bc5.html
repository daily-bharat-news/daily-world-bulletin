NASA shares view of August 2026 solar eclipse

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 09:31 AM · Source checked

Details are limited as NASA highlighted a celestial event where the Moon moved between the Sun and Earth. This alignment created a striking total solar eclipse that will be visible from Spain in August 2026. The phenomenon offers scientists and observers a remarkable opportunity to witness the rare cosmic alignment.
