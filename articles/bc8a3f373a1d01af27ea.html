Nigerian Army Rescues 33 Kidnapped People in Zamfara

Vinay · 10 Aug 2026, 01:27 AM · Source checked

The Nigerian military has successfully rescued 33 individuals who were abducted by armed men. The victims were taken on Saturday while travelling through Zamfara state, located in the north-western region of the country. Officials confirmed their safe recovery following the incident, though further details regarding the rescue operation and the condition of those freed remain limited at this time.
