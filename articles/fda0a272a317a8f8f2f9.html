Complaint Filed Against Pappu Yadav Over Parliament Skit

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 09:46 AM · Source checked

A formal complaint has been filed against independent MP Pappu Yadav following a controversial skit enacted outside Parliament regarding alleged temple donation scams. The protest, which featured saffron robes, drew sharp condemnation from the BJP, Vishva Hindu Parishad, and various seers, who demanded the expulsion of the participating MPs. Meanwhile, Rajya Sabha proceedings were adjourned amid widespread protests. While regional parties like the SP urged the opposition to focus on the Ayodhya issue, the Congress party shifted its primary attention toward police crackdowns, according to reports.
