Karoline Leavitt to step down as White House press secretary

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 04:28 AM · Source checked

US President Donald Trump has announced that White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt is leaving her position. According to the president, Leavitt has decided to step down from her role in order to spend more time with her young children and her family. Further details regarding her departure and who might succeed her in the administration have not been immediately provided by the administration.
