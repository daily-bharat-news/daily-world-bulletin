Shafique shines as Pakistan take control in second Test

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 06:46 AM · Source checked

On day three of the second Test against West Indies, Pakistan gained the upper hand as spinners dominated proceedings. West Indies were bowled out for 344 runs in their first innings following a morning collapse. Opening batsman Shafique played a significant role, breaking a long-standing drought and helping Pakistan secure a commanding position in the match. While batting challenges remained for both sides, disciplined bowling helped the visiting team take control of the contest.
