Mumbai weather: Moderate rain likely today, IMD issues yellow alert for Thane

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 10:51 AM · Source checked

The weather department has forecast moderate rainfall for Mumbai today. Meanwhile, the India Meteorological Department has issued a yellow alert for the neighbouring district of Thane. Residents are advised to stay updated on local weather conditions as precipitation is expected to continue. Official sources have shared timely advisories to help the public plan their daily commutes and activities accordingly.
