Mini Mathur wins reality show Alliance, takes home Rs 50 lakh

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 01:20 PM · Source checked

Mini Mathur has emerged victorious in the reality show Alliance, defeating Aly Goni to lift the trophy and win a cash prize of Rs 50 lakh. Describing her victory journey as absolutely surreal, she reflected on age and gender biases, stating that people often underestimate individuals beyond a certain age and gender. She also addressed discussions regarding controlling co-participant Nikhil Chinapa, humorously noting that they acted as each other's puppets during the competition.
