Indian hockey teams change jersey to saffron for World Cup

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 02:33 PM · Source checked

The Indian hockey teams have switched from their traditional blue kit to a saffron jersey ahead of the World Cup, drawing criticism from opposition politicians and former players, including Viren Rasquinha who questioned the logic behind the color change. Hockey India has defended the decision against the backlash. Meanwhile, political figures have also commented on the development, with criticism directed at the ruling party over the move. As details are limited, further official statements from the federation regarding the specific reasoning behind the new kit choice are awaited.
