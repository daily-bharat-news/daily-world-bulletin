The Odyssey crosses USD 1 billion at the global box office

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 09:42 AM · Source checked

Christopher Nolan's directorial venture 'The Odyssey' has successfully crossed the USD 1 billion milestone globally. With this achievement, it becomes the fifth film to enter the billion-dollar club in 2026. This also marks Christopher Nolan's first film to reach the coveted financial milestone since 'The Dark Knight Rises'. Featuring Matt Damon in a prominent role, reports indicate the feature is on track to emerge as the filmmaker's highest-grossing cinematic release to date, driven by massive box office collections.
