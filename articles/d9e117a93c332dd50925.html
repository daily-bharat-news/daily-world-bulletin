Check energy bills now to claim potential £150 discount

Vinay · 10 Aug 2026, 05:17 AM · Source checked

Residents in the UK are being advised to verify their energy account details promptly. Certain individuals must ensure their names are officially listed on their utility bills prior to the upcoming deadline in order to qualify for financial assistance worth £150. Failing to update these records could mean missing out on crucial support for household energy costs. Consumers should review their provider statements and take necessary action quickly to secure the eligible reduction.
