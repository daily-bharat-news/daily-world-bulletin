Manpreet Singh eyes World Cup title to end India's wait

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 11:59 AM · Source checked

Senior Indian hockey player Manpreet Singh has indicated that the upcoming tournament could potentially be his final World Cup appearance. As the team prepares for the prestigious event, players and coaches are focusing on managing tournament pressure, trusting their rigorous preparations, and refining key areas like defence and set-pieces. With inputs from former greats and dedicated mental conditioning through visualisation, the squad remains determined to lift the trophy and end the country's long-standing wait for the title, according to reports.
