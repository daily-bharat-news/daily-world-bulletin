Mumbai Weather: IMD Issues Rain Warning And High Tide Alert

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 12:04 PM · Source checked

The India Meteorological Department has issued a rain warning and a high tide alert for Mumbai. Citizens are advised to stay updated on the latest weather forecasts and follow local advisories. Details regarding potential school closures and official safety measures are limited at this stage. Authorities continue to monitor conditions closely as the weather system develops across the region.
