Gaurav Khanna injured during stunt on Khatron Ke Khiladi 15

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 03:20 PM · Source checked

Contestant Gaurav Khanna faced severe pain and sustained bruises and laser burns on his back after being hit by rubber bullets and pellets during a task on the reality show Khatron Ke Khiladi 15. Describing the incident as his most painful experience ever, the actor's ordeal sparked sharp reactions online, with internet users criticizing the use of such tactics and calling the treatment unacceptable. Viewers expressed shock over the severe nature of the stunt, highlighting the physical toll taken on the participants during the filming process.
