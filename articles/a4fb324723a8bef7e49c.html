IMD issues heavy rain warning for four states, orange alert in Himachal

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 11:02 AM · Source checked

The India Meteorological Department has issued warnings for heavy to very heavy rainfall across four states. Additionally, authorities have placed Himachal Pradesh under an orange alert due to impending severe weather conditions. Residents in the affected regions are advised to stay updated with local forecasts and exercise caution as persistent downpours may disrupt normal daily life, travel, and local infrastructure.
