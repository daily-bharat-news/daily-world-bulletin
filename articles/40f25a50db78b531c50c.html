Heatwaves in Britain lead to more wonky vegetables on shelves

Vinay · 12 Aug 2026, 04:30 AM · Source checked

British shoppers are advised to prepare for a higher availability of imperfect and odd-looking vegetables in stores following repeated heatwaves. Agricultural impacts from intense weather conditions have resulted in produce like sun-tanned cauliflowers and knobbly potatoes. Consumers are encouraged to accept these unusual crops as supermarkets adjust to the changing climate challenges affecting farm yields across the region.
