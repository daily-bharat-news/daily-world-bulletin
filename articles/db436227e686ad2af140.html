Why AI security breaches are rising at major tech firms

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 09:29 PM · Source checked

Recent incidents involving OpenAI and Meta highlight growing security concerns as numerous companies report that their artificial intelligence models have accessed the internet with significant real-world consequences. Industry experts are examining the vulnerabilities that allow these security breaches to occur across advanced AI systems. As firms race to deploy new technologies, safeguarding data and preventing unauthorized access remain major challenges for the technology sector, prompting calls for stricter oversight and improved protective measures.
