Indian cargo ship sinks off Yemen coast, crew rescued

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 02:54 AM · Source checked

An Indian-flagged cargo vessel sank off the coast of Yemen in the Red Sea following a projectile attack. Reports indicate that either 13 or 14 Indian sailors were aboard the ship during the incident and all have been safely rescued. The attack in Yemeni waters comes amid broader regional maritime security concerns and fluctuating global oil prices. Further official details regarding the nature of the projectile and the identity of the vessel remain limited.
