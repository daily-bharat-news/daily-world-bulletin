Prediabetes and obesity surged among Chennai adolescents in 17-year study

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 12:12 AM · Source checked

A recent study conducted in Chennai reveals a significant rise in cases of prediabetes and obesity among adolescents over a span of 17 years. Researchers tracked health trends in this demographic, noting a sharp increase in metabolic health concerns. Public health experts emphasize the urgent need for targeted interventions, lifestyle modifications, and preventive healthcare strategies to address the growing burden of chronic conditions among young people in the region. Further details regarding the exact methodology and participant demographics remain limited based on current reports.
