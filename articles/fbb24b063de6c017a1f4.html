Manushi Chhillar recalls tough phase of retaking NEET 2015 after paper leak

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 12:10 AM · Source checked

Actor Manushi Chhillar has opened up about the difficult period she faced when she had to retake the NEET entrance exam in 2015 following a paper leak. She shared that the experience was extremely challenging, testing her endurance and forcing her to make significant personal sacrifices. Meanwhile, Nora Fatehi also extended her support to students, stating that no examination result or system should ever cost a person's life.
