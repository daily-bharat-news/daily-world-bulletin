West Virginia Engineer Finds Inspiring Path to NASA Career

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 12:33 AM · Source checked

Fletcher Newell grew up in Grafton, West Virginia, developing an early fascination with science fiction and space. His passion for exploration intensified in 2013 during a fourth-grade school field trip to a nearby NASA facility, specifically NASA’s Katherine Johnson Independent Verification and Validation facility. This pivotal early experience eventually guided him toward pursuing a professional career in engineering and space science within his home state, connecting his childhood dreams with practical contributions to ongoing space missions.
