NASA Langley Opens First Major New Wind Tunnel in Decades

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 01:49 AM · Source checked

NASA leadership and Virginia government officials inaugurated the Flight Dynamics Research Facility at NASA’s Langley Research Center in Hampton, Virginia, on Friday, July 31, 2026. This marks the agency's first major new wind tunnel in over forty years. The advanced facility is designed to support research and technology development aimed at furthering NASA's objectives in aeronautics, space exploration, and science. Details regarding specific upcoming projects or testing schedules are currently limited.
