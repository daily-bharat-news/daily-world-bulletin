NASA Astronaut Trains Aboard WB-57 Aircraft for Future Space Missions

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 08:45 PM · Source checked

NASA astronaut Adam Fuhrmann recently participated in a training flight using the agency's WB-57 aircraft. Conducted in July 2026, this high-altitude exercise is designed to help crew members adapt to confined spaces and practice operating complex systems while wearing pressure suits. Such rigorous preparations equip astronauts for upcoming spaceflights destined for the International Space Station, the Moon, and beyond.
