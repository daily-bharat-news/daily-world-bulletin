Massive Attack respond after Singapore ban over Palestine display

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 07:01 AM · Source checked

British band Massive Attack have expressed surprise and disappointment following action taken against them in Singapore over a pro-Palestine display. The group faced scrutiny after shouting free Palestine during a live performance in the country. Authorities imposed restrictions, prompting the musicians to publicly address the decision and voice their concern regarding the handling of the incident.
