IMD Issues Heavy Rain Warning For Delhi-NCR, MP, And Gujarat

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 10:04 AM · Source checked

The India Meteorological Department has issued warnings for heavy rainfall across multiple states. Regions including Madhya Pradesh, Delhi-NCR, and Gujarat are expected to experience intense weather conditions. Citizens and local authorities in these affected areas have been advised to remain alert and take necessary precautions against potential disruptions caused by the severe downpour. Further updates regarding the progression of the weather system will be provided as more information becomes available.
