WHO Agency Classifies Three Common Medicines as Carcinogenic

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 08:06 AM · Source checked

The International Agency for Research on Cancer, part of the WHO, has classified three widely used medicines as carcinogenic. Following this classification, medical experts have strongly urged patients currently taking these drugs not to discontinue their treatment without consulting a qualified physician. The active ingredients found in popular blood pressure medications were specifically evaluated by the expert panel. Further details regarding the exact nature of the classification and medical advice are limited.
