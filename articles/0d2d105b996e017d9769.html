New Study Shows Vitamin D Deficiency Affects Most Children

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 01:59 AM · Source checked

A recent study published by EMJ highlights that vitamin D deficiency is prevalent among a significant majority of children. The research emphasizes the widespread nature of this health concern in the pediatric population. Experts suggest that further investigation is needed to understand the implications of these findings on child health. Currently, specific details regarding the methodology, geographical scope, and exact participant numbers in the study are limited in the available reports.
