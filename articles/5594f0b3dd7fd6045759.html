US Report Alleges Dozens of Nations Helped China Dodge Tariffs

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 02:09 PM · Source checked

A recent United States report claims that numerous countries assisted China in circumventing higher import duties imposed during the Trump administration. According to the findings, Chinese goods were routed through nations with lower tariff rates to avoid the steeper levies. Details regarding the specific countries involved and the full scope of the report remain limited based on the initial information provided.
