Jharkhand: Protests intensify over alleged exam irregularities

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 03:00 PM · Source checked

Demonstrations are escalating in Jharkhand regarding purported irregularities in examinations. Political figures have urged authorities to heed the grievances of the affected youth. Students in Ranchi have formed an eleven-member delegation to engage in discussions with state officials. Meanwhile, a student activist on a hunger strike continues to demand immediate reforms. Comprehensive official details regarding the talks and the specific examination grievances remain limited at this stage.
