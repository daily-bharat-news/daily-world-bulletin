Golmaal Actor Susmita Mukherjee On Doing C-Grade Films To Clear Debt

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 01:28 PM · Source checked

Veteran actor Susmita Mukherjee recently revealed that she was forced to work in C-grade films out of helplessness after facing a massive financial crisis and a debt of Rs 1 crore. The Golmaal fame actor stated that the severe monetary trouble pushed her to take up roles she later regretted to clear her dues. Reports indicate that she described the phase as selling her soul due to the extreme financial distress experienced by her family at the time.
