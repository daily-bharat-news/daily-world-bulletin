Batwara 1947 Day 1 Box Office: Sunny Deol Starrer Earns Over 5 Crore

Vinay · 15 Aug 2026, 02:18 AM · Source checked

Sunny Deol and Preity Zinta's partition drama 'Batwara 1947' collected approximately 5.75 crore on its opening day in India. Early trends indicate that the film faced tough competition and lagged behind 'Awarapan 2'. Critics noted that despite good intentions, the movie suffered from dated storytelling and stilted execution, being described as old wine in an old bottle. Reviews from multiple publications highlighted that the veteran actor was let down by the overall script and presentation.
