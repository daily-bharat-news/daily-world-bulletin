BCCI plans standardised fitness tests following England tour setbacks

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 06:32 AM · Source checked

Following concerns regarding player fitness during the recent England tour, the Board of Control for Cricket in India has decided to implement standardised Bronco and two-kilometre fitness tests. Reports indicate that the cricket board has questioned the national team physiotherapist over a noticeable decline in overall fitness standards. Additionally, discussions are ongoing regarding workload management strategies for fast bowlers like Jasprit Bumrah amidst rising injury concerns, aiming to improve player longevity and endurance for future international assignments.
