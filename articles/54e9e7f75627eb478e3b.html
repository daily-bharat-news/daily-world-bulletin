Chhavi Mittal considered treating cancer without medicine, later chose therapy

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 06:00 AM · Source checked

Actor Chhavi Mittal revealed that she initially thought about treating her cancer diagnosis without medication. However, she ultimately decided to undergo proper medical treatment because saving one's life is the topmost priority. Mittal has been quite vocal about her health journey and recovery, sharing her experiences to raise awareness among her followers and fans regarding serious illnesses and the importance of timely medical intervention.
