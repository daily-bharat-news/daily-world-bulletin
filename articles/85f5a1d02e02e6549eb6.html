Supreme Court refuses to relax bail conditions for Ashish Mishra

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 12:00 PM · Source checked

The Supreme Court of India has declined to relax the bail terms of Ashish Mishra, the son of a Bharatiya Janata Party leader, in connection with the Lakhimpur Kheri violence case. Various media reports confirmed that the apex court rejected the plea seeking relaxation of the existing conditions. Mishra was previously granted bail related to the mass murder case that took place during farmers' protests in Uttar Pradesh. Further details regarding the court proceedings remain limited based on the current inputs.
