NASA Announces Florida Event to Celebrate American Air and Space Leadership

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 02:38 AM · Source checked

NASA has scheduled a news conference for Friday, August 14, at 2:30 p.m. Eastern Daylight Time. The event will take place live from the agency's Kennedy Space Center in Florida, specifically within the XLV Hangar at the Shuttle Landing Facility. Media representatives and digital creators are invited to attend the gathering in person. Officials will use the announcement to preview a forthcoming event at the space center, highlighting achievements in American air and space leadership. Further details regarding the upcoming schedule remain limited based on current announcements.
