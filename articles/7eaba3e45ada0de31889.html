Iran says deal with Oman on Strait of Hormuz is in final stages

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 06:49 AM · Source checked

Tehran has announced that negotiations regarding an agreement with Oman concerning the Strait of Hormuz are currently in their final stages. This vital waterway is crucial for global shipping and energy supplies. Meanwhile, neither the United States nor the Omani government has issued any official comments regarding the proposed pact on this important shipping route. Further details about the specific terms and implementation of the arrangement remain limited at this moment.
