Two More People Cured of HIV After Stem Cell Transplants

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 01:56 AM · Source checked

Recent reports indicate that two additional individuals have experienced remission from HIV following stem cell transplants. This development builds on a landmark 2007 case where a cancer treatment provided unexpected insights into long-term HIV remission. While these procedures remain complex and are typically used as a last resort for cancer, the outcomes offer valuable scientific clues for future research and treatment strategies targeting the virus. Further details on the specific cases and medical findings are currently limited.
