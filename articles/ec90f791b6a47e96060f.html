'Jana Nayagan' Day 7 Box Office: Vijay and Pooja Hegde film sees midweek dip

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 05:46 AM · Source checked

On its seventh day at the box office, the Thalapathy Vijay and Pooja Hegde starrer 'Jana Nayagan' experienced a standard midweek collection drop. Despite the dip, the movie successfully crossed the significant Rs 250-crore milestone globally, reaching an estimated Rs 255.63 crore worldwide. Producer K Venkat Narayana acknowledged the actor's strong appeal for the film's theatrical run, while industry observers note that regional cinema continues to perform well overall despite the recent slowing down of collections.
