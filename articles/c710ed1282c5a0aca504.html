Sri Lanka captain warns India ahead of Galle Test series

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 07:59 AM · Source checked

Ahead of the upcoming Test series in Sri Lanka, the home team captain has issued a warning, stating that they hold the upper hand against India. The hosts are placing their trust in a strong spin bowling attack to deliver results on familiar pitches. Former all-rounder Farveez Maharoof has noted this as the best opportunity for Sri Lanka to defeat India, highlighting Dinesh Chandimal's crucial role. Meanwhile, Indian batters have been cautioned to remain careful against the local bowling threats at Galle.
