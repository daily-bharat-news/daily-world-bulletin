IIT alumnus Jagdish Aphale appointed secretary of Ayodhya Ram Temple trust

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 11:52 AM · Source checked

Jagdish Aphale, an alumnus of IIT Bombay and an RSS leader, has been appointed as the new secretary of the Ayodhya Ram Temple trust. This appointment by the Ram Mandir trust comes amid an ongoing donation theft controversy surrounding the temple management. Reports indicate that the decision aims to streamline administrative functions and bring in experienced leadership to handle the temple's affairs following the recent issues related to contributions. Further details regarding his official responsibilities and tenure are expected to be announced by the trust soon.
