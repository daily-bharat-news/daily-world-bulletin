NASA Students Conduct Airborne Research in Texas

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 02:06 AM · Source checked

Students participating in NASA's Student Airborne Research Program recently concluded their summer session on July 27 along the Texas Gulf Coast. During the program, participants engaged in hands-on atmospheric research by launching a scientific instrument known as an ozonesonde, which is a large balloon used to study the atmosphere despite challenging gusty wind conditions.
