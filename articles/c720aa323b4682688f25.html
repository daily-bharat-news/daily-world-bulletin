Nvidia secures $500bn from major investors for AI data centres

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 04:01 AM · Source checked

Chipmaker Nvidia has secured $500bn from major investors to build advanced infrastructure for artificial intelligence. The substantial funding will be directed towards establishing new data centres. These facilities are designed to house, operate, and cool extensive configurations of computer chips that handle complex AI data processing and associated tasks.
