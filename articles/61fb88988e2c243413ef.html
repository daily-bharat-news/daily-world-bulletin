Nutrition Experts Urge Balanced Diet to Fight Lifestyle Diseases

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 11:32 AM · Source checked

Health experts have warned that rising consumption of processed foods and increasingly sedentary lifestyles are driving a metabolic health crisis across India. Nutrition specialists highlight that obesity rates are climbing, emphasizing the urgent need for balanced diets. According to reports, everyday foods can help restore the body's internal balance and combat lifestyle-related illnesses. Medical professionals are calling for greater awareness regarding dietary habits and physical activity to improve overall health outcomes in the country.
