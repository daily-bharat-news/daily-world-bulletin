Global Trial Tests Phage Therapy for Recurrent UTIs

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 09:05 PM · Source checked

A major global clinical trial backed by €15 million has been launched to evaluate phage therapy as a potential alternative to antibiotics for treating recurrent urinary tract infections. The research aims to combat the growing issue of antibiotic resistance, offering a new approach to prevention and management. Institutions like the University of Reading are involved in the initiative, which seeks to transform how these common infections are handled globally. Further details regarding trial phases and participant criteria are currently limited.
