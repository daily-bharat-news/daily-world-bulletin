NASA's Curiosity Mars Rover Captures Sand-Capped Butte

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 09:38 PM · Source checked

NASA's Curiosity Mars rover recently photographed a distinctive sand-capped butte nicknamed "Miraflores" using its Mast Camera on June 11, 2026. Marking the 4,923rd Martian day of the mission, this geological feature stands approximately 20 feet tall. Scientists explain that the formation remained intact as the surrounding rock gradually eroded over time, deepening the local valley. Details are limited beyond the initial rover observations provided in the mission log.
