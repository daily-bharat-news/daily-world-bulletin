Mahesh Babu’s First Look in S.S. Rajamouli’s Varanasi Unveiled

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 11:17 AM · Source checked

The first look of actor Mahesh Babu in S.S. Rajamouli’s upcoming film Varanasi has been officially released. The project also features Prithviraj Sukumaran as a tech genius named Kumbha, with Mahesh Babu playing a character named Rudhra. Meanwhile, actor Priyanka Chopra shared behind-the-scenes photographs with Mahesh Babu from their Africa schedule, describing the experience as an ultimate adventure. While the makers have revealed initial details and posters, audience reactions on social media have been mixed regarding the newly unveiled visuals from the international schedule.
