Keep Babar Azam away from English media, PCB advised ahead of tour

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 05:00 AM · Source checked

Ahead of Pakistan's cricket tour to England, a former Pakistan captain has advised the Pakistan Cricket Board to keep batter Babar Azam away from the English media to ease pressure. Reports suggest that instead of Babar, Test captain Shan Masood's name has been floated for media duties. Analysts and former players have described the upcoming tour as a difficult assignment, prompting suggestions to shield the star batsman from intense media scrutiny during the series. Detailed information regarding official PCB responses remains limited.
