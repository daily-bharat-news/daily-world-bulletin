Xbox Series X prices increase in the UK due to rising memory chip costs

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 04:48 PM · Source checked

Xbox gaming consoles are now significantly more expensive in the United Kingdom following a price hike driven by increasing memory chip costs. According to reports, the cost of one specific model has jumped by as much as forty-three percent, resulting in a substantial increase of one hundred and seventy pounds. Detailed information regarding the revised pricing structure across other regional markets remains limited at this time.
