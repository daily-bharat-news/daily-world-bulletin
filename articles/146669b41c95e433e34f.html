Ebola outbreak severely impacts women and children in eastern DR Congo

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 11:14 AM · Source checked

United Nations reports indicate that the Ebola outbreak in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo has severely affected women and children. Over 300 child deaths have been linked to the crisis, with UNICEF warning that essential health services are under threat. Meanwhile, Africa CDC and WHO have appealed for urgent community-led interventions to control the spread. Health workers have also raised concerns regarding unpaid wages and severe occupational hazards while responding to the epidemic, which has surpassed 4,000 total cases.
