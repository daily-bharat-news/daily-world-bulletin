Maharashtra bans non-dairy paneer with strict penalties

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 09:36 AM · Source checked

The Maharashtra government has prohibited the use of non-dairy and analogue paneer across all hotels and restaurants for a period of one year due to potential health concerns. Establishments found violating this new directive will face severe legal consequences, including a jail term of up to six months and a financial penalty of Rs 1 lakh. Authorities have highlighted the importance of food safety and advised citizens to check product purity, while also encouraging the consumption of healthy alternatives available in the market.
