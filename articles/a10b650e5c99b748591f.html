Ebola tracing improves in DR Congo, but virus continues to spread

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 01:26 AM · Source checked

Efforts to trace Ebola contacts have improved in the Democratic Republic of Congo, yet the ongoing outbreak remains ahead of containment measures. According to the WHO chief, the epidemic is on course to become the deadliest in the region's history. The fast-moving virus has now affected a sixth province, with officials confirming a new fatality. Experts note that the outbreak stems from a new animal transmission event, posing continuous challenges for health workers trying to control the spread.
