US Vice-President JD Vance calls PM Modi to discuss bilateral ties

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 03:36 AM · Source checked

United States Vice-President JD Vance recently spoke with Prime Minister Narendra Modi over the phone to explore ways to strengthen and expand the India-US partnership. This diplomatic interaction occurred shortly after the US Senate cleared a legislation regarding a 100 percent tariff bill. Additionally, the discussions between the two leaders touched upon ongoing matters such as Russia sanctions. Both sides focused on enhancing cooperation and further building upon the existing bilateral relationship between the two nations.
