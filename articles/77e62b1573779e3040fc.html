Awarapan 2 leads Batwara 1947 at Independence Day box office

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 10:50 AM · Source checked

The Independence Day box office clash features Emraan Hashmi's thriller 'Awarapan 2' taking the lead against Sunny Deol's partition drama 'Batwara 1947'. While 'Awarapan 2' has raced ahead in ticket sales, reviews indicate that the Emraan Hashmi starrer faces script limitations and criticism over relying on nostalgia. Meanwhile, 'Batwara 1947' has received positive reviews from audiences and critics alike, setting up an interesting theatrical competition between the two major releases during the holiday period.
