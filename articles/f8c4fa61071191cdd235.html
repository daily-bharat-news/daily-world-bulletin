Yash defends Kiara Advani over Toxic song trolling

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 08:28 AM · Source checked

Kannada actor Yash has come out in support of Kiara Advani following online trolling over a song from their upcoming film Toxic. Praising her dedication and hard work, Yash advised the actor to ignore the criticism and focus entirely on her beliefs and craft. He described her as down-to-earth and praised her commitment to the director's vision despite facing difficult circumstances during the project. Further details regarding the controversy and the film's release remain limited at present.
