President Grants Assent To Anti-Paper Leak Bill Enhancing Punishments

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 08:13 AM · Source checked

The President has granted assent to the amendment enhancing punishments for unfair means in public examinations. This development follows the passage of the anti-paper leak Bill by Parliament amid an Opposition walkout in the Rajya Sabha. Meanwhile, political reactions have emerged, with leaders like Raghav Chadha speaking on the NEET issue and the Punjab BJP welcoming the new legislation aimed at curbing examination malpractices. Further details regarding the implementation and specific penalty structures remain limited based on the provided updates.
