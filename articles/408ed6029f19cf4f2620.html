Maharashtra Cop's Quick Response Saves Sukhbir Badal

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 11:37 AM · Source checked

A prompt reaction by a Maharashtra police officer prevented a major incident during an attack on Shiromani Akali Dal leader Sukhbir Badal in Nanded. Following the incident, Mr. Badal stated he was unafraid. Medical professionals confirmed that the SAD chief underwent a surgery lasting ninety minutes and is currently in stable condition. Meanwhile, leaders including Harsimrat Kaur Badal expressed gratitude for Prime Minister Narendra Modi's support, as political discussions regarding the attack and related events continue across news platforms.
