Two workers killed in quake-hit Japan mall blast

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 02:10 PM · Source checked

Two female employees lost their lives in an explosion after re-entering a quake-affected shopping mall in Japan. The victims had reportedly gone back inside the building to secure cash following instructions from their employer. Following the tragic incident, their employer issued an apology for sending them back into the dangerous structure. Details regarding the exact sequence of events and safety protocols remain limited based on the initial reports.
