Stolen Matisse artworks recovered by police in Brazil

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 02:04 PM · Source checked

Brazilian police have recovered eight stolen artworks by the renowned French artist Henri Matisse following an investigation. Authorities arrested a man after locating the valuable pieces inside a residential home situated in the greater São Paulo area. Details regarding the exact circumstances of the recovery and the condition of the artworks are currently limited. The police action marks a significant development in tracking down the missing cultural items.
