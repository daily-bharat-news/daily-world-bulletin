India Auto Retail Reaches Record 25.91 Lakh Units in July 2026

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 02:10 AM · Source checked

According to data from Autopunditz, India's auto retail sector achieved a record milestone with 25.91 lakh units sold in July 2026. This growth was significantly driven by passenger vehicle sales, which crossed the four lakh mark for the very first time. The data highlights robust consumer demand across the country during this period, marking a notable achievement for the domestic automotive industry as overall retail figures reached unprecedented levels.
