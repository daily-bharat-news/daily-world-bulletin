Psyche Spacecraft Completes Mars Gravity Assist

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 08:55 PM · Source checked

Details are limited regarding the recent event, but NASA has released a video showing the Psyche spacecraft executing a flyby of Mars. During this trajectory, the probe successfully received a gravity assist from the planet. Visual documentation provided by the space agency captures the vehicle's passage, marking an important milestone in its ongoing journey through the solar system. Further information about the maneuver and the spacecraft's subsequent path has not been elaborated in the current report.
