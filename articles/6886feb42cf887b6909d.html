Delhi weather: IMD predicts cloudy skies, light rain

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 11:33 AM · Source checked

Ahead of Independence Day, the national capital is expected to experience generally cloudy skies accompanied by light rain, according to the latest weather forecast issued by the India Meteorological Department. These climatic conditions are likely to offer pleasant weather conditions for citizens participating in the national celebrations. Authorities and commuters have been advised to stay updated on localized forecasts as minor showers may influence daily movement across the city during this festive period.
