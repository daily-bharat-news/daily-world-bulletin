Maharashtra Monsoon: IMD Predicts Heavy Rain in Mumbai, Pune and Other Districts

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 07:47 AM · Source checked

The India Meteorological Department has issued a forecast indicating that heavy rainfall is very likely to lash several districts across Maharashtra. Areas including Mumbai, Thane, Nagpur, Nashik, Satara, Sangli, and Pune are expected to experience significant downpours as the monsoon remains active in the region. Residents and local authorities in these affected areas have been advised to stay updated on weather developments and take necessary precautions regarding the heavy precipitation conditions.
