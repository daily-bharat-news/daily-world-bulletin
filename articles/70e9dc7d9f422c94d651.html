Spain and France Prepare for New Heatwaves

Vinay · 28 Jul 2026, 10:21 AM · Source checked

France and Spain are facing severe heatwave conditions with temperatures projected to exceed 40C in several areas. The rising heat is expected to create additional challenges for emergency personnel as they battle an advancing wildfire near Bordeaux. Authorities remain on high alert while firefighting efforts continue to be hampered by the extreme weather.
