PM Modi and leaders observe Partition Horrors Remembrance Day

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 08:19 AM · Source checked

On Partition Horrors Remembrance Day, Prime Minister Narendra Modi stated that the historical event tore apart several lives, recalling the immense pain and suffering endured by the people. Political leaders across the nation paid tributes to those affected, remembering the courage and resilience shown during the crisis. Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma noted that the scars of the partition will never be forgotten, while other leaders emphasized honouring the lives lost and impacted by the tragedy, ensuring that the historical lessons remain alive in the national memory.
