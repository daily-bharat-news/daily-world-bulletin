Why more women are investing and achieving higher returns than men

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 05:02 AM · Source checked

Data shows that women can achieve higher financial returns than men, yet only about a quarter of women in the UK currently have investments. This compares with approximately forty percent of men who invest. Experts highlight that starting early can help build substantial sums, encouraging more women to participate in financial markets and bridge the investment gap. However, overall engagement remains lower among female demographics, pointing to a need for greater awareness and participation in wealth building opportunities across the region.
