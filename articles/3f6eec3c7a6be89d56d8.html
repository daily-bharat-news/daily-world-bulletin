Ebola death toll reaches 1,700 in eastern Congo amid fast-growing outbreak

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 08:15 AM · Source checked

The death toll from the Ebola virus has reached 1,700 in the eastern region of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. This marks the rapid progression of one of the fastest-growing epidemics recorded in the area. In response to the escalating crisis, the WHO chief has urgently called for a stronger and more effective response to contain the disease. Authorities continue to monitor the situation as the virus spreads at an alarming rate compared to previous outbreaks.
