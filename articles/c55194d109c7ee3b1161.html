NASA MAVEN Mission Studies Martian Auroras and Atmosphere

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 08:50 PM · Source checked

Scientists utilizing NASA’s MAVEN mission have discovered that specific types of auroras on Mars develop through processes comparable to those seen on Earth. A recent illustration highlights charged particles originating from a solar storm as they strip away elements of the Martian atmosphere. This phenomenon represents one of several key mechanisms regarding atmospheric loss that are currently being examined by researchers studying the Red Planet. Further details concerning the exact mechanisms and findings remain limited in the current report.
