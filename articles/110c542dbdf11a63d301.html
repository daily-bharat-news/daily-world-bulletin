Oman reports positive Hormuz talks as Iran issues warning

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 12:48 AM · Source checked

Oman has stated that recent discussions regarding the Strait of Hormuz are positive, with both parties indicating progress toward establishing a new shipping route. However, a definitive breakthrough is not yet clear. Meanwhile, Iran has issued a warning suggesting that a potential agreement would not result in the reopening of the strait. Details regarding the ongoing diplomatic efforts remain limited as officials continue to assess the situation.
