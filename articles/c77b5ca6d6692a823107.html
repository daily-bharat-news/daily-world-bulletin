IMD Issues Heavy Rain Alert for Delhi, Punjab, Chhattisgarh and Karnataka

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 06:14 AM · Source checked

The India Meteorological Department has issued heavy rainfall alerts for several regions across the country, including Delhi, Punjab, Chhattisgarh, and Karnataka, for August 9. Residents and local authorities in these areas are advised to check official weather forecasts and follow safety advisories issued by disaster management authorities to minimize disruptions caused by severe weather conditions. Transport services and daily commuting may face minor delays due to waterlogging and downpours in vulnerable pockets.
