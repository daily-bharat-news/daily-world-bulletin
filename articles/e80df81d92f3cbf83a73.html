Police raid Starbucks Korea head office following Tank Day furore

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 12:34 PM · Source checked

South Korean police have searched the headquarters of Starbucks Korea after individuals lodged formal complaints against the company. The investigation follows allegations that the coffee chain trivialised the deadly Gwangju Uprising through a promotional event referred to as 'Tank Day'. Authorities are examining the circumstances surrounding the marketing controversy, which sparked public backlash. Further details regarding the ongoing police inquiries remain limited at this stage.
