Bombay HC orders removal of deepfakes and fake posts against Nitin Gadkari

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 12:10 PM · Source checked

The Bombay High Court has directed social media platforms and relevant entities to remove defamatory, obscene, and deepfake posts targeting Union Minister Nitin Gadkari. The controversial content and AI-generated images were linked to discussions surrounding the E20 fuel programme and ethanol policy. Observing that the online material was abusive and vile, the court mandated the immediate takedown of these unauthorized posts. Further details regarding the specific platforms and respondents involved in the case remain limited based on the current reports.
