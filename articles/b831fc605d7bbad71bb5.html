Listen to youth to prevent protest violence, says Supreme Court

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 12:25 PM · Source checked

The Supreme Court has observed that listening to the youth is the most powerful force for preventing protest violence, advising authorities to engage with Gen Z rather than repressing them. Amid recent NEET-UG protests, the apex court emphasized that counselling and pacification, combined with restraint from law enforcement, serve as a much better response to student demonstrators than aggression. These observations emerged alongside related legal petitions regarding recent demonstration management.
