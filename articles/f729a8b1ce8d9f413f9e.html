Bhiwandi Building Collapse: Ten Dead As Dangerous Structure Falls

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 12:49 AM · Source checked

Ten people have died following a building collapse in Bhiwandi. The civic body stated that the structure was previously declared dangerous, with three notices issued, water and power supplies cut, and residents told to vacate, though residents have denied these claims. Prime Minister Narendra Modi expressed grief over the fatalities and announced ex gratia financial relief for the affected families. Authorities are currently investigating illegal repairs carried out on the hazardous premises. Further details regarding rescue operations and accountability remain limited at this stage.
