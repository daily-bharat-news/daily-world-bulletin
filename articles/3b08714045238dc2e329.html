Himachal Bus Accident: 8 Killed And 10 Injured In Chamba District

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 10:38 AM · Source checked

At least eight people lost their lives and ten others sustained injuries when a private bus overturned on a mountain route along the Teesa-Bairagarh road in the Chamba district of Himachal Pradesh. Prime Minister Narendra Modi expressed deep sorrow over the tragic mishap and condoled the loss of lives. Emergency responses and rescue operations were initiated following the accident on the hilly terrain. Further details regarding the exact cause of the crash and the condition of the injured passengers are currently limited based on the available reports.
