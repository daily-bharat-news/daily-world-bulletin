Counting underway for Datia Assembly bypoll in Madhya Pradesh

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 09:15 AM · Source checked

Vote counting is currently underway for the Datia Assembly bypoll in Madhya Pradesh amidst strict security arrangements. Reports indicate that the Congress nominee, Ghanshyam Singh, took a lead over the BJP rival after a slow start. The electoral outcome is considered significant as it may impact broader state politics. Prior to the counting day, political developments included the suspension of a former Datia MLA by the Congress party, while discussions continued regarding the strategies and candidate changes of the political parties involved.
