Teenage hitmen recruited for violent crimes across Europe

Vinay · 02 Aug 2026, 04:33 AM · Source checked

Reports indicate that the Foxtrot Network is allegedly linked to approximately 35 murders throughout Europe, employing methods centered on violence-as-a-service. Criminal groups are reportedly recruiting teenagers to carry out contract killings and other violent acts. Authorities are monitoring these expanding networks and their tactics of engaging young individuals for criminal operations. Further details regarding specific arrests, investigations, and the full scope of these operations remain limited at this time.
