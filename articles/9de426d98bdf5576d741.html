Ukraine strikes two oil refineries deep within Russian territory

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 09:11 PM · Source checked

Ukrainian forces targeted two oil refineries situated deep inside Russian territory, according to the country's president. The military action is intended to restrict financial resources that Moscow utilizes to support its ongoing war efforts. Official statements indicate these targeted strikes form part of a broader strategy to diminish the revenue streams funding the military campaign. Further details regarding the extent of the damage or operational impacts remain limited at this stage.
