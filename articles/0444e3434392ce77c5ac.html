Romania shuts sole nuclear plant due to Danube River heat

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 01:16 AM · Source checked

Romania has closed its only nuclear facility, the Cernavodă plant, because a significant drop in the water level of the Danube River was caused by intense heat. The plant is responsible for generating twenty percent of the nation's electricity supply. Authorities do not anticipate restarting operations at the facility within the next ten days. Further details regarding the long-term impact on power generation remain limited at this time.
