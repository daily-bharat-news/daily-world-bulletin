Brits warned to check travel plug adaptors before summer holidays

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 04:30 AM · Source checked

Campaign group Electrical Safety First has strongly advised holidaymakers travelling abroad this summer to carefully check their travel plug adaptors prior to purchase. Authorities have highlighted potential safety hazards associated with substandard devices, urging consumers to remain vigilant and prioritize electrical safety while preparing for their trips. Specific details regarding banned models or exact technical faults are currently limited, but travelers are encouraged to source equipment only from reputable retailers to avoid risks while away.
