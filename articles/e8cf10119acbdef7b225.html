NASA Tests Webbings for Oxygen-Rich Lunar and Martian Missions

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 12:35 AM · Source checked

NASA has published flammability test results for commercial-off-the-shelf webbings intended for future missions to the Moon and Mars, which involve elevated oxygen environments. Evaluations focused on materials meeting stringent space agency safety standards. Specifically, testing showed that a natural webbing made from sixty percent Kevlar and forty percent polybenzimidazole, manufactured by Sturges Manufacturing Company, successfully cleared the required flammability assessments.
