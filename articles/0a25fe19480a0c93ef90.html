Kerala teachers protest against exam body; Amarnath Yatra updates

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 01:32 PM · Source checked

On 8 August 2026, teachers in Kerala staged a unique protest by eating grass to voice their grievances against an exam-conducting body. Meanwhile, developments related to the annual Amarnath Yatra emerged from Jammu and Kashmir. The pilgrimage experienced temporary suspensions due to inclement weather conditions and security reasons, while thousands of devotees continued to offer prayers at the holy cave, taking the total number of pilgrims past 4.7 lakh. Authorities managed the movement of pilgrims from Jammu as the yatra resumed.
