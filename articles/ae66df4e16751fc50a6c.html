Charlie Chauhan Marries Cricketer Ramandeep Singh In Private Ceremony

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 02:15 PM · Source checked

Actor Charlie Chauhan has tied the knot with Kolkata Knight Riders cricketer Ramandeep Singh in a private wedding ceremony. Several notable cricketers, including Shubman Gill, Abhishek Sharma, Arshdeep Singh, Naman Dhir, and Jayant Yadav, extended their warm wishes to the newlyweds. The private nuptials drew congratulatory messages from across the entertainment and sports fraternities, highlighting the union of the Kaisi Yeh Yaariyan star and the professional cricket player.
