Canada Wildfire Spreads Over 53 Square Miles, Residents Warned

Vinay · 10 Aug 2026, 05:47 AM · Source checked

Residents in British Columbia have been advised to prepare for severe conditions as the Bald Range wildfire continues to burn out of control. According to official reports, the aggressive blaze has already expanded across more than 53 square miles, equivalent to 136 square kilometres. Authorities are closely monitoring the situation as firefighting operations are underway to contain the rapidly spreading flames. Further details regarding evacuations and containment efforts remain limited at this stage.
