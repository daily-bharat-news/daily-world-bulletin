Ebola Outbreak Outpaces Response in DR Congo Despite Trials

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 01:09 AM · Source checked

Recent reports indicate that the ongoing Ebola outbreak in DR Congo is outpacing response efforts despite the rapid rollout of clinical trials. Meanwhile, Moderna has initiated its first human trials for the Bundibugyo Ebola vaccine, following Canadian approval for clinical evaluations. The World Health Organization notes that treatment trials focusing on the specific strain behind the Congo outbreak are showing promise. Financial analysts point out that these Phase 1 trials primarily signal platform expansion rather than immediate near-term revenue for the company.
