Hundreds of migrants swim from Morocco to Spanish enclave of Ceuta

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 02:54 AM · Source checked

Hundreds of migrants swam from Morocco to the Spanish enclave of Ceuta, creating a major challenge for the local territory. The leader of the enclave stated that this sudden influx is severely overwhelming local resources and urged the central Spanish government to step in and provide immediate assistance. Authorities are currently managing the situation as the arrival strains regional infrastructure.
