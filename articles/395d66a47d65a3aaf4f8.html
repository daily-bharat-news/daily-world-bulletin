Rs 6,000 Crore Drug Case Kingpin Extradited From UAE

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 10:03 AM · Source checked

Fugitive drug kingpin Virender Singh Basoya has been brought to India from Dubai in connection with a major multi-crore drug case. Home Minister Amit Shah highlighted the government's strict approach toward dismantling narcotics networks following the extradition. Basoya, described as a key cartel boss, was wanted in connection with a massive drug bust involving cocaine. Law enforcement agencies executed the transfer from the United Arab Emirates, marking a significant development in the high-profile investigation.
