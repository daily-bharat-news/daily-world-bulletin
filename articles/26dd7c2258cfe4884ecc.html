Historic Judo Golds for Asmita Dey and Harsh Singh at Commonwealth Games 2026

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 01:04 AM · Source checked

At the Commonwealth Games 2026, Indian judokas Asmita Dey and Harsh Singh secured historic gold medals, marking a significant milestone for the country in the discipline. Asmita Dey triumphed in her weight category to claim India's first-ever Commonwealth Games judo gold, overcoming a personal tragedy following her father's passing seven months ago. Meanwhile, Harsh Singh clinched the men's -60kg gold by defeating an experienced Australian opponent. Adding to India's success on a memorable day in Glasgow, Yamini Mourya secured a silver medal, capping off a remarkable campaign for the national judo contingent.
