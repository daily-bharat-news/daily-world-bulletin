Parliamentary Panel Threatens Meta With Safe Harbour Withdrawal

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 12:52 PM · Source checked

A parliamentary committee has directed Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg to issue an apology within three months regarding the removal of the Prime Minister's speech from Facebook and wrongful account actions. The panel has threatened to strip the tech giant of its legal safe harbour immunity if it fails to comply. Additionally, the government intends to question Meta executives regarding child sexual abuse material lapses and artificial intelligence-generated content when a global team visits India in August.
