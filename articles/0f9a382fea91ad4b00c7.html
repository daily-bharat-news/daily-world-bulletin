US beef prices soar amid supply shortages, squeezing farm profits

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 04:53 AM · Source checked

Beef prices in the United States have reached record highs driven by significant supply shortages. Despite consumers paying more for the meat than ever before, farmers and producers are facing a severe squeeze on their profit margins. Industry challenges have created a gap between retail costs and the actual earnings of agricultural workers, as rising expenses impact the livestock sector.
