US military helicopter fires missiles at ship in Gulf of Oman

Vinay · 12 Aug 2026, 02:44 AM · Source checked

United States Central Command reported that a military helicopter fired missiles to disable a cargo vessel in the Gulf of Oman. The Panama-flagged ship was struck in the engine room. According to the authorities, the action was taken because the vessel was allegedly attempting to break the blockade imposed on Iran. Further details regarding the incident and the condition of the crew remain limited at this time.
