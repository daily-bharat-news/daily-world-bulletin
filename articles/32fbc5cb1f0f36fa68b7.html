Huma Qureshi defends co-stars Kiara Advani and Yash amid Tabaahi row

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 05:57 AM · Source checked

Actor Huma Qureshi has strongly defended her 'Toxic' co-stars Kiara Advani and Yash following online trolling regarding the 'Tabaahi' row. Describing the social media backlash as sick and disgusting, she spoke out against the targeted criticism. Meanwhile, discussions around the controversy continued as observers noted a birthday wish for Kiara Advani from her husband Sidharth Malhotra, with reports questioning if it addressed the ongoing online trolling incident. Details regarding the full context of the dispute remain limited based on the provided reports.
