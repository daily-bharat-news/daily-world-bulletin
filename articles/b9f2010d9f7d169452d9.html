Paramount chief executive defends Warner Bros merger

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 01:18 AM · Source checked

Paramount-Skydance chief executive David Ellison has defended the company's massive merger with Warner Bros, addressing criticism surrounding the deal. Ellison stated that skeptics are relying on an outdated Hollywood model rather than recognizing the changing media landscape. The proposed consolidation has drawn significant attention due to its scale and potential impact on the entertainment industry. Further details regarding the transaction and its regulatory approval process remain limited at this stage.
