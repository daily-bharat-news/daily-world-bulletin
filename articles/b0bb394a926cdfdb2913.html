Netherlands appoint Xavi Hernandez as head coach until 2030

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 12:48 AM · Source checked

The Netherlands national team has appointed former Spain great Xavi Hernandez as their new head coach on a four-year contract that runs through the 2030 World Cup. With this appointment, Xavi becomes the first foreign-born manager to lead the Dutch side since 1978. The announcement brings a surprise new destination for the former Barcelona coach, marking a significant milestone in his managerial career as he takes charge of the national team.
