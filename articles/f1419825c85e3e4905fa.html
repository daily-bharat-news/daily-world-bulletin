Four New Car Brands Including Genesis And JSW Motors Eye Indian Market

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 03:20 AM · Source checked

Reports indicate that four new automobile brands are preparing to enter the Indian market. According to recent updates, the brands eyeing expansion into India include JSW Motors, Genesis, Leapmotor, and Slate Auto. Industry observers are closely tracking these developments as global and domestic players seek to capture a larger share of the growing domestic automotive sector. Further announcements regarding their official entry timelines, vehicle lineups, and manufacturing strategies are expected in due course.
