Afghan boxer in court over death of Scottish woman

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 01:58 PM · Source checked

Sharif Ahmadzai, a twenty-six-year-old Afghan boxer, has appeared in court in connection with the death of Elisabeth Jane Ross from Edinburgh. Authorities are currently holding the accused as legal proceedings regarding the incident move forward. Further details about the circumstances surrounding the case are limited at this stage as investigations continue.
