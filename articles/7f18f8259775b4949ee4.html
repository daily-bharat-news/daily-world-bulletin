BTS withdraws from Grammys following new Asian pop award category introduction

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 09:13 AM · Source checked

Entertainment sources report that South Asian group BTS has withdrawn from the Grammys. This decision comes approximately a month after the awards introduced a new Asian pop prize category. Various international media outlets, including the BBC, Reuters, The Guardian, and The Hindu, covered the development, highlighting the group's choice to boycott the event. Observers note that the legendary K-pop act's musical impact and enduring legacy do not require formal award validation. Specific official statements from the group regarding the exact reasons behind this pullout remain limited in the provided reports.
