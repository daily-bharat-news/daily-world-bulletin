Jemimah Rodrigues Shares Warm Hug With Pakistan's Fatima Sana

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 08:59 AM · Source checked

Indian cricketer Jemimah Rodrigues was seen sharing a warm hug with Pakistan player Fatima Sana following a match in The Hundred. A video capturing this moment has gone viral on social media, drawing widespread attention. The interaction between the two athletes took place amidst ongoing political tensions and protocols between India and Pakistan. Media reports highlighted the gesture, noting the exchange of warmth between the players despite the frosty diplomatic relations between their respective nations.
