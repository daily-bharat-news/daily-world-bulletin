Six dead in unprecedented Japan rainstorm

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 12:30 PM · Source checked

Severe downpours described as unprecedented have resulted in six deaths across Japan. The heavy storm disrupted daily life by cutting power supplies to over twenty thousand households. Additionally, transport operations were significantly affected, leaving approximately seven thousand travellers stranded at Tokyo's Narita airport. Authorities are responding to the emergency caused by the extreme weather conditions. Further details regarding the total extent of the damage remain limited as recovery efforts continue.
