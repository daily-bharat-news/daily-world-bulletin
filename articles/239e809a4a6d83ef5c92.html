Kulgam terror attack death toll rises to two as injured worker succumbs

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 08:40 AM · Source checked

A second non-local worker from Chhattisgarh injured in a militant attack in Kulgam has succumbed to his injuries, taking the total death toll from the incident to two. The attack marks the first instance of violence targeting migrant workers in Jammu and Kashmir in over twenty-one months. Experts suggest the terror incident in Kashmir is an attempt to divert attention from Pakistan-administered Kashmir. Details regarding further security measures and official investigations are limited at present.
