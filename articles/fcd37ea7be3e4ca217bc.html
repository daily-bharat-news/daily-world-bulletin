IMD Issues Rain Alert For Delhi, UP, Bihar And Other States

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 02:43 AM · Source checked

The India Meteorological Department has issued weather warnings and rainfall alerts for multiple regions across the country, including Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, and Bihar. Citizens and local authorities in these areas have been advised to stay updated with the latest forecasts as wet weather conditions prevail. Official updates from weather sources provide further details on the progression of these showers across various affected states, ensuring the public remains informed about potential disruptions.
