Trump denies US weapons shortage and warns leakers

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 10:56 PM · Source checked

Donald Trump has dismissed reports of a United States weapons shortage, asserting that the country has sufficient military supplies. He added that individuals leaking classified information are currently being hunted down. Although exact data remains classified, public details indicate that the US has utilized large quantities of complex and difficult-to-manufacture armaments.
