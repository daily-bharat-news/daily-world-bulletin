JSW Group Eyes Majority Stake in Skoda Auto VW India JV

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 12:06 PM · Source checked

JSW Group is reportedly looking to acquire a majority stake in the proposed joint venture between Skoda Auto and Volkswagen India. According to industry reports from Autopunditz, discussions are underway regarding the potential partnership. If finalized, this strategic move could significantly alter the ownership structure and operational dynamics of the German automotive group's Indian subsidiary. Further official details regarding the stake purchase, financial valuations, and final agreements remain limited as talks progress between the involved corporate entities.
