Tarun Tejpal sentenced to 10 years in jail for 2013 rape case

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 03:19 PM · Source checked

Indian journalist and Tehelka founder Tarun Tejpal has been sentenced to 10 years of rigorous imprisonment in connection with a 2013 rape case. The Bombay High Court convicted Tejpal and set aside his previous acquittal by a trial court, noting that the trial court allowed the victim to be humiliated during proceedings. The High Court has granted the journalist a period of two weeks to surrender to the authorities. Further details regarding the court proceedings remain limited based on the available news reports.
