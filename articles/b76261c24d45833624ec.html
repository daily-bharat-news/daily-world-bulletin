Tokenomics: Why making AI pay is tricky

Vinay · 04 Aug 2026, 04:51 AM · Source checked

Makers and users of artificial intelligence services face a complex challenge regarding pricing and expenses. Purchasers find it difficult to manage their spending on these advanced tools, while vendors struggle to determine the right price points. This economic dilemma highlights the uncertainties involved in monetising artificial intelligence technologies effectively in the current market, as both sides try to balance utility against financial sustainability without clear industry standards.
