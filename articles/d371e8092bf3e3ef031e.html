UK to witness best solar eclipse since 1999 as Moon blocks Sun

Vinay · 12 Aug 2026, 04:47 AM · Source checked

The United Kingdom is set to experience its most significant solar eclipse since 1999, with up to ninety-six percent of the Sun blocked by the Moon. While the UK will see a partial covering, regions in Spain, Greenland, Iceland, Portugal, and Russia are expected to witness a total eclipse. Astronomers and skywatchers across Europe are preparing for the celestial event, which offers a rare viewing opportunity for millions of people in the affected paths.
