Nigerian security forces rescue over 300 abductees

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 10:05 AM · Source checked

Nigerian joint security forces have rescued more than 300 abducted individuals in what the government has described as the country's largest single-day rescue operation. Authorities confirmed the successful mission, though further specifics regarding the identities of those freed and the exact location of the operation remain limited in the current reports. The coordinated effort highlights ongoing security challenges in the region and marks a significant milestone for the country's joint security forces in addressing mass abductions.
