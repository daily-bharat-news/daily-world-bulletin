Thai PM vows stricter gun laws after school shooting

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 02:49 AM · Source checked

Following a tragic shooting incident where eight people lost their lives, the Prime Minister of Thailand has promised to implement stricter gun regulations. The tragedy unfolded when a 14-year-old opened fire at a residence and a school before taking his own life. Authorities are dealing with the aftermath of the event, prompting renewed discussions regarding firearm control and public safety measures across the country.
