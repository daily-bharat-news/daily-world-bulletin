Young Adults in Kashmir Face Growing Sleep Deprivation Risks

Vinay · 15 Aug 2026, 01:28 AM · Source checked

Recent reports indicate that young adults are increasingly searching for information on sleep disorders, as sleep deprivation threatens health and well-being in the region. Medical professionals highlight that many individuals struggle to stay focused and feel fresh daily, often unaware of underlying exhaustion. Experts advise that addressing these modern health challenges should begin with prioritizing proper sleep habits and consulting healthcare providers when necessary.
