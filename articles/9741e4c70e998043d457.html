PM Modi expresses disappointment over low number of female IIT Delhi medal winners

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 12:39 PM · Source checked

Prime Minister Narendra Modi expressed his disappointment at the 57th Convocation Ceremony of IIT Delhi over having only one or two female students among the medal winners, stating that the number should have been significantly higher. Addressing the graduating students, he encouraged them to have the courage to find answers rather than just asking questions, while also emphasizing that in a rapidly changing world, those who continuously learn will emerge victorious.
