Mamta Kulkarni reacts to Mahira Sharma's song Ranaji 2.0

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 12:47 PM · Source checked

Former actor Mamta Kulkarni has shared her reaction to Mahira Sharma's newly released music video 'Ranaji 2.0', stating that the original track remains deeply imprinted in public memory. Meanwhile, the new release has drawn heavy trolling from social media users over a perceived lack of facial expressions, leading to online discussions regarding product promotion versus artistic value. Netizens have openly expressed a preference for the classic original version over the recent remake. Overall, details are limited regarding further official statements from the artists involved.
