FIFA decries concerted effort to undermine President Gianni Infantino

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 04:02 PM · Source checked

FIFA has strongly criticised what it calls a concerted campaign aimed at undermining President Gianni Infantino as the organisation faces a deepening crisis. Recent media reports have raised various allegations involving UEFA payouts and former employees, leading to strong denials and responses from international football authorities. While investigations and media scrutiny continue to unfold regarding the leadership, officials maintain their stance against the mounting external pressure.
