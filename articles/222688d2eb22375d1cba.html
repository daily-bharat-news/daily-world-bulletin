Manoj Muntashir terms Adipurush a failed attempt

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 08:12 AM · Source checked

Adipurush writer Manoj Muntashir has stated that his film should not be compared with the upcoming Ramayana project, calling his own work a failed attempt. He mentioned there is no shame in admitting that the movie could not meet audience expectations. Furthermore, he praised actor Yash for his portrayal of Ravana and urged fans to avoid drawing comparisons between the two cinematic works.
