Prashant Kishor widens lead over BJP candidate in Bankipur bypoll

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 08:28 AM · Source checked

In the Bankipur bypoll results, Prashant Kishor has extended his lead over the BJP candidate, marking a notable electoral trend for Jan Suraaj. Reports indicate that namesakes of prominent leaders Nitish Kumar and Lalu Prasad failed to impact the contest in Bihar. As vote counting progressed, Kishor remarked that the outcome conveys a message to the BJP. Detailed updates remain limited as the election results continue to unfold across the constituency according to various media reports.
