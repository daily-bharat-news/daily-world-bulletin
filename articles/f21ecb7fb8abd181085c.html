Amruta Khanvilkar and Himanshu Malhotra living separately

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 11:32 AM · Source checked

Actors Amruta Khanvilkar and Himanshu Malhotra have reportedly been staying separately for the past one and a half years after eleven years of marriage. Sources indicate that despite living apart, the couple remains on cordial terms and has not initiated any divorce discussions. Amruta Khanvilkar clarified their current status, stating that they are still legally married. Further details regarding their personal situation remain limited at this moment.
