EasyJet agrees to £5.7bn takeover by US firm Apollo

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 10:05 PM · Source checked

Budget airline EasyJet has agreed to a £5.7bn acquisition by United States-based firm Apollo. This development comes after a competing bidder withdrew its interest from the process. The no-frills carrier accepted the buyout proposal following the departure of the rival suitor. Further particulars regarding the transaction timeline and regulatory approvals remain limited based on the current available information from official sources.
