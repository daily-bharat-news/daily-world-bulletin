Glasgow bids farewell to Commonwealth Games with handover to India

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 04:01 AM · Source checked

Glasgow has officially concluded the Commonwealth Games, passing the flag and baton to the next host nation, India. During the closing ceremony, boxer Jasmine served as one of India's flag-bearers. Ahmedabad has extended a 'Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam' invitation to host the upcoming 2030 Commonwealth Games. Further details regarding the handover ceremony and future preparations remain limited based on the provided updates.
