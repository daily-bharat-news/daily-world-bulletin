EVs, Hybrids and CNG Power Over 40% of India's PV Sales in June

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 11:10 AM · Source checked

In June, alternative fuel vehicles including electric vehicles, hybrids, and CNG models accounted for more than forty percent of total passenger vehicle sales across India. This milestone highlights a significant shift in consumer preferences toward greener and more sustainable mobility options within the domestic automotive market. Detailed figures regarding individual segment performance and specific manufacturer contributions remain limited in the current report, but the overall trend marks a notable transition in the country's transport sector.
