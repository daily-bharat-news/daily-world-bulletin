Sonam Wangchuk Praises Vande Bharat and Indian Railways

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 01:23 PM · Source checked

During a journey to Ladakh, environmentalist Sonam Wangchuk commended Indian Railways and the Vande Bharat service, describing it as a wonderful experience. Alongside his appreciation for the rail infrastructure, he highlighted the importance of trust between the government and the youth. He urged the Centre to fulfill previous assurances regarding Ladakh, noting that while he takes pride in the nation's railway system, he also wishes to feel equally proud of fulfilled governmental promises.
