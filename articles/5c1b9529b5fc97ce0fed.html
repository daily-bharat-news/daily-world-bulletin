Ramayana to Clash With Godzilla Minus Zero on November 6

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 08:10 AM · Source checked

Upcoming fantasy action epic 'Ramayana' is scheduled for a major box office clash with 'Godzilla Minus Zero' on November 6. The film features Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram, sparking wide public interest regarding the star cast and hidden references. Actor Kajal Aggarwal recently highlighted the project's global appeal, describing the epic narrative as a fitting representation of the country. With new promotional trailers and continuous fan discussions, the upcoming release is set for significant competition in the global entertainment market.
