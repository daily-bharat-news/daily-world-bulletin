IMD issues flood warning as heavy rain threatens north and central India

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 08:38 AM · Source checked

Weather authorities have issued a flood warning following forecasts of heavy rainfall across northern and central regions of the country. The India Meteorological Department expects downpours to impact daily life and local infrastructure in the affected areas. Residents and local authorities have been advised to remain cautious and prepare for potential waterlogging and riverine flooding. Further updates from weather news sources will provide specific regional impacts as the situation develops, while details regarding exact rainfall measurements and timeline specifics are currently limited.
