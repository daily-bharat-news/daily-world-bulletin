Island of Ireland prepares for deep partial solar eclipse

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 10:49 AM · Source checked

Skywatchers across the island of Ireland are set to witness a deep partial solar eclipse next week. This celestial event will be followed shortly by the peak of the Perseid meteor shower. Officials and enthusiasts anticipate clear skies to observe these rare astronomical occurrences. Further details regarding exact timings and viewing safety measures are limited at present.
