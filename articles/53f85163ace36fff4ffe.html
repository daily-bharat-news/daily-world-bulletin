Explosive-Laden Drone Found at German Airport

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 01:31 AM · Source checked

Police in Germany have discovered a drone carrying explosives near Ukrainian cargo planes at an airport. This security incident occurred shortly before a separate incident where another object collided with a cargo aircraft in mid-air. Authorities are currently investigating these developments concerning the cargo planes. Further details regarding the origin of the drone and the mid-air collision remain limited as police continue their inquiries into the security breach at the facility.
