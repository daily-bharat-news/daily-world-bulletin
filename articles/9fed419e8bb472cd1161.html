West England Growers Adapt to Drought by Seeking New Crops

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 10:49 AM · Source checked

In the West of England, winemakers are thriving amidst changing weather patterns, whereas vegetable farms struggle with severe drought conditions. To cope with the evolving climate, local growers are actively searching for alternative crops that can better withstand dry conditions. This shift highlights the diverse impacts of climate change on regional agriculture, as different agricultural sectors experience contrasting fortunes in adapting to environmental pressures.
