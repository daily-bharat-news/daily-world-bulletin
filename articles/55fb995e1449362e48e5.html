FIFA President Infantino Offered Morocco 2030 World Cup Final For Support, Says Report

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 12:24 AM · Source checked

According to media reports, FIFA President Gianni Infantino promised to award the 2030 World Cup final to Morocco in exchange for their backing. Various publications noted that the offer was made as part of efforts to secure support and remain in his position. The claims have emerged from multiple news outlets, highlighting discussions surrounding the upcoming tournament hosting decisions and leadership dynamics within international football governance. Further details regarding the official responses from FIFA or Moroccan sports authorities remain limited at this stage.
