Rare solar eclipse wows millions across UK and Europe

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 03:23 AM · Source checked

Millions of people across the UK and Europe witnessed a rare solar eclipse, described as a once-in-a-generation event. In Cornwall, the Moon covered more than 95 percent of the Sun, while certain regions in Spain experienced a total eclipse. Observers across affected areas watched the celestial phenomenon as day momentarily turned to dusk. Such astronomical alignments draw widespread public interest, offering scientists and enthusiasts alike a chance to observe the rare alignment firsthand.
