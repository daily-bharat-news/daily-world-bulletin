Man dies in old-age home, daughters attend funeral via video call

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 08:34 AM · Source checked

A Mumbai businessman residing in an old-age home in Sonipat, Haryana, passed away recently. Due to their inability to be present in person, his three daughters participated in his final rites remotely through a video call. Additionally, a payment of Rs 5,100 was transferred for the arrangements. Details regarding the exact circumstances of his stay at the facility remain limited from the available reports.
