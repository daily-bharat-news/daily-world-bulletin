India concludes Commonwealth Games 2026 campaign in fourth place

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 03:12 AM · Source checked

India finished fourth on the medals table as the Commonwealth Games 2026 campaign concluded in Glasgow. Australia topped the standings once again. The Indian contingent delivered several notable performances, highlighted by historic highs that outweighed a modest overall medal tally. Notable displays included Mirabai Chanu securing a hat-trick of gold medals and Tejaswin Shankar claiming a decathlon medal. Sport-specific reports graded boxing as A-plus, athletics as B-plus, and bowls as C-plus. Attention now shifts towards Ahmedabad following the conclusion of the multi-sport event.
