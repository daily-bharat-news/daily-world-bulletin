Rawalakot Gripped by Deadly Protests in Pakistan-Administered Kashmir

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 09:02 AM · Source checked

BBC has gained unique access to Rawalakot in Pakistan-administered Kashmir, which is currently gripped by deadly protests and violent clashes between security forces and demonstrators. A local resident shared the tragic story of losing her only son, who was shot while standing up for justice during the unrest. The ongoing situation highlights severe tensions in the region, with further details regarding the extent of the violence and casualties remaining limited based on the current reports.
