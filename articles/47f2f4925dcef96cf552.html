TN Budget 2026: One-sovereign gold coin for brides announced

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 01:40 PM · Source checked

Tamil Nadu Finance Minister Marie Wilson presented the maiden Budget of the TVK-led government, announcing a one-sovereign gold coin for brides alongside laptops. The state unveiled a Rs 4,414 crore industrial push, focusing on an artificial intelligence city and a space investment zone. The administration also stated an ambitious target to achieve a $1.5 trillion economy by the year 2031, while asserting strict financial accountability measures.
