Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 02:41 AM · Source checked

In the seventeenth match of The Hundred 2026, Trent Rockets successfully defended their position to claim a tight victory against the Sunrisers. Despite a fierce batting assault by Sutherland, strong performances by Amir, Dunkley, and Sciver-Brunt helped the Rockets maintain their momentum at the top of the standings.
