Israel accused of weaponising archaeology at West Bank sites

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 05:13 AM · Source checked

Israel is reportedly planning large-scale land expropriations across historic Palestinian areas in the West Bank. Critics have accused the authorities of using archaeology as a tool for these measures. Details regarding the specific locations, timelines, and implementation of these planned expropriations remain limited based on the current reports from the region.
