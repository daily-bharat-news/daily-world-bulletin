Lower protein intake may improve health and lifespan: Study

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 04:28 PM · Source checked

A recent study indicates that reducing protein consumption could potentially slow down the aging process and enhance overall health outcomes. Experts suggest that a lower intake might offer longevity benefits specifically for individuals with sedentary lifestyles. Meanwhile, medical professionals continue to debate the safety of high-protein diets and their potential connection to heart-related complications. Further research is necessary to fully understand these dietary impacts.
