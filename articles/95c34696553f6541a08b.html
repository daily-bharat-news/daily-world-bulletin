Severe storms overturn vehicles and cause flooding in Ohio

Vinay · 12 Aug 2026, 02:26 AM · Source checked

Severe storms have struck Ohio, causing widespread flooding and overturning a vehicle. Officials have confirmed at least one fatality due to the extreme weather conditions. Local residents are now being strongly urged by authorities to prepare themselves for additional severe weather expected to hit the region in the coming days. Further details regarding the extent of the damage and ongoing rescue operations are currently limited.
