Salman Khan shares weight-loss transformation details for his upcoming film

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 11:39 AM · Source checked

Actor Salman Khan has revealed details about his weight-loss transformation for an upcoming film featuring Nayanthara. Additionally, the actor shared past experiences regarding his time in jail, mentioning conditions where numerous individuals shared a single bathroom. He also made a playful remark about actor Riteish Deshmukh and experienced an emotional reunion with Sohail Khan, combining lighthearted humor with personal recollections during recent media interactions.
