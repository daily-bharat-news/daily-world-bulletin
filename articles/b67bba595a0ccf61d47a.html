Malaysia Airlines flight lands safely in Kolkata after laser beam distraction

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 03:11 PM · Source checked

A Malaysia Airlines flight experienced a brief delay during its approach to Kolkata airport after a stray laser beam temporarily distracted the pilot. As a safety precaution, the aircraft circled mid-air before making a secure landing. Authorities noted the momentary disruption caused by the unauthorized light source. Further details regarding the incident and the source of the laser beam are limited at present.
