Supreme Court Sets Aside Trial Court's Order Summoning Ex-PM Manmohan Singh

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 12:38 PM · Source checked

The Supreme Court has set aside a trial court order that summoned former Prime Minister Manmohan Singh in connection with a coal block allocation case. By accepting the clean chit given by the Central Bureau of Investigation, the apex court cleared him in the matter. Following this development, political reactions emerged, with the Congress party demanding an apology from the BJP, while other voices highlighted the need for criminal justice system reforms based on the outcome of this case.
