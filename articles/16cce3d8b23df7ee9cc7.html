Canada wildfire doubles in size, emergency declared in British Columbia

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 06:11 PM · Source checked

Authorities have declared a state of emergency in British Columbia, Canada, following a rapid expansion of the Bald Range wildfire. The fast-moving blaze has doubled in size, spreading across more than 39 square miles, which is approximately 103 square kilometres. Firefighters and emergency response teams are currently battling the blaze, which remains officially classified as out of control. Local officials are closely monitoring the situation as weather conditions continue to impact containment efforts, threatening nearby areas.
