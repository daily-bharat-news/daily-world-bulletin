Living in disused spaces as property guardians for discounted rent

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 04:35 AM · Source checked

Property guardians in the UK are opting to live communally in disused buildings, such as a former care home where a resident pays £580 per month, in exchange for significantly reduced rent. This arrangement allows individuals to find more affordable housing options while occupying vacant properties. Guardians showed journalists around their shared living spaces, highlighting how alternative housing models are emerging amidst high property costs. Details regarding specific locations, broader housing market impacts, and contractual terms are limited based on the provided information.
