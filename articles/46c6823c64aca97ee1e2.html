Brock Lesnar confirms retirement from WWE after SummerSlam

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 12:51 AM · Source checked

Combat sports icon and ten-time WWE champion Brock Lesnar has confirmed his retirement from professional wrestling following a defeat against Oba Femi at SummerSlam. Following the announcement, fellow wrestler Roman Reigns paid a heartfelt tribute, praising him as one of the greatest performers in the history of the sport. Media reports and trending videos highlighted the conclusion of his illustrious career, sparking discussions across the combat sports community regarding his future plans.
