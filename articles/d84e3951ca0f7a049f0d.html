Kharge Questions Anti-Paper Leak Bill In Parliament Monsoon Session

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026 · Source checked

During the Parliament Monsoon Session 2026, Leader Mallikarjun Kharge questioned the effectiveness of the anti-paper leak legislation, calling government amendments a hogwash while supporting NEET protesters. The Bharatiya Janata Party criticized Kharge over his remarks concerning Home Minister Amit Shah. Meanwhile, parliamentary proceedings faced disruptions, and the Rajya Sabha was adjourned twice as opposition members demanded the presence of the Home Minister to address protest crackdowns, reflecting heightened political tensions in the House.
