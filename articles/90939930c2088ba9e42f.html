Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 10:21 AM · Source checked

Weather updates indicate that several regions across Maharashtra, including Mumbai, Thane, Pune, Satara, Sangli, Vidarbha, and Palghar, are expected to experience heavy rainfall today. Residents in these affected areas are advised to stay updated on local weather forecasts and take necessary precautions while commuting due to potential waterlogging and disruptions caused by the downpour.
