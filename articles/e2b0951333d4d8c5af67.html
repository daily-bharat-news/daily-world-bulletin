Kia India Aims for a 10-Model Portfolio by 2030

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 03:54 PM · Source checked

Kia India has announced plans to expand its product portfolio to ten models by the year 2030. The upcoming lineup will place significant emphasis on strong-hybrid variants of popular vehicles like the Seltos, alongside a range of new electric vehicles and compressed natural gas cars. This strategic move aims to cater to changing consumer preferences in the country's fast-growing automotive market. Details regarding specific launch timelines and pricing remain limited as the company prepares for its long-term expansion.
