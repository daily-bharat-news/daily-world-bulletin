US and Japan jointly intervene to prop up yen

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 08:13 AM · Source checked

In a rare move, the United States and Japan have jointly intervened in foreign exchange markets to prop up the falling yen. Both nations stated they will not hesitate to take similar coordinated action in the future if required. This joint effort highlights growing official concern over the currency's rapid depreciation against the US dollar.
