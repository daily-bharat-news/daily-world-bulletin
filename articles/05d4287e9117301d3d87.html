Singapore Fines French Teenager $465 for Viral Straw-Licking Stunt

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 08:24 AM · Source checked

A nineteen-year-old French national has been fined 465 dollars in Singapore following a viral social media stunt that involved licking a straw. According to his legal representatives, the teenager expressed that he was truly sorry for the trouble caused by his actions. Authorities in Singapore handled the incident, while details regarding the broader circumstances of the case remain limited based on the provided reports.
