Gulveer Singh makes history with two medals at CWG 2026

Vinay · 02 Aug 2026, 01:13 AM · Source checked

At the Commonwealth Games 2026, Naib Subedar Gulveer Singh created history by becoming the first Indian athlete to secure two medals in a single edition of the Games. He clinched a bronze medal in the 5,000m event during the final day of athletics, adding to his earlier success. Meanwhile, para shot putter Rana secured a gold medal, and the Indian boxing contingent concluded their campaign with an impressive haul of seven gold and three silver medals, marking a successful outing for the national delegation.
