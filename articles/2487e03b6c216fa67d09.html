BJP workers clash with police demanding Punjab minister's exit

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 12:07 PM · Source checked

Bharatiya Janata Party workers clashed with police forces outside the Aam Aadmi Party office in Delhi during a demonstration. The protesters demanded the resignation of the Punjab education minister following allegations regarding paper leaks in the state. Meanwhile, opposition parties in Punjab have intensified pressure on the government over the controversy. The AAP administration has dismissed these allegations. Additionally, the High Court has put the recruitment process for Punjab pharmacists on hold due to reports of a hi-tech cheating racket.
