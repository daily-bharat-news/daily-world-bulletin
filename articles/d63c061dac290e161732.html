Russian strike kills at least 17 as Zelensky urges for interceptors

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 01:59 PM · Source checked

A recent Russian military strike resulted in the deaths of at least 17 people. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky stated that the deployment of adequate interceptors could have saved these lives. Meanwhile, the Russian defence ministry reported that its forces successfully targeted logistics hubs and supply centres located in Kyiv and the surrounding region. Official details regarding the full extent of the damage and casualties remain limited as authorities continue to assess the situation on the ground following the attack.
