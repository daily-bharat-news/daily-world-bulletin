Upcoming Compact SUVs in India: Hyundai EV, New Nexon and More

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 07:56 AM · Source checked

Automotive reports indicate that several new compact sport utility vehicles are expected to launch in the Indian market soon. Prospective buyers can look forward to upcoming models including a new Hyundai electric vehicle, an updated Tata Nexon, Kia Sonet, and the Vision S, among others. These anticipated vehicle arrivals highlight the ongoing expansion and growing competition within India's popular compact SUV segment, offering consumers a wider range of choices across different powertrains and designs in the near future.
