Parliament Monsoon Session Day 13: Opposition Protests Continue

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 01:38 PM · Source checked

On day thirteen of the Parliament Monsoon session, opposition protests persisted as a deadlock continued over demands for answers from Home Minister Amit Shah. Parliament passed two bills amidst disruptions regarding Ayodhya and student crackdowns. Congress leaders Mallikarjun Kharge and Rahul Gandhi led a demonstration near the Prime Minister's residence, demanding resignations from the Prime Minister and the Home Minister. The Lok Sabha faced adjournment until two in the afternoon. With the opposition hardening its stance, reports indicate a complete washout of the session is likely.
