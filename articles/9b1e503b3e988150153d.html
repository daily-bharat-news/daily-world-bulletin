Fashion brand Jaded London faces ad ban for glamorising smoking

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 04:50 AM · Source checked

The advertising watchdog has banned a marketing email from fashion brand Jaded London after ruling that it glamorised smoking. According to the regulatory authority, the promotional material presented cigarette use in an appealing manner, which violates established advertising standards. Details regarding any further penalties or the exact content of the email are currently limited based on the provided information.
