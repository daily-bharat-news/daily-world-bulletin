Salman Khan shares emotional reunion photos with Sanjay Dutt

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 03:28 AM · Source checked

Actor Salman Khan recently posted pictures on social media capturing an emotional reunion with Sanjay Dutt. Describing Dutt as his elder brother and using the affectionate moniker Baba forever, Khan shared a heartfelt and quirky midnight wish that quickly caught the attention of fans across various platforms. The widely covered interaction highlights their long-standing friendship spanning decades within the Hindi film industry, generating significant interest among admirers of both actors.
