NASA Invites U.S. Students for 2027 Lunar Tech Challenge

Vinay · 15 Aug 2026, 01:30 AM · Source checked

NASA has invited collegiate teams based in the United States to pitch innovative concepts for the 2027 edition of its student challenge. The initiative focuses on aerospace innovation to help the space agency visualize a future on the Moon supported by advanced technologies. Participants in the Revolutionary Aerospace Systems Concepts - Academic Linkage competition are encouraged to explore novel operational paradigms and develop technologies required for future lunar exploration.
