Israel strikes south Lebanon following evacuation warning

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 01:33 AM · Source checked

The Israeli military launched strikes in southern Lebanon after issuing its first evacuation warning in weeks. According to Lebanon's health ministry, one person lost their life in the attacks. The military stated that the action was taken in response to a ceasefire violation by Hezbollah. Further details regarding the situation remain limited at this time.
