IMD issues heavy rain alert across India; Delhi expected to see more showers

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 08:53 AM · Source checked

The India Meteorological Department has issued a heavy rainfall alert for various regions across the country, while the national capital Delhi is expected to experience additional showers. Official updates provide limited specific regional details regarding the intensity and exact timing of the upcoming precipitation. Residents in affected areas are advised to stay updated with local weather forecasts and take necessary precautions as conditions develop over the coming days.
