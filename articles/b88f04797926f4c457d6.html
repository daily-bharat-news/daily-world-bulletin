Dutch bike maker Accell, owner of Raleigh, files for insolvency

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 06:04 PM · Source checked

Accell, the Netherlands-based parent company of the renowned bicycle manufacturer Raleigh, has officially filed for insolvency. The firm has held ownership of the iconic British bike brand since 2012. Further details regarding the financial restructuring and the future operations of Raleigh and its parent company are currently limited as proceedings unfold.
