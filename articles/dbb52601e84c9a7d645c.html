IAF officer arrested for leaking defence secrets to Pak handler

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 01:24 PM · Source checked

An Indian Air Force officer has been arrested for allegedly sharing classified military information with a Pakistan-based operative. According to police reports, the accused was lured through online chats by a woman in what appears to be a honey-trap operation. He has been booked under the provisions of the Official Secrets Act. Authorities are currently investigating the extent of the sensitive information compromised during the incident, as law enforcement agencies look further into the espionage network.
