Targeting Cancer Cell Sugar Coating May Boost Immune Response

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 12:31 PM · Source checked

Recent scientific findings indicate that a thick sugar shield, caused by high glucose levels, helps cancer cells hide from the immune system. By thinning this protective coating, researchers believe the body's immune defenses could better recognize and attack tumours. This discovery points to a potential new therapeutic target for cancer treatment, helping to break the invisibility cloak that malignant cells use to evade detection. Further studies are needed to understand the exact mechanisms and develop practical clinical applications.
