Ajinkya Rahane retires from international cricket

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 01:49 PM · Source checked

Ajinkya Rahane has announced his retirement from international cricket. The Board of Control for Cricket in India has paid tribute to his distinguished career representing the national team. Media outlets and cricket bodies have acknowledged his significant contributions, recalling his memorable Test innings and steadfast dedication as a key pillar of the Indian batting lineup. Further details regarding his future plans remain limited.
