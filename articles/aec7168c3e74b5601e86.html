Actress Kirti Kulhari loses Rs 2.4 lakh to cyber fraud

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 08:17 AM · Source checked

Bollywood actress Kirti Kulhari has fallen victim to cyber fraud, losing approximately Rs 2.4 lakh through unauthorised credit card transactions. Despite not sharing card details, the artist discovered the fraudulent withdrawals and subsequently filed a formal complaint with the Mumbai Police. Media reports indicate that around Rs 2.44 lakh was stolen from her account via unauthorized card payments. Further details regarding the ongoing police investigation into the digital scam are currently awaited as authorities look into the matter.
