Malaysia's Forest City raided by police over alleged scam operations

Vinay · 12 Aug 2026, 05:01 AM · Source checked

Malaysian authorities recently conducted raids at the Forest City seaside residential complex following allegations that scam operations had set up base in the largely unoccupied development. The mega-project, which was initially marketed to buyers as a luxurious tropical paradise, transformed into a virtual ghost city due to low occupancy rates. Last month, law enforcement officers intervened after receiving intelligence regarding illicit activities taking place within the premises. Official investigations into the alleged financial scams operating from the coastal apartments are currently underway.
