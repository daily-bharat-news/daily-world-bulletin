Census 2027 to feature 40 questions including caste and vaccination details

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 10:54 AM · Source checked

The central government has notified the questionnaire for the household schedule of Census 2027, featuring forty questions. The upcoming census will gather information on various subjects, including caste details, COVID-19 vaccination status, and identification proofs such as Aadhaar. Meanwhile, the Congress party has raised questions regarding the intent behind the exercise. Further specific details about the implementation and schedule remain limited based on current reports.
