Australian H5N1 Bird Flu Cases Cross 200

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 11:37 AM · Source checked

The H5N1 bird flu outbreak in Australia has exceeded two hundred cases, prompting calls for urgent vaccination of high-risk wildlife. In response to the rising detections, Victoria has introduced new poultry housing requirements. Meanwhile, suspected mass bird flu deaths have emerged in a second state, with authorities warning that this development marks the beginning of a broader concern. Experts continue to monitor the situation closely as authorities implement measures to contain the spread across the affected regions.
