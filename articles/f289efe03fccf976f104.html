Fifa criticises concerted effort to undermine president Gianni Infantino

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 03:36 AM · Source checked

Fifa has strongly criticised what it describes as a concerted and ongoing effort to undermine the organisation and its president, Gianni Infantino. The football governing body issued a statement addressing the campaign aimed at destabilising the leadership, though specific details regarding the origin or nature of the opposition remain limited in the current reports. Infantino continues to face scrutiny from various quarters, prompting this defensive response from the international sports administration as they navigate ongoing challenges to their authority.
