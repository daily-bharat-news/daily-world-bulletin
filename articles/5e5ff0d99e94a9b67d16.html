Indian Boxers Eye Asian Games and Olympics After Success

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 09:08 AM · Source checked

Recent reports highlight the inspiring journeys of Indian boxers, including Commonwealth Games champions Preeti Pawar and Arundhati Chaudhary, alongside Sakshi's calculated success. Preeti has now turned her focus toward the upcoming Asian Games and the long-term goal of securing an Olympic medal at the Los Angeles 2028 Games. Meanwhile, Arundhati's remarkable path to gold involved stepping away from an IIT aspiration to pursue boxing full-time. These developments underscore the dedication and challenges within Indian boxing as athletes aim for higher international milestones.
