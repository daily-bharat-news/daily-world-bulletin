Irregular heartbeat linked to paralysis risk: Dr Dinesh Kumar

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 07:18 AM · Source checked

According to Dr Dinesh Kumar, patients suffering from irregular heartbeats face a risk of paralysis. Medical discussions also highlight why irregular heart rhythms can appear decades early in certain individuals, alongside treatments for atrial fibrillation. Reports indicate that millions of Indians may be living with undiagnosed heart conditions. Experts emphasize recognizing early signs to prevent severe health complications.
