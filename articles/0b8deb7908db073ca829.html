Rains and floods claim lives and displace thousands in southern India

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 10:11 AM · Source checked

Heavy monsoon rains and flash floods have struck southern India, causing widespread disruption, displacing thousands of residents, and resulting in multiple fatalities across states like Kerala and Karnataka. According to reports from various news sources, merchant bodies stated that flash floods have severely impacted Kerala's trading sector. Rescue and relief operations are underway as authorities monitor the situation and assist affected populations displaced by the severe weather conditions. Further updates on casualties and damages remain limited at this stage.
