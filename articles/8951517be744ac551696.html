US Senate panel votes to hold Fauci in contempt over Covid hearing

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 08:17 PM · Source checked

A US Senate committee has voted to hold Dr Anthony Fauci in contempt of Congress. The decision follows his refusal to answer questions regarding his management of the Covid-19 pandemic. The committee will now refer the matter for possible prosecution. Details regarding the next steps in the legal process are limited at this stage.
