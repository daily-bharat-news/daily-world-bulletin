Zendaya and Tom Holland emerge as top box office earners for 2026

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 03:25 AM · Source checked

According to recent entertainment reports, Zendaya has achieved the status of the highest-grossing actor for the year 2026. This comes as Tom Holland joins her in the upcoming project Spider-Man: Brand New Day. Media outlets including Forbes and The Economist highlight that their collaboration cements the duo as the most bankable and successful box office couple in Hollywood. Various publications note this milestone as a significant movie triumph for the younger generation of actors in the film industry.
