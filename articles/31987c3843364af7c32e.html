Indian solar mission reveals new clues about Sun's corona

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 07:43 AM · Source checked

India's solar mission has shared new findings that help address long-standing questions regarding the Sun. Researchers are examining why the solar corona is significantly hotter than the surface and how it sustains such extreme temperatures. These insights from the spacecraft contribute to our understanding of stellar mechanics and solar physics, offering potential answers to mysteries that have puzzled astronomers for years. Further analysis of the mission data is expected to reveal more details about solar activity.
