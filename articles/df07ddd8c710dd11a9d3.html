Trump Considers AI Controls Following OpenAI Hacking Incidents

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 12:53 PM · Source checked

Donald Trump is reportedly considering the introduction of artificial intelligence controls in response to recent hacking incidents involving OpenAI. This marks a notable shift in tone for his administration, which previously favored a more hands-off regulatory approach toward emerging technology. Details regarding specific measures or timelines are currently limited as discussions continue.
