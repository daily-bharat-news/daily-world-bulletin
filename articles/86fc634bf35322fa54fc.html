Trainer aircraft crashes near Baramati airstrip in Maharashtra

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 02:17 PM · Source checked

A trainer aircraft belonging to a private company has skidded off the runway and crashed near the Baramati airstrip in Maharashtra. Official reports confirm that there are no casualties resulting from the incident, and the pilot is reported to be safe. This event marks the second such aircraft incident in the area following an earlier mishap involving Ajit Pawar. Authorities have initiated an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the runway excursion. Further details regarding the exact cause of the crash are currently limited as investigations proceed.
