Ancient algae known as pink zebra joins Natural History Museum

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 05:13 AM · Source checked

An ancient algae, often referred to as the pink zebra seaweed due to its unique appearance and elusive nature, is set to join the permanent collection at the Natural History Museum. First discovered four decades ago, this rare organism has fascinated researchers for years, leading to dedicated scientific study. The inclusion of this specimen in the museum will help preserve its history and allow further research into its distinct characteristics and evolutionary background, marking a significant addition to the institution's extensive biological archives.
