Putin can no longer claim victory in Ukraine, says Dmitry Muratov

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 10:25 AM · Source checked

In an exclusive interview with the BBC, Nobel Peace Prize winner Dmitry Muratov stated that the Kremlin leader can only destroy Ukraine and not conquer it. With the conflict now entering its fifth year, the prominent journalist shared his perspective on the ongoing war during the discussion with Steve Rosenberg, highlighting the current limitations faced by the Russian leadership in achieving its original military objectives.
