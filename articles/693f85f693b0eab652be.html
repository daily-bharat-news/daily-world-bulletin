AIFF Considers FIFA ASEAN Cup Withdrawal For Brazil Match

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 06:08 PM · Source checked

The All India Football Federation is evaluating a potential withdrawal from the FIFA ASEAN Cup to prioritize a high-profile friendly match between India and Brazil. Following an Executive Committee meeting at Football House, players expressed a mix of excitement and anxiety regarding the upcoming fixture. While captain Sandesh Jhingan eagerly anticipates performing on the major stage, the decision has also sparked discussions and differing viewpoints within the football fraternity regarding tournament priorities.
