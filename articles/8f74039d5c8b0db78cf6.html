BBC analyses viral AI disaster videos in China amid extreme weather

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 03:30 AM · Source checked

As extreme weather events increase, fake artificial intelligence videos are spreading rapidly across online platforms in China. The BBC has examined these viral clips, highlighting how manufactured footage of disasters is causing genuine problems in the real world. The rapid sharing of such misleading content underscores growing concerns over the authenticity of digital media during crises.
