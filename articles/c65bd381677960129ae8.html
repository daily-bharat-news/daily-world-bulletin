Progress made in tackling giant hogweed in County Tyrone

Vinay · 28 Jul 2026, 10:53 AM · Source checked

A conservation group in County Tyrone reports making headway in controlling infestations of giant hogweed. The plant, known for causing painful blisters and scars upon contact, has been the focus of ongoing eradication efforts by local conservationists. Details regarding the specific methods used or the exact scale of the reduction are currently limited. The initiative aims to mitigate the environmental and public health risks associated with the invasive species across the affected areas in the county.
