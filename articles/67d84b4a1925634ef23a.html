Former SpaceX engineer Andre Lavoie looks to cash in his company shares

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 10:30 AM · Source checked

Andre Lavoie, who started working as an engineer at SpaceX in 2009, is now planning to sell portions of his equity in the private aerospace enterprise. As employee shareholders seek opportunities to realize value from their holdings, individuals like Lavoie are opting to divest small amounts of their stock whenever possible. Such transactions highlight the evolving financial landscape for long-tenured staff at high-profile private companies, allowing early workers to benefit from the firm's substantial growth and valuation milestones over the years.
