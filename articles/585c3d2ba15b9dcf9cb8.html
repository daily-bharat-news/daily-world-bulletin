Tata & Mahindra Lead Electric Car Market With Triple-Digit Growth in July

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 11:20 AM · Source checked

Tata and Mahindra have captured the forefront of the electric car segment, recording remarkable triple-digit growth during the month of July. According to industry reports from HT Auto, this significant surge highlights the accelerating adoption and demand for electric vehicles in the domestic market. Both automotive manufacturers have strengthened their market positions through strong sales performance, reflecting a positive shift towards sustainable mobility solutions among Indian consumers.
