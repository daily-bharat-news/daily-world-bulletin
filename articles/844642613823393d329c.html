HPV Vaccination Drive Gains Momentum In Karnataka Amid Rising Demand

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 01:01 AM · Source checked

A free human papillomavirus vaccination drive is gaining strong momentum across Karnataka due to a surge in public demand, offering renewed hope in the prevention of cervical cancer. Meanwhile, health reports from other regions note that low turnout at certain vaccination camps is driven by fears and misinformation, prompting medical professionals and officials to urge the public to trust scientific research rather than apprehensions. Raising awareness about HPV remains a crucial focus for eliminating cervical cancer globally and nationally.
