Kidnapped rare Serbian eagle freed into wild after rescue

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 02:10 AM · Source checked

Feliks, a rare Serbian eagle, has been successfully released back into the wild following a dramatic rescue. The bird was abducted shortly after its first flight from the nest in Serbia and ended up in the Middle East with poachers. The captors had demanded a ransom for the rare animal before authorities and conservationists intervened to secure its freedom. Environmental experts have welcomed the safe return of the eagle to its natural habitat.
