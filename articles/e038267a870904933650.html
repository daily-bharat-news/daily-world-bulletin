Israel Rejects Trump's Gaza Plan Unless Hamas Disarms

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 05:42 PM · Source checked

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has turned down a proposed 15-point peace plan for Gaza, stating clearly that the nation's military forces will not withdraw from the territory until Hamas is thoroughly and genuinely disarmed. This rejection highlights the ongoing deadlock in negotiations and underlines Israel's strict security conditions for ending the current conflict in the region, according to the latest reports from international broadcasters.
