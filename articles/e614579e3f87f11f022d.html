Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 04:55 AM · Source checked

His investigation explores the environmental and health legacy of hazardous industrial waste disposal in the region. Programme details remain limited based on the current announcement, but the documentary focuses on uncovering the extent of these toxic substances and their potential impact on local communities.
