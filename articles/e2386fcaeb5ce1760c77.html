India electric car sales jump 82pc in July

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 05:00 AM · Source checked

India witnessed an eighty-two percent increase in electric car sales during the month of July. This significant growth was primarily driven by high fuel prices, which have successfully boosted the demand for electric vehicles across the country. Consumers are increasingly shifting towards electric mobility options as traditional fuel costs remain elevated. Industry reports highlight this positive trend as a major milestone for the domestic automotive sector.
