Sheikh Hasina vows to return to Bangladesh, sparking political fury

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 02:48 PM · Source checked

Exiled former Bangladesh Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina has stated her intention to return to the country, drawing sharp reactions and fury in Bangladesh where she faces conviction. Her recent virtual address from Delhi has further strained bilateral relations between India and Bangladesh. While facing public outrage, Indian officials have clarified that India was not involved in organising her press event. Meanwhile, aides of Bangladesh leadership have challenged her to return immediately, raising questions about whether the country will accept the convicted former leader.
