Arsenal Explore Alternatives as Vinicius Junior Set to Stay at Real Madrid

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 06:33 PM · Source checked

Arsenal are reportedly working on alternative transfer plans as reports indicate that Vinicius Junior will remain at Real Madrid beyond the upcoming summer transfer window. With a potential move for the Real Madrid winger appearing increasingly unlikely, media reports suggest the Gunners are considering other attacking options. Names such as Bradley Barcola and Marcus Rashford have been mentioned in discussions regarding potential alternatives for the North London club. However, official confirmation and specific details regarding these prospective moves remain limited at this stage.
