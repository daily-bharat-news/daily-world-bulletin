Tata Steel sells Jamshedpur FC stake to Churchill Brothers

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 01:32 PM · Source checked

Tata Steel has transferred its complete ownership in Jamshedpur FC to Churchill Brothers for a nominal amount of one hundred rupees. The transaction also includes the transfer of the Indian Super League license. Meanwhile, discussions regarding broadcast partners and the upcoming season schedule continue among clubs ahead of the anticipated October start for the league.
