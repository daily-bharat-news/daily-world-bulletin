Houthi attacks kill at least 30 Yemeni government forces

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 09:02 PM · Source checked

Recent Houthi strikes have resulted in the deaths of at least 30 Yemeni government troops, according to reports. The group claimed responsibility for launching drones and missiles targeting what they described as Saudi military build-ups situated within the central provinces of Marib and Hadramawt. Further details regarding the aftermath of these incidents remain limited as monitoring continues.
