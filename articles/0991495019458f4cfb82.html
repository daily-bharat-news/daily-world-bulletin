CJI Surya Kant disapproves BCI action against NALSAR students

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 10:59 AM · Source checked

Chief Justice Surya Kant has strongly criticized the Bar Council of India for its disciplinary actions against NALSAR University students. The students had previously staged a protest regarding judicial matters. Emphasizing that peaceful dissent is a fundamental right, the Chief Justice questioned the authority of the Bar Council to intervene in matters concerning students and the judiciary. He remarked that any concerns should remain strictly between the students and himself, leading to widespread calls for a reconsideration of the official regulatory measures.
