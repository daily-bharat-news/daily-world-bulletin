Office summer wear: What is acceptable to wear to work?

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 06:15 PM · Source checked

Navigating summer office wear involves balancing personal comfort during hot weather with professional standards. Employees often wonder about the appropriateness of items like shorts, strappy tops, and sandals in a corporate setting. Understanding workplace dress codes helps professionals maintain a cool and confident look while respecting company policies. Clarifying these expectations with employers ensures workers can dress successfully for the season without crossing professional boundaries.
