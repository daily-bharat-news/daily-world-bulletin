New technology seeks to remove recycling barriers caused by paper labels and glue

Vinay · 28 Jul 2026, 04:30 AM · Source checked

Labels and adhesives often hinder the paper recycling process by preventing proper material recovery. However, emerging technological solutions are currently being developed to address this persistent challenge. These innovations aim to make paper products easier to recycle without interference from sticky residues or synthetic attachments, potentially improving overall recycling efficiency. Details are limited regarding the exact mechanisms of this new technology, but industry experts are hopeful it will transform standard recycling practices for paper goods.
