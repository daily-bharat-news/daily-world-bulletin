Pavitra Rishta Actor Usha Nadkarni Opens Up On Life At 80

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 09:23 AM · Source checked

Veteran television actress Usha Nadkarni, known for her role in Pavitra Rishta, has shared details about her personal life and struggles. At 80 years old, she has been living alone since 1987. She reflected on surviving a violent childhood and facing forty years of loneliness. The actress also spoke about her relationship with her son and stated that she is no longer afraid of death.
