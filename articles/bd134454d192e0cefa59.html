Rahul Gandhi criticizes government and RSS over student protests

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 03:06 PM · Source checked

During the Lok Sabha proceedings, Rahul Gandhi launched an attack against the central government and the RSS regarding student protests. He demanded that Prime Minister Narendra Modi remove Amit Shah following alleged police brutality against young protesters. In response, the BJP accused Gandhi of spreading misinformation concerning police actions and demanded an apology. Meanwhile, the Lok Sabha passed the Vande Mataram bill before adjournment, and the Rajya Sabha debated the paper leak bill during the ongoing Monsoon Session.
