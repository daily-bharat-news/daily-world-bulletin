New UK Chancellor Healey Announces October Budget

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 09:04 AM · Source checked

The newly appointed chancellor has announced that the upcoming budget will take place in October. As part of the announcement, the chancellor pledged to decentralize financial resources and authority away from Westminster, aiming to distribute funds and power to communities across every postcode in Britain. Further details regarding the specific policy measures and allocations within the budget remain limited at this stage.
