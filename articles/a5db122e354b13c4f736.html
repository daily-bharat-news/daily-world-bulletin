Supreme Court Quashes Defamation Case Against Rahul Gandhi

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 12:17 PM · Source checked

The Supreme Court has quashed a criminal defamation complaint and summons issued against Congress leader Rahul Gandhi. The legal action originated from his remarks regarding Veer Savarkar, wherein he termed him a British collaborator. The top court set aside the Lucknow court order, providing relief to the politician. Legal reports confirm the cancellation of the proceedings tied to the controversial comments. Details regarding the specific observations made by the bench are limited at present.
