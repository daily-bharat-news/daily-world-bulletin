Fuel theft costs UK forecourts nearly £200,000 daily amid rising incidents

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 06:34 AM · Source checked

Industry analysis reveals that incidents of fuel being taken without payment across the United Kingdom have increased by a fifth over a five-month period. Data indicates that approximately £200,000 worth of fuel is now being stolen from forecourts every day since the conflict involving Iran began. This sharp rise highlights growing financial pressures and security concerns for fuel retailers, prompting calls for closer monitoring and preventative measures to curb ongoing losses at service stations nationwide.
