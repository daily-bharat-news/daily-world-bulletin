Russian drones target medics and hospitals in Ukraine, warn workers

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 05:32 AM · Source checked

Ukrainian emergency personnel have reported an escalation in targeted Russian drone attacks affecting hospitals and ambulances across the country. According to the BBC World report, frontline medical workers are facing extreme risks while carrying out rescue operations. Staff described harrowing experiences of fleeing strikes, knowing that staying behind could mean certain death. These incidents highlight the severe dangers confronting healthcare providers operating within active conflict zones in Ukraine, as emergency services struggle to maintain critical life-saving operations amidst ongoing hostilities.
