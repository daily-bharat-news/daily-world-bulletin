Tom Percy Advances NASA's Artemis Lunar and Mars Goals

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 10:05 PM · Source checked

As NASA prepares for long-term space exploration focused on the Moon and Mars, the agency is accelerating its Artemis missions. Tom Percy, manager of systems engineering and integration for the Human Landing System Program, is playing a key role in this effort. He acts as a primary focal point for collaborating with commercial providers such as SpaceX to help bring these ambitious exploration plans to fruition.
