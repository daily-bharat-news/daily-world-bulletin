India Slams New York Times For Misleading PoK Headline

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 08:30 AM · Source checked

The Indian Embassy in the United States and official authorities have strongly criticized The New York Times over its terminology regarding Pakistan-occupied Kashmir. Condemning the headline as misleading and incorrect, Indian representatives emphasized that the region is strictly referred to as Pakistan-occupied Kashmir, rejecting any notion of Pakistani Kashmir. Details regarding the specific context of the publication and subsequent diplomatic communications remain limited based on the current reports.
