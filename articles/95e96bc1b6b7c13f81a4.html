Haryana's boxing champions receive grand homecoming after CWG success

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 04:37 AM · Source checked

Haryana recently rolled out a heroes' welcome for its boxing champions following their impressive performance at the Commonwealth Games, where India secured a record number of gold medals. Notable stories include Jaismine Lamboria, who overcame significant challenges, from a hospital bed to winning gold in Glasgow. Athletes shared personal anecdotes, such as choosing boxing after defeating boys in school. These triumphs in the ring have successfully set the stage for the upcoming Los Angeles 2028 Olympic preparations, showcasing the strength of Indian pugilists on the international sports stage.
