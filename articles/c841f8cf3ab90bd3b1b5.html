Zendaya Discusses Working With Tom Holland on Spider-Man

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 01:51 AM · Source checked

At the London premiere of their latest film, Spiderman: Brand New Day, actors Zendaya and Tom Holland walked the white carpet together. Speaking about sharing the screen with her partner, Zendaya noted that the working experience feels natural because they are best friends. The couple made a joint appearance to promote the new movie, drawing attention from fans and media alike at the high-profile capital event. Further details regarding the production remain limited at this time.
