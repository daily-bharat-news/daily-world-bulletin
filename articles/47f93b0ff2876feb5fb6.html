NASA Announces MAX POWER Air and Space Expo for America's 250th

Vinay · 15 Aug 2026, 12:14 AM · Source checked

In honor of America's upcoming 250th anniversary, NASA has announced MAX POWER, a public exposition celebrating American air and space innovation. Scheduled for November 7 and 8, the multi-day family event will take place on and near the agency's Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Visitors will have the opportunity to explore next-generation aircraft, advanced spacecraft, autonomous vehicles, and cutting-edge technologies that are set to shape the future of aerospace exploration and transportation.
