Auto R&D spending in India to face pressure from Chinese rivals and slow EV uptake

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 02:57 PM · Source checked

According to a Kotak report, research and development spending within India's automobile sector is expected to remain under pressure. This situation arises due to mounting competition from Chinese players alongside a slower adoption rate of electric vehicles in the domestic market. Industry stakeholders must navigate these market dynamics carefully as financial commitments toward new product development and technological advancements are impacted by these emerging trends.
