BJP Slams Rahul Gandhi Over Mock Hug Jibe At PM Modi

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 09:17 AM · Source checked

The Bharatiya Janata Party has strongly criticized Congress leader Rahul Gandhi following his recent remarks concerning Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Several party leaders expressed strong disapproval, accusing the opposition leader of crossing lines of decency regarding foreign policy discussions. In response to the comments, BJP leader Nishikant Dubey referenced historical photographs to counter the remarks. The ongoing war of words has sparked a major political uproar across various media platforms, with the ruling party demanding maturity from the senior opposition figure.
