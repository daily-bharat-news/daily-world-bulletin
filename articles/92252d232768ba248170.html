Mamata Banerjee Alleges Attack On Her Car Near Kolkata

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 04:02 PM · Source checked

West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee has alleged that her convoy came under attack near Kolkata. The incident reportedly occurred while she was visiting the family of a Trinamool Congress worker who died while in police custody. According to reports, stones and shoes were thrown at her vehicle, and mud was smeared on the cars during the visit. The Chief Minister stated that she could have died in the incident. Meanwhile, she also faced protests from locals during her travel to the residence of the deceased party worker. Further details regarding the security breach are awaited.
