H1N1 and Viral Fever Cases Rise Across Delhi-NCR

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 10:36 AM · Source checked

Medical professionals have reported a notable increase in H1N1 cases, influenza-like illnesses, and viral fevers across the Delhi-NCR region during the ongoing monsoon and seasonal transition. Doctors note that changing weather conditions have triggered this health surge and are advising residents to exercise caution. Citizens are urged to remain vigilant and watch out for common symptoms associated with these seasonal infections. Further specific details regarding patient numbers and hospital admissions remain limited based on current reports.
