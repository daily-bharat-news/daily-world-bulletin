Andy Burnham to give regional mayors share of income tax

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 03:02 AM · Source checked

Prime Minister Andy Burnham has announced plans to grant regional mayors a share of income tax revenue. Supporters claim the policy will empower local communities across every postcode, ensuring greater financial autonomy for regional authorities. However, critics have raised concerns, arguing that the current proposals lack necessary detail and require further clarification regarding their implementation and long-term economic impact.
