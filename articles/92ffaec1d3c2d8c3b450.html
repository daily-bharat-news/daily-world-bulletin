Rishabh Pant appeals to Uttarakhand CM Dhami for land to build home

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 12:35 PM · Source checked

Cricketer Rishabh Pant has made a public appeal to Uttarakhand Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami for assistance in purchasing land to build a home in his native state, expressing a strong desire to return. Chief Minister Dhami responded positively to the request, stating that officials would contact the cricketer. The appeal gained significant attention on social media, drawing widespread reactions from the public and media outlets alike regarding the challenges faced in acquiring property in the region.
