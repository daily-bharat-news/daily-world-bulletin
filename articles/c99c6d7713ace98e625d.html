Australia takes Telegram to court over pro-terror material

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 08:33 AM · Source checked

Australia has initiated legal action against the messaging platform Telegram, alleging it failed to remove pro-terrorist content from its service. Details regarding the specific court proceedings and the nature of the content remain limited based on the available information. In response to the legal challenge, Telegram has stated to the BBC that it completely rejects the allegations made against the platform and intends to vigorously contest them in court as the case moves forward.
