Jharkhand CM Vows Action As Exam Protests Continue

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 05:44 PM · Source checked

Jharkhand Chief Minister has assured justice for protesting youth, stating that strict action will be taken against those found guilty of irregularities in examinations. Following discussions with the state government, student representatives announced that their protests would continue. A government panel also met a protester on a hunger strike to urge him to end his fast. Meanwhile, participants maintained that future opportunities for the youth must be protected, as demonstrations and talks unfold across the state.
