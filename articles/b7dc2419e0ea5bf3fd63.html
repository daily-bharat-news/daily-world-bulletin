VVS Laxman defends CoE amid India's growing player injury crisis

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 02:19 PM · Source checked

National Cricket Academy head VVS Laxman has clarified that the Centre of Excellence is not just a rehabilitation facility, as discussions intensify regarding player fitness concerns. The statement comes amidst a growing injury crisis affecting the Indian cricket team. Meanwhile, BCCI secretary Saikia is scheduled to visit the CoE to review the situation and meet with Laxman. Reports also highlight concerns about players managing injuries during the IPL due to financial and franchise pressures, bringing renewed focus on scheduling and workload management.
