India Needs 37 Runs to Win Tour Match Against Sri Lanka XI

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 05:30 PM · Source checked

During the tour match day three updates against Sri Lanka XI, India reached 170 for three wickets and required 37 more runs for victory. Jadeja was at the crease as the innings progressed. Earlier, Devdutt Padikkal made a strong claim for the number three position by scoring 142 runs, which highlighted India's batting response. Openers Shubman Gill and Yashasvi Jaiswal also helped keep the target chase on track with a solid start during the warm-up fixture, bringing positive takeaways for the team.
