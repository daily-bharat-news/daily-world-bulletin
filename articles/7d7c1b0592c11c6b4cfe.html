Drone Footage Shows Wisconsin Tornado Damage

Vinay · 28 Jul 2026, 07:33 PM · Source checked

Newly released drone video captures the extent of the damage following an EF-2 tornado that struck areas in Wisconsin. Authorities confirmed that no injuries or deaths were immediately reported in the aftermath of the severe weather event in the Midwest state. Official assessments of the destruction are currently underway as local teams evaluate the affected properties and infrastructure.
