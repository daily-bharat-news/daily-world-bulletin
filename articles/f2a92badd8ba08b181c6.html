WHO Chief warns DRC Ebola outbreak could become deadliest ever

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 12:15 AM · Source checked

The World Health Organisation Chief has expressed serious concern over the escalating Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo, warning that it is on track to become the deadliest ever recorded in the region. With fatalities having already crossed the two thousand mark, global health authorities have established a strict three-month timeline to bring the crisis completely under control. This situation marks the second-largest outbreak in history, prompting intensified international health interventions and monitoring across affected areas.
