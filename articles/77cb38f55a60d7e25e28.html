Southern Brave secure fifth successive win in The Hundred

Vinay · 02 Aug 2026, 03:48 AM · Source checked

Southern Brave extended their winning streak to five matches with a comfortable victory in the 16th fixture of the tournament held in London on August 1, 2026. Contributions from players like Archer and Stoinis kept their playoff qualification hopes robust, while Molineux delivered an effective spin bowling performance to sustain the team's flawless campaign. Meanwhile, Jemimah Rodrigues had a quiet outing on matchday 12 as Southern Brave maintained their dominance in the ongoing competition.
