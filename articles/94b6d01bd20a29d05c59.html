Cipla and INASL Launch Nationwide Hepatitis B Screening Campaign

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 02:33 PM · Source checked

On World Hepatitis Day, Cipla and the Indian National Association for the Study of the Liver introduced a countrywide screening initiative for Hepatitis B. Experts from AIIMS emphasized that early diagnosis remains crucial for preventing deaths associated with the condition. Meanwhile, the Ministry of Health reported that over 21 crore individuals have undergone screening as part of India's ongoing efforts to combat the virus. Medical professionals also highlighted important warning signs of this silent epidemic, urging patients to seek timely medical assistance.
