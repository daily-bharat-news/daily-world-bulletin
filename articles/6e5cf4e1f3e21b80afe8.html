Fact-checking Netflix's Operation Safed Sagar on Kargil War

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 11:58 AM · Source checked

Netflix's new war drama series, Operation Safed Sagar, has sparked discussions regarding historical events of the Kargil War, including the actions and roles of Pervez Musharraf, Atal Bihari Vajpayee, and Nawaz Sharif. Critics have shared varied reviews about the show, examining its depiction of real-life military heroes, its narrative approach avoiding excessive bloodshed, and its critique of jingoistic tropes. While some publications praised the performances of Siddharth and Jimmy Shergill, others evaluated the show's balance between cinematic storytelling and historical accuracy.
