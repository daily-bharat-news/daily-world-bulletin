CWG 2026: Lovepreet wins silver, Seema bags bronze

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 04:49 AM · Source checked

At the Commonwealth Games 2026 in Glasgow on Thursday, Indian weightlifter Lovepreet secured a silver medal after narrowly missing gold by one kilogram to David Liti. Meanwhile, Seema earned a bronze medal in the discus throw event. With these additions, India concluded its weightlifting campaign with a total of eight medals. Following the results on Day 8 of the competition, India moved down to the 10th position in the overall medal standings. Further details regarding other events on the day remain limited.
