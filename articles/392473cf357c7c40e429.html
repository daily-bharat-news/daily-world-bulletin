Colorado marmot lab turns to OnlyFans for funds

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 03:26 AM · Source checked

A research laboratory studying marmots in Colorado has launched an OnlyFans account to raise money following recent funding cuts. The team managing the ground-squirrels developed this unusual financial strategy to sustain their ongoing scientific work and care for the animals. Details regarding the success of the campaign or the specific budget shortfall are currently limited.
