EU holds emergency meeting on Ceuta migrant arrivals

Vinay · 02 Aug 2026, 01:39 AM · Source checked

The European Union has convened an emergency meeting to address the recent arrival of migrants in the Spanish enclave of Ceuta. The situation has led to tense exchanges between Spain and other member states concerning the management of the influx. Officials are discussing cooperative measures to handle the border situation effectively. Further details regarding the outcomes of these discussions are currently limited, as European authorities continue to assess the developments.
