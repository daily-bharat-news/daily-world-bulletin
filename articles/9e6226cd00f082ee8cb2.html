Famous California bald eagle Jackie dies after intensive care

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 04:10 AM · Source checked

Jackie, a well-known bald eagle in California who captured global attention alongside her partner Shadow, has died following weeks of intensive care. The pair had been continuously observed by millions of online viewers over the years via the popular Big Bear Eagle Nest Cam, making their nesting journey a major point of interest for wildlife enthusiasts worldwide before her recent passing.
