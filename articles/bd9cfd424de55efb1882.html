Canada flight cancelled after child refuses to wear seat belt

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 02:36 AM · Source checked

A commercial flight in Canada was cancelled after a child passenger refused to wear a seat belt prior to takeoff. The unusual incident has triggered a discussion regarding child safety protocols and passenger discipline aboard commercial aircraft. Authorities and airlines continue to review safety compliance rules for families traveling with young children, highlighting the challenges crews face when enforcement becomes difficult during boarding.
