FIFA backs President Infantino after World Cup media rights plan collapses

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 04:38 AM · Source checked

FIFA has expressed full support for President Gianni Infantino following the collapse of a proposed media rights plan for the World Cup. During an executive meeting, Infantino addressed the situation and chose to remain in his position. Media reports indicate that the governing body is facing increased scrutiny over various commercial initiatives, including the shelved FIFA Forward Enterprise. Critics and observers are raising broader questions about football administration and governance standards as the organization navigates this ongoing leadership and commercial challenge.
