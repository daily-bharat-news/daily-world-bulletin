India prepares for Galle Test amid pitch intrigue and crosswinds

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 07:44 AM · Source checked

The Indian cricket team has stepped up training sessions in Galle ahead of their upcoming first Test match against Sri Lanka. While questions surround the nature of the pitch and challenging local crosswinds have added tactical intrigue to the fixture, reports indicate that the visiting squad is likely to choose a conventional and cautious path for the opening encounter. Meanwhile, players including KL Rahul and Rishabh Pant were spotted unwinding during the tour preparations, as the team puts in rigorous practice hours before the series begins.
