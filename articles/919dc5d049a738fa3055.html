Manchester Super Giants Reach The Hundred 2026 Final

Vinay · 15 Aug 2026, 01:40 AM · Source checked

Manchester Super Giants advanced to the final of The Hundred 2026 after defeating Sunrisers Leeds in the Eliminator match held on August 14, 2026, in London. Paul Walter and Heinrich Klaasen delivered a strong batting performance, while Josh Tongue claimed four wickets to secure the victory. With this win, the Super Giants successfully booked their place in the tournament's title clash.
