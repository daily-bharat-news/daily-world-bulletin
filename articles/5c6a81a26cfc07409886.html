Teen hackers tell BBC how police guide them to use skills for good

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 06:12 AM · Source checked

The UK police Cyber Prevent initiative is working with teenage boys and young men who are at risk of getting involved in cybercrime. Instead of punishment, the program aims to channel their technical abilities into legal and positive career paths. Teenagers who participated have shared their experiences with the BBC, explaining how police guidance helped them steer away from illicit online activities. This proactive approach focuses on education and rehabilitation, targeting young individuals before they make irreversible mistakes in the digital world.
