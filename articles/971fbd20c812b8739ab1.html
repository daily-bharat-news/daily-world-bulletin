Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 04:52 AM · Source checked

As summer temperatures rise, employees often wonder about workplace dress codes and how much control managers have over summer outfits like shorts, strappy tops, and sandals. Balancing personal comfort with professional standards can be challenging during hot weather.
