BSP's lone UP MLA Umashankar Singh passes away at 55

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 01:02 AM · Source checked

Umashankar Singh, the lone Uttar Pradesh legislator of the Bahujan Samaj Party, has passed away at the age of 55 following a battle with cancer. BSP chief Mayawati paid her final respects to the departed leader in Lucknow and expressed profound grief over his demise. Several other political figures, including Uttar Pradesh Assembly Speaker Satish Mahana, also conveyed their condolences over the passing of the party's sole MLA in the state.
