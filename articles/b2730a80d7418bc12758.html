Thousands Evacuated on Greek Island of Crete Amid Wildfires

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 02:40 PM · Source checked

Thousands of residents and visitors have been evacuated from the Rethymno region on the Greek island of Crete as raging wildfires continue to spread. Strong winds in the area have significantly fanned the flames, worsening the ongoing wildfire crisis across the European region. Authorities are actively responding to the disaster as firefighters struggle to contain the blazes driven by severe weather conditions. Further information regarding the full extent of the damage and ongoing rescue operations is currently limited.
