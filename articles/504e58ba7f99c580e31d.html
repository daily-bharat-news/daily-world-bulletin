LA warehouse fire leaves tonnes of rotting food creating foul stench

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 05:45 AM · Source checked

A massive fire destroyed a large warehouse in Los Angeles, leaving behind huge piles of decaying chicken, poultry and fish. The rotting food has created an unbearable stench in the surrounding area, while also attracting flies and rats. Local authorities are dealing with the aftermath of the blaze as residents cope with the foul odour and potential health concerns arising from the waste. Further details regarding the cleanup operations and exact causes remain limited at this stage.
