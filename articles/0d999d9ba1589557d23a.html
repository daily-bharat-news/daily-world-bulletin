World number one Aryna Sabalenka stunned by Alexandrova at Canadian Open

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 12:47 PM · Source checked

World number one Aryna Sabalenka suffered a shock defeat against Ekaterina Alexandrova at the Canadian Open in Toronto, marking another setback ahead of the US Open. Sabalenka struggled to find her rhythm as her opponent successfully broke through her defense to secure a place in the quarterfinals. Alongside this upset, other notable players such as Cameron Norrie and Jessica Pegula also faced unexpected exits during the fourth round of the tournament, leaving the draws wide open.
