Hunter Biden says pardon harmed America and his father's legacy

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 03:37 AM · Source checked

In a wide-ranging BBC interview, Hunter Biden stated that his presidential pardon was not beneficial for the United States or for his father's political legacy. He denied having any ambition to run for public office and additionally shared that his father's cancer has unfortunately spread. The remarks provide insight into the family's current reflections following the high-profile pardon and ongoing health challenges.
