Food preservatives may raise heart disease risk, doctors warn

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 05:30 AM · Source checked

Medical experts have warned that consuming food preservatives can elevate the risk of developing heart disease. Doctors have advised individuals to identify and avoid specific processed food items containing these additives to protect their cardiovascular health. While detailed information from the source is currently limited, health professionals consistently recommend opting for fresh, wholesome foods over heavily processed alternatives to maintain long-term well-being and prevent chronic ailments.
