Trump signs order to limit childhood vaccines and split MMR shots

Vinay · 11 Aug 2026, 08:13 AM · Source checked

United States President Donald Trump has signed an executive order aimed at limiting childhood vaccines and splitting up the combined measles, mumps, and rubella shots. In response to this announcement, the American Academy of Pediatrics has strongly criticized the directive, calling the official recommendation regarding the MMR vaccines dangerous for public health. Further details regarding the implementation and timeline of this new executive order remain limited at this stage.
