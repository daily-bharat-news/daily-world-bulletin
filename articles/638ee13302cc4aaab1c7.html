Indian-flagged ship sinks off Yemen coast after projectile strike

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 02:54 AM · Source checked

An Indian-flagged vessel sank off the coast of Yemen following a projectile strike. India's ministry of external affairs confirmed that all fourteen individuals on board the ship were successfully rescued. Details regarding the attackers and the precise nature of the projectile remain limited. Authorities continue to monitor the situation closely to ensure maritime security in the region.
