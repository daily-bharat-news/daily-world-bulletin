Lung cancer risk factors beyond smoking explained by doctors

Vinay · 12 Aug 2026, 05:00 AM · Source checked

Recent medical discussions highlight that lung cancer affects non-smokers too, driven by environmental and daily lifestyle factors. Oncologists and health experts note that risks are present in indoor air quality, households, and routine habits, prompting calls for updated screening methods in India that look beyond traditional smoking histories. Experts emphasize the importance of recognizing early warning signs and adopting advanced detection technologies like artificial intelligence to improve case-finding among diverse patient groups.
