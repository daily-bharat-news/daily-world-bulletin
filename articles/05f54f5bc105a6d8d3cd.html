Cloudy skies bring mixed fortunes for solar eclipse watchers in Scotland

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 11:10 AM · Source checked

Scotland recently experienced its best opportunity to view a solar eclipse in 27 years, yet overcast weather resulted in mixed experiences for spectators. While the cloud cover caused disappointment for many hopeful stargazers across the region, there were also brief, magical moments when the phenomenon became visible. Observers had eagerly anticipated the rare celestial event, hoping for clear conditions to witness the alignment, but the weather proved to be unpredictable throughout the viewing window.
