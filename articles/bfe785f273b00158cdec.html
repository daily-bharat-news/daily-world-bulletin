US Navy sailors face severe hardships aboard deployed aircraft carrier

Vinay · 14 Aug 2026, 04:36 AM · Source checked

Thousands of crew members aboard the USS Abraham Lincoln have reportedly endured challenging conditions during a deployment lasting over 250 days. Reports indicate that the sailors faced issues such as food shortages and broken plumbing facilities while at sea. The situation has raised significant concerns, with some individuals even considering jumping overboard due to the prolonged and difficult circumstances. Further official details regarding the ship's current operational status and mitigation measures remain limited at this time.
