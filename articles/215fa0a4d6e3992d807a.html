Centre deploys team as Chandipura virus cases surge

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 07:39 PM · Source checked

The Central government has deployed a national joint outbreak response team to support states dealing with a Chandipura virus outbreak. A multi-institutional probe has intensified as the virus caused 22 deaths among children in Gujarat during the ongoing monsoon season. Reports highlight outbreaks impacting states like Gujarat and Rajasthan, prompting critical public health measures. Authorities are closely monitoring the situation while focusing on symptoms, treatment protocols, and prevention strategies to contain the public health threat.
