Sreeshankar secures silver at Commonwealth Games

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 02:10 AM · Source checked

Murali Sreeshankar made history at the Commonwealth Games by claiming his second consecutive long jump silver medal, while fellow Indian athlete Lokesh Sathyanathan finished in fifth place. This achievement marks a significant milestone as Sreeshankar becomes the first Indian long jumper to win two medals at the Games. Meanwhile, para-athlete Dilip Mahadu Gavit also secured a gold medal for the country. Further details regarding the events and the overall medal standings for India on Day 7 of the competition remain limited based on the provided reports.
