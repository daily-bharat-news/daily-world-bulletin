Live imaging of cancer cells may transform drug discovery

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 06:56 AM · Source checked

Recent scientific explorations combining artificial intelligence, chip technology, and Ramanomics are enabling researchers to observe living cancer cells in real-time. Often described as turning cell biology into live cinema, this innovative approach allows scientists to watch cancer cells live, which could significantly transform drug discovery and advance medical research. Experts note that these advanced imaging techniques provide deeper insights into cellular behavior, helping to improve the development of new treatments and therapies.
