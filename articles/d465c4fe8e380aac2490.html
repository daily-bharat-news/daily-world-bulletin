Cockroach Janta Party announces nationwide drive and expansion plans

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 03:52 PM · Source checked

The Cockroach Janta Party has announced a nationwide membership drive, a public dialogue initiative named 'Kya Bolti Public', and an expansion plan starting in September. Aiming to function as a pressure group, the party intends to make unemployment a major national issue and support upcoming protests in Jharkhand. Additionally, the party has formed a National Working Committee to improve its overall outreach and organizational structure across the country.
