Vomiting acts as body's defence mechanism, explains gut health expert

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 03:00 PM · Source checked

A gut health expert and dietitian recently shared insights regarding vomiting, describing it as a natural defence mechanism used by the human body. According to the specialists, this physiological response helps expel harmful substances or toxins ingested through food or caused by infections. While often unpleasant, it serves an important protective function for overall digestive health. Detailed guidance on managing symptoms and when to seek medical attention was discussed, though specific clinical recommendations remain limited in the current report.
