Ebola outbreak heading to be deadliest ever: WHO

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 07:01 AM · Source checked

According to the World Health Organization chief, the ongoing Ebola outbreak is on track to become the deadliest in history. Declared on 15 May, the health emergency has already resulted in more than 2,000 deaths, alongside thousands of additional reported cases. Authorities are closely monitoring the situation as the disease continues to spread and impact affected regions significantly.
