NASA Highlights Tidal Dynamics and Shipping Routes at Elbe River Mouth

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 09:31 AM · Source checked

Data from NASA illustrates the changing tides and dynamic tidal flats located at the mouth of the Elbe River along the German coast. The region features a heavily used maritime shipping lane where the ocean ebbs and flows, creating complex coastal patterns captured through satellite observation.
