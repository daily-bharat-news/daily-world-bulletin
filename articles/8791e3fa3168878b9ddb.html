Youth protests weakened PM Modi, says activist

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 12:08 AM · Source checked

An Indian activist who undertook a hunger strike stated that youth-led demonstrations have diminished the political strength of Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The protests, known as the cockroach movement, represented one of the most significant public expressions of opposition against the Indian leader in recent years. Details regarding the specific demands and outcomes of the hunger strike remain limited based on the available information.
