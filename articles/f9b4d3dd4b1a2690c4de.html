'Awarapan 2' opens strongly with Rs 27 crore on Day 1

Vinay · 15 Aug 2026, 05:44 AM · Source checked

Emraan Hashmi starrer 'Awarapan 2' has recorded an opening day box office collection of Rs 27 crore. The film has received mixed reviews from critics and audiences alike. While some fans on social media have praised the movie, calling it entertaining, several reviewers expressed varied opinions on its nostalgic appeal and narrative. Detailed box office trends and further commercial performance reports will emerge as the theatrical run continues over the upcoming days.
