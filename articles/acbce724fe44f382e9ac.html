Over 120 MPs urge chancellor to review student loan repayments

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 12:08 AM · Source checked

More than 120 members of parliament and peers have urged John Healey to conduct an urgent review into student loan repayments, describing the current rates as an unsustainable burden. The cross-party group has raised concerns regarding the financial pressure these repayments place on individuals. As discussions continue, further details on the proposed review and any potential changes to the system remain limited based on the current information provided.
