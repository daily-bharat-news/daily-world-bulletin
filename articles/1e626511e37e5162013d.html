Hamas agrees to disarm following Trump Board of Peace plan announcement

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 07:18 AM · Source checked

A senior official from Hamas has informed the BBC that the group has agreed to the Board of Peace plan, which involves disarming. Meanwhile, Israel has not yet issued any official comments regarding the development. Details are limited at this stage as further information regarding the implementation and official responses from other parties involved remains awaited.
