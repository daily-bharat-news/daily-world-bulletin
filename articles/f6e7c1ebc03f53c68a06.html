Delhi sees wettest August first week in 16 years, IMD issues yellow alert

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 07:12 AM · Source checked

Delhi has registered its highest rainfall for the first week of August in sixteen years, leading to significant weather changes across the region. In response to the continuous wet spell, the IMD has issued a yellow alert for today. Commuters and residents are advised to stay updated on local advisories as authorities monitor the situation. Further details regarding specific rainfall measurements and traffic disruptions are currently limited.
