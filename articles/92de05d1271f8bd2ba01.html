Why endometriosis remains frequently undiagnosed across India

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 12:06 PM · Source checked

Endometriosis continues to go largely undiagnosed in India, often being dismissed as mere period pain. Chronic pelvic pain associated with this condition carries significant physical and emotional costs for patients. Medical experts highlight that the true disease burden remains underrecognized within the healthcare system. Consequently, individuals suffering from this condition frequently face delays in receiving proper medical evaluation and patient care. Details regarding specific diagnostic challenges and prevalence rates are currently limited.
