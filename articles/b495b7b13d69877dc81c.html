Batwara 1947 Cast Salaries Revealed Ahead of Release

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 01:16 PM · Source checked

Cast remuneration details for the upcoming film 'Batwara 1947' have emerged, with reports indicating that lead actor Sunny Deol received sixty crore rupees, while Preity Zinta took home ten crore rupees. Ahead of the August fourteenth release, the cast has been actively promoting the movie. Sunny Deol urged youth to watch the film, emphasizing that nothing is greater than humanity. Additionally, Sunny Deol and Preity Zinta met Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath, and Deol also visited the Partition Museum in Amritsar along with Karan Deol.
