Supreme Court Seeks Centre's Response on Jantar Mantar Protests

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 12:26 PM · Source checked

The Supreme Court has asked the central government to examine an important public interest litigation arguing that Delhi's Jantar Mantar is no longer an appropriate location for demonstrations. Hearing petitions on the matter, the bench noted that the site faces significant access issues. Consequently, the court sought a response from the authorities regarding the suitability of the venue and the possibility of designating an alternative location for public protests in the national capital.
