Opposition protests in Parliament over Ram temple donation theft and Amit Shah

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 01:03 PM · Source checked

Opposition MPs held a protest inside Parliament grounds, raising slogans and displaying banners concerning the theft of Ram temple donations and Home Minister Amit Shah's absence. The demonstrators also voiced opposition to police action against student protests, demanding a statement from Amit Shah. Amid continuous uproar by the opposition members, the Lok Sabha was subsequently adjourned until 2 pm.
