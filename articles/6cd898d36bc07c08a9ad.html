What psychiatric genetics can and cannot tell an Indian family

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 09:00 AM · Source checked

Recent discussions highlight the growing role of psychiatric genetics in understanding mental health conditions within Indian households. Experts examine the extent to which genetic testing and research can accurately predict mental health risks for family members. While scientific advancements offer valuable insights into hereditary patterns, limitations remain regarding certainty and environmental triggers. Families are advised to approach genetic information with caution, understanding that it provides probabilities rather than definitive answers for complex psychiatric conditions.
