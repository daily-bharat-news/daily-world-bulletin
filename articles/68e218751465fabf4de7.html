Spain's sports minister claims country deserves 2030 World Cup final

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 02:24 PM · Source checked

Spain's sports minister has stated that the country deserves to host the final of the 2030 FIFA World Cup rather than Morocco. Meanwhile, recent media reports suggest that FIFA president Gianni Infantino allegedly offered the 2030 tournament final to Morocco in exchange for support during his election bid. The competing claims highlight discussions surrounding the venue selection for the major football event, with various reports detailing the political and administrative dynamics involved in deciding the final match location.
