FIFA opens disciplinary proceedings against Argentina trio

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 10:39 PM · Source checked

Football's governing body FIFA has initiated disciplinary proceedings and an assault case against three members of the Argentina national team following the World Cup final. The action comes in the wake of chaos and a brawl during the tournament's climax, alongside related incidents. Further details regarding the specific individuals involved and potential sanctions remain limited as the investigation unfolds.
