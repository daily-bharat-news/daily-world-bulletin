Bird flu spreads among wildlife in Australia

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 09:05 AM · Source checked

Recent reports indicate that Australia is facing a rise in avian flu cases among wild birds, with detections doubling in under four days and total cases surpassing two hundred. This spike has led to safety concerns and the cancellation of poultry events at an agricultural show. Experts and observers note the severe environmental impact, drawing comparisons to the devastating seabird mortality previously witnessed in the United Kingdom when the virus struck there. Authorities continue to monitor the situation closely as the disease affects local flocks.
