Auto Retail Sales Jump 26% in July

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 05:43 PM · Source checked

Vehicle retail sales across the country registered a notable twenty-six percent increase in July. Reports indicate that alternative fuel vehicles are steadily catching up to the market share traditionally held by petrol vehicles. Industry data highlights shifting consumer preferences towards greener mobility options. Further details regarding specific segment performances and regional breakdowns are currently limited.
