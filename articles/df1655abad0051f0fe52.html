Noida girl apologises after case over remarks against PM Modi

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 08:39 AM · Source checked

A fifteen-year-old girl from Noida, who was booked following a video showing her using abusive language against Prime Minister Narendra Modi during a protest, has released an apology video. In her statement, she expressed deep regret, stating she is very young and that it was her first and last mistake, while seeking forgiveness from the entire nation. Meanwhile, the legal case has been transferred to Delhi, and a salon owner has alleged that she concealed her identity during an interaction.
