FIFA chief Infantino drops World Cup sell-off plan

Vinay · 01 Aug 2026, 05:07 AM · Source checked

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has dropped a controversial proposal for private investment in the World Cup following widespread opposition and backlash. Football Australia and the Asian Football Confederation welcomed the climbdown. Meanwhile, UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham became the first leader of a major country to call for the FIFA president's resignation over the issue. UEFA and its fifty-five national associations also issued a statement regarding the developments.
