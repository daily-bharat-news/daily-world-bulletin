Two Dead After Firefighting Helicopters Collide in Greece

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 06:33 AM · Source checked

Two crew members lost their lives when two firefighting helicopters collided during an operation in Greece. The fatal incident resulted in the deaths of one Danish national and one Greek national. Meanwhile, a British pilot and another Greek crew member managed to survive the crash. Authorities are examining the circumstances surrounding the collision as firefighting efforts continue in the region. Further details regarding the incident remain limited at this time.
