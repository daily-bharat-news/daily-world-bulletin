Sonu Nigam sings during surgery in viral video

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 12:04 PM · Source checked

Popular playback singer Sonu Nigam recently turned a medical procedure into an impromptu musical session. While undergoing surgery, he performed the classic Mohammed Rafi track 'Suhani Raat Dhal Chuki' for his doctors. Nigam later shared a video of the incident on social media, accompanied by an emotional note celebrating the profound joy and healing power of music. Media reports highlighted how the artist sang through discomfort, delighting the medical team and fans alike with his spontaneous performance.
