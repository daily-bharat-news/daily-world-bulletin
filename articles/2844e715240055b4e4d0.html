SpaceX shares fall following earnings report and high AI spending plans

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 01:48 PM · Source checked

SpaceX shares dropped after the company released its inaugural earnings report, which highlighted significant investments in artificial intelligence. During a financial analysts call, Elon Musk stated that observers were underestimating his firm's capabilities and future potential. The announcements provided new financial insights into the aerospace manufacturer, leading to immediate market reactions regarding its heavy capital allocation toward artificial intelligence development.
