Luigi Mangione murder trial to feature anonymous jury due to media attention

Vinay · 12 Aug 2026, 12:42 AM · Source checked

The state murder trial of Luigi Mangione will use an anonymous jury comprising 12 members. The presiding judge made this decision to protect the jurors because of the intense media spotlight surrounding the legal proceedings. Further details about the schedule and specific trial arrangements remain limited based on the current reports from BBC World.
