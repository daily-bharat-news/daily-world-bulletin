Mountaineer Discovers Rare 'Sea Dragon' Fossil After Near-Death Experience

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 02:47 PM · Source checked

Following a perilous incident where he became trapped in snow on Yr Wyddfa, Matthew Myerscough shifted his focus from climbing mountains to searching for fossils on beaches. This career change led to the remarkable discovery of a rare 'sea dragon' fossil. Details regarding the exact species and location of the find are limited based on the current report, but the remarkable find highlights an unexpected pivot from mountaineering to paleontological discovery following his survival story.
