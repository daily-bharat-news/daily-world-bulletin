Over 20 Lakh Government Teachers Face Job Risks, Stage Protests

Vinay · 04 Apr 2026, 12:30 PM · Source checked

More than twenty lakh government teachers across the country are facing the risk of losing their employment, prompting widespread protests among educators. Details regarding the specific reasons and underlying policies causing this sudden threat to their job security are currently limited in the available reports. Educator groups and teaching associations have expressed deep concern over the situation, taking to the streets to demand immediate intervention and a clear resolution from the concerned authorities regarding their future service status.
