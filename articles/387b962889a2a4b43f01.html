Pranit More gets emotional during first live show after Rs 370 biryani row

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 09:46 AM · Source checked

Comedian Pranit More turned emotional and held back tears during his first live stand-up performance following the recent Rs 370 biryani controversy. Overwhelmed by the response, he expressed gratitude to the audience and received a standing ovation. Reflecting on the turbulent period, he questioned whether people would accept him back and thanked attendees for giving him another chance to perform on stage.
