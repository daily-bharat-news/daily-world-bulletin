NASA and SpaceX to Observe Rocket Stage Lunar Impact

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 01:30 AM · Source checked

NASA and SpaceX are actively tracking a used Falcon 9 upper stage from a commercial mission that is expected to impact the Moon near the Einstein and Bell craters. Researchers plan to use various ground-based telescopes and space-based assets to observe the event and collect valuable lunar data. Officials have confirmed that the upcoming impact poses no danger to Earth. Details regarding the exact scientific measurements and instruments being deployed for this observation campaign remain limited.
