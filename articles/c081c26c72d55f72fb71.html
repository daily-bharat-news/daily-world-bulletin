Karnataka Mandates HIV Camps in Colleges Following New Health Report

Vinay · 29 Jul 2026, 05:51 PM · Source checked

A recent health report from Karnataka reveals that two lakh people are currently receiving HIV treatment in the state. Following the findings, authorities have mandated HIV testing and counselling camps across all engineering and degree colleges. The Karnataka State AIDS Prevention Society has also launched awareness campaigns in various workplaces and educational institutions to promote testing. Media reports indicate that over seven thousand students have tested positive, prompting the government to take proactive steps to address the issue among the youth.
