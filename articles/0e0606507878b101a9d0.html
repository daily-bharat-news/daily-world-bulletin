Uefa maintains boycott threat after FA withdraws support for Infantino

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 09:41 PM · Source checked

European football governing body Uefa has stated that FIFA's decision to support president Gianni Infantino during a meeting changes nothing regarding their stance. Meanwhile, the Football Association has withdrawn its support for Infantino. Uefa indicated that a potential boycott by European associations may still proceed despite the recent developments at FIFA. Further updates on the situation are awaited as discussions continue among the concerned sports authorities.
