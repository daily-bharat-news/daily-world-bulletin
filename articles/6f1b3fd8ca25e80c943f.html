England to withdraw support for FIFA president Gianni Infantino

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 04:25 PM · Source checked

Reports indicate that the English Football Association is prepared to follow Wales in withdrawing backing for FIFA president Gianni Infantino. This development aligns with related news regarding European football governance, as UEFA welcomed FIFA's decision to abandon plans concerning a private equity proposal and the sale of a stake in the World Cup. Real Madrid also expressed gratitude to UEFA for protecting the sport following these changes. Comprehensive details regarding the official declarations and subsequent actions by the associations remain limited based on the current updates.
