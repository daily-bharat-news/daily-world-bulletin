PETA India Launches Dairy-Free Campaign for World Breastfeeding Week

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 04:02 PM · Source checked

During World Breastfeeding Week, PETA India has organised a unique campaign featuring giant 'babies' in public spaces. The initiative aims to encourage passers-by to stop consuming dairy products and transition to a dairy-free lifestyle. By using striking visuals, the campaign highlights its message about animal milk consumption. Further details regarding the exact locations and public response to this awareness drive remain limited at this time.
