Rare turtle flown home to Caribbean after washing up in Wales

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 12:00 PM · Source checked

A rare turtle discovered stranded on a Welsh beach is embarking on a remarkable five-thousand-mile journey to return to its natural habitat in the Caribbean Sea. Conservationists have described the upcoming relocation as an extremely emotional undertaking for everyone involved in the rescue and rehabilitation process. Further details regarding the specific species of the reptile and the exact logistics of the long-distance flight remain limited at this time.
