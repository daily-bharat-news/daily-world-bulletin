IMD warns of very heavy rain in central and western India

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 04:56 PM · Source checked

According to the India Meteorological Department, a deep depression is likely to cause extremely heavy rainfall across central and western regions of the country. Officials have issued warnings as the weather system approaches, urging caution in the affected areas. At this stage, further updates on the exact path and intensity of the weather event remain limited, but authorities are closely monitoring the situation to provide timely advisories to the public.
