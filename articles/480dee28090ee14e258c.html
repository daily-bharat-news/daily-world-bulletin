Centre Tightens Surveillance on GLP-1 Weight-Loss Drugs and Bans Surrogate Promotion

Vinay · 05 Aug 2026, 04:34 AM · Source checked

The central government has increased monitoring of GLP-1 weight-loss medications and prohibited surrogate advertising following concerns raised by officials. This regulatory action addresses the rising promotion of such treatments. Meanwhile, global health authorities have emphasized the necessity for safe usage, and experts have warned against the risks associated with self-administering unverified weight-loss products obtained from unauthorized markets.
