IMD Issues Rain Alert For Delhi, Assam, Punjab And Other States

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 01:11 PM · Source checked

The India Meteorological Department has issued weather warnings for rainfall across several regions in the country on July 30. States including Assam, Delhi, Odisha, Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, and Uttarakhand are among those placed under the rain alert. Residents in the affected areas are advised to stay updated on the latest meteorological forecasts and take necessary precautions as heavy showers are expected to impact normal routines. Specific regional intensity and localized warnings have been highlighted by the weather office.
