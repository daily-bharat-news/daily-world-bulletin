BYD India Announces Festive Offers On Electric Cars

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 12:17 PM · Source checked

BYD India has announced special festive season offers for its electric vehicle lineup in the country. The company aims to attract more customers during the festive period by introducing these limited-period benefits on its eco-friendly cars. Prospective buyers can check with authorized dealerships to get detailed information regarding the specific discounts, schemes, and benefits applicable to various electric models available in the Indian market.
