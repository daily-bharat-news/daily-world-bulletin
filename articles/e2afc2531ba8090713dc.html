UEFA Vows World Cup Boycott As Concacaf Rejects FIFA Plan

Vinay · 31 Jul 2026, 01:23 AM · Source checked

UEFA and its fifty-five national associations have stated they will boycott the World Cup if FIFA president Gianni Infantino moves forward with controversial private equity and investment plans. Meanwhile, Concacaf and its forty-one member associations have also rejected the proposal. Reports indicate that FIFA's sales pitch involves introducing more tournaments and increasing debt, while allegedly omitting the women's game entirely. Further details regarding the unfolding situation remain limited at this stage.
