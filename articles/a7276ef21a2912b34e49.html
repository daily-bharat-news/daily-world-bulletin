IMD predicts heavy rain across states with yellow alert in Kerala and Rajasthan

Vinay · 13 Aug 2026, 09:28 AM · Source checked

The India Meteorological Department has issued weather forecasts indicating very heavy rainfall across multiple states in the country. Authorities have put yellow alerts in place for Kerala and Rajasthan as precautionary measures. Residents in the affected regions are advised to stay updated on local weather bulletins and take necessary safety precautions due to the expected downpours. Official sources have not provided further details at this time, and information remains limited.
