Civil Aviation Minister Naidu denies rumours of ethanol blend with ATF

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 04:41 PM · Source checked

Civil Aviation Minister Naidu has dismissed reports regarding any proposal to blend ethanol into Aviation Turbine Fuel. The clarification comes amid claims raised by political figures concerning government plans for ethanol blending in vehicle fuels and aviation turbine fuel. Officials have firmly denied these rumours, assuring that no such mixture is currently under consideration for aircraft. Citizens are advised to rely on official statements regarding fuel policies.
