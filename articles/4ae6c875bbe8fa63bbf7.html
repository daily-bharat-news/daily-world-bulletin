Israeli Scientists Find Viagra May Help Stop Cancer Spread

Vinay · 04 Aug 2026, 01:00 AM · Source checked

Researchers in Israel have discovered a potential new benefit involving the active ingredient in Viagra, suggesting it may help prevent the spread of cancer. According to early studies, the medication could block a specific pathway that cancer cells rely on to spread throughout the body. While these findings offer fresh hope for future treatments, further research is necessary to fully understand the effects. Details are limited at this stage as scientists continue to evaluate the potential of the medication against cancer.
