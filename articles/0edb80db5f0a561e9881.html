Ariana Grande plans break from public life after tour

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 04:29 PM · Source checked

US singer Ariana Grande will step back from public visibility once her world tour concludes next month, according to her representative. The decision comes following continuous scrutiny and relentless attention from the media and public. The 33-year-old artist intends to take a well-deserved break to distance herself from the constant spotlight after wrapping up her upcoming concert commitments. Further specifics regarding the duration of her hiatus were not immediately disclosed by her team.
