Dilip Gavit Wins Gold And Mohammed Basil Secures Silver At CWG

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 10:10 AM · Source checked

Para-athlete Dilip Gavit secured a gold medal, while Mohammed Basil Morssinganakath claimed a silver, achieving a historic one-two finish for India in the men's 100m T47 final at the Commonwealth Games. Initially known as a 400-meter specialist, Gavit transitioned to secure the record-breaking victory. Following his triumph on Guru Purnima, Gavit dedicated his gold medal to his coach, Vaijanath Kale, highlighting the inspiring journey from humble beginnings to international glory under his mentor's guidance.
