Ex-leader Hasina plans Bangladesh return amid death penalty conviction

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 12:32 AM · Source checked

Former Bangladesh leader Sheikh Hasina is reportedly planning a return to the country despite facing a death penalty sentence. The seventy-eight-year-old politician was previously convicted of crimes against humanity following a deadly crackdown during a political uprising in 2024. Further details regarding her travel plans and security arrangements remain limited based on current reports.
