Nicaraguan Leader Proposes Extending Presidential Term

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 04:04 PM · Source checked

Nicaraguan leader Daniel Ortega has proposed extending the presidential term by another year. This development marks another instance where the constitution has been altered to prolong his time in office. President Ortega is currently eighty years old. Further details regarding this proposed constitutional amendment and its potential implications remain limited based on the current available information from the source.
