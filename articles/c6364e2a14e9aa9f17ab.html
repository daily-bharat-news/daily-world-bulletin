Indian Boxers Shatter Birmingham Record with 10 Medals at CWG 2026

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 07:20 AM · Source checked

Indian pugilists have surpassed their previous Birmingham performance by assuring ten medals for the country at the upcoming Glasgow Commonwealth Games. During a stellar outing where the contingent achieved a flawless six out of six wins in a single day, athletes displayed remarkable determination. Notably, Arundhati Choudhary managed to shed two kilograms in a single day prior to securing her medal berth. This achievement highlights the resilience and dominance of Indian boxing on the international stage, with multiple competitors advancing further in their respective categories.
