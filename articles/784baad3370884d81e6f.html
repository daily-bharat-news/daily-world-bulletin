India formally names 27 places in Arunachal Pradesh

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 12:45 AM · Source checked

In a strategic move to counter China, the Indian government has officially mapped and assigned standard names to 27 locations in Arunachal Pradesh. This action comes as a direct response to repeated attempts by Beijing to rename various sites within the Indian territory using Mandarin characters. Major national publications reported that the mapping initiative establishes official nomenclature for these border areas, reinforcing India's administrative authority and sovereignty over the region following earlier controversial renaming efforts by the neighbouring country.
