Lashkar Commander Associated With Hafiz Dies Mysteriously in Pakistan

Vinay · 09 Aug 2026, 10:53 AM · Source checked

A commander of the Lashkar-e-Taiba terror group, Qari Saeed, who was responsible for paying pensions to families of terrorists killed in India, has died mysteriously in Pakistan. Reports indicate he passed away hours after consuming food at an unfamiliar eatery. Saeed had been associated with Hafiz Saeed since 1990. Meanwhile, claims have emerged from a Lashkar leader that Indian agents have killed over thirty LeT operatives in Pakistan since 2020. Official details regarding the exact circumstances of his sudden death remain limited at this stage.
