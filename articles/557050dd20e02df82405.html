Muslim leaders claim Bengal police forcing mosques to remove loudspeakers

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 06:14 PM · Source checked

Muslim leaders in West Bengal have alleged that police are pressuring mosques to remove loudspeakers. Reports indicate that authorities have removed numerous loudspeakers from religious sites, citing noise regulation rules. The action has drawn political reactions, with leaders urging intervention to halt the removal process. Further details regarding the statewide implementation of these directives remain limited as local authorities continue enforcing compliance with sound standards.
