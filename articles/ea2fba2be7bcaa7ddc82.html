Raj Thackeray's Party Sends Toy Cars To Mumbai Civic Body

Vinay · 03 Aug 2026, 04:34 PM · Source checked

Raj Thackeray-led MNS has sent toy cars to the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation following a controversy over vehicle upgrades. Municipal leaders requested new SUVs worth Rs 1.5 crore, citing back pain caused by the existing Scorpio vehicles. This demand sparked a political debate, with opposition parties questioning the necessity of the expenditure. Meanwhile, a BJP leader in the civic body has advocated for uniformity in vehicle allocation, while others have denied the claims regarding health issues.
