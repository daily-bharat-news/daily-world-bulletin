Anthropic Claude AI chat logs found publicly accessible online

Vinay · 28 Jul 2026, 12:40 PM · Source checked

Hundreds of private conversations involving Anthropic's Claude artificial intelligence chatbot were recently discovered to be publicly available on the internet. This security issue has raised concerns regarding the privacy and data protection of user interactions with the AI platform. At this stage, further details concerning the exact cause of the exposure and the complete scale of the affected chats are currently limited. Users are advised to exercise caution when sharing sensitive information with automated chatbot services while investigations continue.
