Shell Profits Double as Oil Prices Rise Amid Strait of Hormuz Disruptions

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 02:46 PM · Source checked

Shell has recorded a doubling of its profits following a notable increase in international energy prices. This rise in market rates is linked to conflict involving Iran, which has caused significant disruptions to global supply chains. In particular, shipments of crude oil and liquid natural gas passing through the vital Strait of Hormuz have been severely impacted. Consequently, restricted global supply has led to higher energy costs worldwide.
