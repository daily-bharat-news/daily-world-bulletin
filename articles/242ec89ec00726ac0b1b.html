Bhopal's 'Lady Singham' Stops Moving Bus in Viral Incident

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026, 03:34 PM · Source checked

A female police officer in Bhopal, widely referred to as 'Lady Singham', has gained attention after standing directly in front of a moving bus to perform her duty. Speaking about the incident, she stated that she simply carried out her responsibility and was not afraid. Details regarding the exact date, location, and circumstances leading up to the confrontation remain limited based on the available reports. Authorities have not yet released further official statements concerning the driver or any subsequent actions taken following the event.
