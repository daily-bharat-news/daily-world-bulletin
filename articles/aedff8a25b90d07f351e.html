Sophia Laforteza leaves global pop group Katseye

Vinay · 08 Aug 2026, 02:35 AM · Source checked

Sophia Laforteza has announced her departure from the international music group Katseye, becoming the second member to leave the ensemble this year. This announcement follows the recent news that fellow bandmate Manon Bannerman has taken a temporary hiatus from the group. Further details regarding the reasons behind these lineup changes and the future plans for the remaining members are currently limited.
