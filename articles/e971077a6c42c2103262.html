Supreme Court Directs Medical Care for Injured Jantar Mantar Protestors

Vinay · 30 Jul 2026 · Source checked

The Supreme Court of India has directed that proper medical treatment be provided to protestors who sustained pellet gun injuries during a demonstration at Jantar Mantar. The apex court also asked the Central Government to preserve the ammunition log of the Rapid Action Force used during the protest led by CJP. While hearing a plea seeking a total ban on pellet guns, the court questioned how pellets could be prohibited when bullets remain permissible in extreme circumstances. Meanwhile, amid the ongoing row, the CRPF chief urged personnel to execute their duties fearlessly.
