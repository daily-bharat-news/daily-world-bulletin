'Ramayana' English trailer impresses fans with AI lip-sync technology

Vinay · 06 Aug 2026, 11:50 AM · Source checked

An English trailer for the upcoming fantasy action epic 'Ramayana', starring Ranbir Kapoor and Yash, has garnered positive reactions from internet users. Fans praised the use of artificial intelligence lip-sync technology, describing the voice work for Ranbir Kapoor as highly accurate. Meanwhile, the movie is reportedly set for a box office clash with 'Godzilla Minus Zero' on November 6, as both anticipated titles compete for IMAX screens. Additional details regarding the full cast and overall project remain limited based on current reports.
